## Meaning

22 is the second of the [[master-numbers|master numbers]], usually called the master builder. Where the rule says so, numerology keeps it whole instead of reducing it to 4 (2 + 2). It is written 22/4.

It keeps the method and discipline of [[number-4|4]] and adds the vision associated with [[number-11|11]]. The result is the capacity to turn a large idea into something real and lasting: an organisation, a building, a system that serves many people. Ulune’s keywords for it are master builder, vision, pragmatism and large-scale work.

On its light side, a 22 combines big-picture thinking with patience for detail. It can plan over years, coordinate many people, and carry ambitious projects through where others stop at the idea.

The shadow is a theme to grow with. A 22 tends to feel both the scale of what is possible and its pressure. The size of its own expectations can freeze it, or push it to control everything. Breaking a big aim into small, finished stages helps.

The tradition says many 22s live as a 4 for years first, and grow into the larger number later.

## In practice

- **[[life-path|Life Path]] 22**: the master form of the 4, a road of building on a large scale. The growing edge is to aim high without being crushed by the size of the aim.
- **[[expression|Expression]] 22**: the abilities of the 4 on a larger scale, the vision to see a big project whole and the sense to build it.
- **[[soul-urge|Soul Urge]] 22**: a wish to build something that outlasts oneself. Celebrating each step helps.
- **[[personality-number|Personality]] 22**: comes across as practical, steady and ambitious, someone who could carry a large project.
- **[[birthday|Birthday]] 22**, for the 22nd only: a talent for practical vision.
- **[[maturity|Maturity]] 22**: a second half of life able to build what outlasts it.
- **[[pinnacle|Pinnacle]] 22**: a stage of building that serves many, where method matters as much as ambition.
- **[[period-cycle|Period cycle]] 22**: room to build on a large scale, with work that can outlast its maker.
- **[[essence|Essence]] 22**: a year for large, practical plans.

## Across traditions

- **Alphabets and cards.** The Hebrew alphabet has 22 letters, and the tarot’s trumps, the Fool included, number 22. These are honest parallels of count, not sources of the master number.
- **The alphabet we use.** V is the 22nd letter. Ulune counts it as 4, yet its reading of V speaks of vision and building, the tone of 22.
- **The [[chaldean|Chaldean]] number.** A name total of 22 is one of Cheiro’s compound numbers. Ulune shows it beside the name and reads the single number, 4.

## Origin and history

The master numbers are modern. Mrs L. Dow Balliett, writing in the early 1900s, is usually credited with first leaving 11 and 22 unreduced. The name master builder is the one most books use today. Ulune follows Hans Decoz, who keeps 22 in the core numbers and the long cycles.

## How Ulune calculates it

A total of 22, or one whose digits add up to 22, such as 499 or 1984, stops at 22 where the rule keeps masters. Ulune keeps it in the core numbers: the Life Path, the three name numbers, the Birthday and the Maturity. It keeps it in the [[attitude-number|Attitude]], the pinnacles, the period cycles, the [[planes|planes]], the essence and the first name.

For a birth on 9 April 1980, the parts give 4 + 9 + 9 = 22: a Life Path 22. Its first pinnacle ends at 36 − 4 = 32, because a master counts as its root there.

A 22 inside a date stays whole and is added as 22. Yolanda Mary Kyle, born 29 November 1984, has a year of 1984 → 22, so her third period cycle, from 56, is a 22. Her month, 11, and her day, 29 → 11, give an Attitude and a first pinnacle of 22. For a birth on 4 April 1975, the year’s 22 joins the sum: 4 + 4 + 22 = 30 → 3.

Ulune reduces 22 to 4 in the [[personal-year|personal year]], month and day, the [[universal-year|universal year]], the [[balance-number|balance]] and the [[rational-thought|rational thought]]. The year 1993 adds up to 22, yet its universal year is a 4. The [[challenge|challenges]] and the [[bridge|bridges]] count 22 as 4.
