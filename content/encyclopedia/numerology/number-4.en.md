## Meaning

4 is the number of the square: four sides, four seasons, four directions. Numerology links it to foundations, order and effort. Its keywords in Ulune are structure, work, stability, method and reliability.

The tradition reads 4 as building things that last, through method and patience: budgets, routines, systems, craft. A 4 tends to trust what can be checked and measured. It prefers a slow, sure result to a fast, risky one. It is dependable and finishes what it starts.

Its growth theme is flexibility. The same steadiness can turn rigid and resist changes that would genuinely help. Worth can come to be measured only by hours worked. Leaving room in the plan for rest and surprises makes a 4 stronger, not weaker.

## In practice

- **[[life-path|Life Path]] 4**: a road of building, where patience and practical effort count for more than shortcuts. The growing edge is to keep room for play inside the structure.
- **[[expression|Expression]] 4**: the abilities of an organiser and a builder, with a feel for systems and detail. Leaving some slack for the unexpected helps.
- **[[soul-urge|Soul Urge]] 4**: a wish for security and order, for things done properly. **[[personality-number|Personality]] 4**: comes across as solid, serious and reliable.
- **[[birthday|Birthday]] 4**: a talent for organisation, for those born on the 4th, 13th or 31st.
- **[[personal-year|Personal year]] 4**: a year of work and foundations, often heavier, whose effort tends to pay off later.
- **[[challenge|Challenge]] 4**: accepting routine and effort without going rigid. **[[bridge|Bridge]] 4**: method and steady effort join the two numbers.
- **[[karmic-lesson|Karmic lesson]] 4**: no letter falls on 4, and a simple routine helps more than good intentions.

Camille Marie Laurent, born 15 June 1990, has a Life Path of 6 + 6 + 1 = 13 → 4. The 13 is kept, and her Life Path is written 13/4. In 2026 she is also in a personal year 4, from the same sum.

## Across traditions

- **Greek number lore.** The four numbers 1, 2, 3 and 4 add up to 10, a figure the Pythagoreans called the tetractys. Agrippa writes that “the Pythagorians call the Number of four Tetractis, and prefer it before all the vertues of Numbers”. He also recalls their oath sworn by it. The *Theology of Arithmetic* calls four “the first number which is even-times-even”.
- **Renaissance magic.** Agrippa ties four to the four elements, the four seasons, the four humours and the four cardinal virtues.
- **Chaldean numerology.** In Cheiro’s table D, M and T are worth 4, and he gives 4 to Uranus.
- **The birth grid.** In [[birth-grid|Phillips’s grid]], 4 sits at the bottom centre. In the Lo Shu square it takes the top left corner.

## Origin and history

Greek arithmetic studied 4 as the first square, 2 × 2 and 2 + 2 at once. Modern numerology kept that solidity and read it as the work of building. The [[karmic-debt|karmic debt]] 13 and the master number 22, both read through 4, belong to 20th-century practice.

## How Ulune calculates it

A total whose digits come down to 4 gives a 4: 13, 31 and 40, or 49 → 13 → 4. A total of 22 is kept whole wherever Ulune keeps the [[master-numbers|master numbers]]. Elsewhere it becomes 4. A Life Path of 4 comes from a total of 13, 31, 40 or 49. Born 22 May 1993, for instance: 5 + 22 + 22 = 49 → 13 → 4.

When 13 appears on the way to a Life Path, a [[name-numbers|name number]] or a Birthday, Ulune keeps it as the [[karmic-debt-13|karmic debt 13]]. Following Hans Decoz, Ulune reads it as a lesson about effort: shortcuts tend to cost twice, and steady work turns it into real strength. Lucie Anne Moreau’s Expression is 5 + 7 + 1 = 13 → 4, another 13/4.

[[number-22|22]] is the master form of 4, written 22/4. Many people with a 22 are said to live it as a 4 first. In a challenge or a bridge, a 22 counts as 4. In the yearly cycles, which keep no master, it becomes 4.

The Birthday is 4 on the 4th, the 13th (13/4) and the 31st; the 22nd stays 22. For a Life Path 4 or 22, the first [[pinnacle|pinnacle]] ends at 32. In the [[letter-cycle|letter cycles]], D, M and V each last four years.
