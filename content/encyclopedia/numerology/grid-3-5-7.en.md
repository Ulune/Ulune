## Meaning

The line 3-5-7 is the falling diagonal of the [[birth-grid|birth grid]] in David A. Phillips’s layout, from the top left to the bottom right. It crosses all three planes: [[number-3|3]] for the mind, [[number-5|5]] at the centre for the soul, [[number-7|7]] for the body. Phillips sees in these three the most symbolic numbers of the grid. They join expression, freedom and the search for knowledge and faith.

When all three cells hold a digit, the line is full. Phillips names it the arrow of spirituality. The tradition reads a quiet inner faith and a calm that others can feel. Such a person tends to learn from their own experience rather than from advice, and to reach their own conclusions. The gift is at its best when this independence stays open to other views.

When all three cells are empty, Phillips speaks of the arrow of the enquirer. He notes that it was first taught to him as the arrow of scepticism. The tradition reads a questioning mind that wants proof before it believes. Once convinced, it can hold to a view with great warmth. Read kindly, it is a gift for honest doubt, not a lack of faith.

A partial line, with one or two of its digits, says little alone.

For a birth from 1900 to 1999, the year already holds a 1 and a 9. So every full line 3-5-7 of that century comes with a full [[grid-1-5-9|1-5-9]], as Phillips observes. The year’s first two digits never supply a 3, a 5 or a 7, so the odds are the same in both centuries. Ulune’s code finds the line full on about 2% of dates from 1900 to 2099, and empty on about 24%.

## In practice

Take a made-up birth date, 7 May 1983. Its digits are 1 3 5 7 8 9. The diagonal holds a 3, a 5 and a 7: the line 3-5-7 is full, and Ulune lists it as “full: an arrow”. The same date fills two more lines: 1-5-9 and the right column, [[grid-7-8-9|7-8-9]].

Now take 29 November 1984. Its digits are 1 1 1 2 4 8 9 9. No 3, no 5, no 7: the diagonal is empty, and Ulune lists the line as “empty: what is missing”.

## Across traditions

The Lo Shu square is laid out 4 9 2 / 3 5 7 / 8 1 6. There, the digits 3, 5 and 7 form the centre row. The line is the same in both layouts, only turned, and it is full or empty on the same dates. Its digits add up to 15, like every line of the Lo Shu. For 29 November 1984, the Lo Shu shows the centre row empty and the top row, 4-9-2, full.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune counts the digits of the local date of birth, day, month and year, with the zeros set aside. The line 3-5-7 is full when the date holds at least one 3, one 5 and one 7. It is empty when the date holds none of them, and partial otherwise, shown with a dash. A repeated digit counts once. The line is a diagonal of Phillips’s layout and the centre row of the Lo Shu, and its state is the same in both.
