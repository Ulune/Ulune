## Meaning

The Life Path is the whole birth date turned into one number. Modern numerology treats it as the most important number of a reading. It describes the main direction of a life: the kind of work, challenges and lessons a person keeps meeting, whatever they decide to do.

The tradition reads it as a road rather than a portrait. The name numbers, such as the [[expression|Expression]], describe the talents a person brings. The Life Path describes the ground those talents walk on. A Life Path 4 is read as a road of building. A Life Path 5 is a road of change, a Life Path 7 a road of study.

Because it comes from the date alone, the Life Path never changes. A new name leaves it as it is. Numerology reads it as the steady theme the other numbers play around.

The Life Path can be any number from 1 to 9, or one of the [[master-numbers|master numbers]] 11, 22 and 33. A master number is read as a more intense form of its root: 11 of 2, 22 of 4, 33 of 6. The tradition says it is often lived as the root first, and grown into later.

## In practice

Take a made-up person, Camille Marie Laurent, born on 15 June 1990. Ulune reduces each part of the date on its own:

- the month, June, is 6;
- the day, 15, gives 1 + 5 = 6;
- the year, 1990, gives 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19, then 10, then 1.

The three results are added: 6 + 6 + 1 = 13, and 13 becomes 1 + 3 = 4. Camille has a Life Path 4. Ulune writes it 13/4, because a 13 reached on the way is a [[karmic-debt|karmic debt]]. The tradition reads it kindly, as a lesson about steady, patient work woven into the 4, never as a punishment.

A second made-up person, Lucie Anne Moreau, born on 4 July 1993, shows a master number. The month is 7 and the day 4. The year 1993 adds to 22, which is kept whole. Then 7 + 4 + 22 = 33, also kept: a Life Path 33.

In a reading, Ulune’s Life Path text speaks of the road and its growing edge. The [[pinnacle|pinnacles]] and the [[period-cycle|period cycles]] then divide the same road into stages.

## Across traditions

- **Indian numerology**, as practised today, also adds the whole birth date. Its *bhagyank*, often translated destiny number, is close to the Life Path. Indian guides often add all the digits at once and give each number a planet.
- **The [[birth-grid|birth grid]]** uses the same date in another way. It places the digits on a square of nine, in David A. Phillips’s layout or the Chinese Lo Shu square, and counts them instead of adding them.
- **Astrology** reads the birth moment through the sky, not through digits. A reader who knows both may set the Life Path beside the [[sun|Sun]], the centre of a chart. Ulune offers this as a comparison for learning, not a rule of either tradition.

## Origin and history

The idea that number lies under the order of things goes back to the Pythagoreans. Aristotle reports that they took the principles of mathematics to be the principles of all things. Modern numerology carries Pythagoras’s name, but the link is a later attribution. No ancient text describes a Life Path.

The method took shape in the early 20th century. L. Dow Balliett presented her system in 1905 as one “taught by Pythagoras”. In her 1908 book, she adds the month, the day and the year to find the “birth number”. She writes of a person’s “birth path”, and sets 11 and 22 apart as “free” numbers. Later teachers, among them Juno Jordan, built on this work. Books still use more than one name, Life Path and Birth Path among them.

Ulune follows Hans Decoz, a widely used modern school. His World Numerology site reduces each part of the date on its own and never reduces 11, 22 or 33 on the way. Adding all the digits at once, it warns, can hide a karmic debt.

## How Ulune calculates it

- Ulune reads the local date of birth, the one on the birth certificate, not the date in Universal Time.
- The month, the day and the year are each reduced by adding their digits. A part that reaches 11, 22 or 33 stops there. November stays 11, the 22nd stays 22, the 29th becomes 11, and 1984 becomes 22.
- The three parts are added, and the total is reduced the same way, keeping 11, 22 and 33.
- If the total passes through 13, 14, 16 or 19, Ulune marks a karmic debt (13/4, 14/5, 16/7, 19/1). Only the total’s steps count: the 19 inside 1990 is not one.
- No name is needed. With a birth date, the Life Path is always there.
