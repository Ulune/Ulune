## Meaning

The essence is the number of a year as the birth name sees it. The name gives three [[letter-cycle|letter cycles]], one for the first name, one for the middle names and one for the last name. At any age, each cycle stands on one letter. The essence adds the values of those three letters and reduces the total.

Numerology reads the essence as the underlying theme of the year, coming from the name. The [[personal-year|personal year]] gives a theme from the birth date. The two are read side by side: the personal year as the tone of the calendar year, the essence as what the person brings to it.

An essence can stay the same for several years, since the letters behind it last from one to nine years each. It changes when any of the three letters changes, on a birthday.

The numbers carry their usual themes, as Ulune’s readings give them:

- **1**: a year about oneself; independence and new starts.
- **2**: relationships, sensitivity, cooperation and patience.
- **3**: expression, friends, communication and lightness.
- **4**: work and order; effort, routine and practical matters.
- **5**: change; movement, travel, new people and the need to adapt.
- **6**: responsibility and care; home, family, love and commitments.
- **7**: reflection; study, solitude and inner questions.
- **8**: achievement; career, authority and results.
- **9**: completion; letting go, generosity and a wider view.
- **11**: sharpened intuition and ideals, with nervous energy to handle gently.
- **22**: large, practical plans; building something that can last.

The two master essences are read after [[number-11]] and [[number-22]].

## In practice

Camille Marie Laurent, a made-up person, was born on 15 June 1990. From her 35th birthday, on 15 June 2025, her letters are M (4) in Camille, R (9) in Marie and R (9) in Laurent. Their sum, 4 + 9 + 9 = 22, is kept whole as a [[master-numbers|master number]]: an essence 22, a year for building on a large scale.

From her 36th birthday, on 15 June 2026, the M gives way to an I (9). The sum becomes 9 + 9 + 9 = 27, then 9: an essence 9, a year of completion. In the same calendar year 2026, Camille has a personal year 4. Numerology reads the two together: a year of steady work on the outside, with a deeper current of letting go.

Elena Ruth Moreau, also made up, was born on 22 April 1992. From her 36th birthday her letters are N (5), H (8) and O (6). The sum, 19, reduces to 10, then to 1: an essence 1. The 19 on the way is not read as a [[karmic-debt|karmic debt]] in an essence.

## Origin and history

The essence comes from twentieth-century American numerology. Juno Jordan’s *Numerology: The Romance in Your Name* (1965) gives a chapter to the meaning of the essence, beside her table of events. Hans Decoz uses the same word for the total of the three letters, which he calls transits. Like the rest of modern numerology, it is often linked to Pythagoras, but that link is a later attribution.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune takes the letter on which each of the three cycles stands at an age, in completed years. Their Pythagorean values are added, and the total is reduced, keeping 11 and 22 whole. Three letters are worth at most 9 each, so the sum never reaches 33. No karmic debt is read in an essence.

The essence needs a birth name. Without one, Ulune shows no letter cycles and no essence. A calendar year’s essence, in Ulune’s tables, is the one that runs from that year’s birthday on.

The Cycles lens, at /numerology/cycles, shows the essence of the year chosen beside the personal year and the long cycles. It opens the year’s reading, with the three letters behind it. The table view gives the essence of every year, with its letters.
