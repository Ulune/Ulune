## Meaning

Numerology reduces a total by adding its digits until one is left: 1990 gives 19, then 10, then 1. A karmic debt is a 13, 14, 16 or 19 met on the way, before a core number reaches its last digit. The number is then written whole, with its total first: 13/4, 14/5, 16/7, 19/1.

Each debt sits behind one number and gives it a lesson:

- [[karmic-debt-13|13/4]]: a lesson about effort. Steady, focused work turns it into strength.
- [[karmic-debt-14|14/5]]: a lesson about freedom used well, with moderation and kept promises.
- [[karmic-debt-16|16/7]]: a lesson about pride and humility, and honesty with oneself.
- [[karmic-debt-19|19/1]]: a lesson about independence, and about accepting help.

The word debt can sound heavy. Numerology, in the form Ulune follows, reads it as a theme that comes back until it is learned, not as a punishment. The number keeps its root’s full meaning: a 13/4 is still a [[number-4|4]], with a lesson woven in. Ulune’s text for each debt ends with its answer, the way the lesson becomes a strength.

A debt is not a verdict on a person or a past. It names a theme that may need more patience, attention or honesty than others. It can be read as a direction for growth.

## In practice

Take a made-up person, Camille Marie Laurent, born 15 June 1990. Ulune works out her [[life-path|Life Path]] like this:

- June gives 6.
- The 15th gives 15 → 6.
- 1990 gives 19 → 10 → 1.
- Then 6 + 6 + 1 = 13 → 4.

Her Life Path is 13/4. The 13 is kept as a karmic debt, so her reading adds the lesson of effort to the meaning of 4.

Two other totals in her chart show where debts are not counted. Her year, 1990, passes through 19 on its own, but that step is inside one part, not in the Life Path’s sum. Her [[personality-number|Personality number]] comes from her consonants: Camille gives 13, Marie 13 and Laurent 19. Each name is reduced first, to 4, 4 and 1, and 4 + 4 + 1 = 9. Her Personality is a plain 9, with no debt.

## Across traditions

- **Cheiro’s compound numbers.** *Cheiro’s Book of Numbers* (1926) reads numbers from 10 to 52 as [[chaldean|Chaldean]] compounds, each with an image. 13 is a skeleton, or Death; 16 a tower struck by lightning, the shattered citadel; 19 the Sun, the prince of heaven.
- **The tarot.** Arthur Edward Waite’s *Pictorial Key to the Tarot* (1910) gives the same numbers to four trumps. They are XIII Death, XIV Temperance, XVI the Tower and XIX the Sun.
- **Karmic astrology.** Astrology has its own karmic reading, centred on the Moon’s nodes, as in Martin Schulman’s *Karmic Astrology* (1975). The [[south-node|South Node]] is read as the past, the [[north-node|North Node]] as the direction of growth.

## Origin and history

Karma is a Sanskrit word for action and its consequences. It entered the Western esoteric vocabulary largely through Theosophy; Helena Blavatsky’s *The Key to Theosophy* (1889) gives it a central place.

Karmic numbers belong to twentieth-century Western numerology. Florence Campbell’s *Your Days Are Numbered* (1931) already speaks of [[karmic-lesson|karmic lessons]]. Who first named 13, 14, 16 and 19 karmic debts is not clear. Ulune follows the form Hans Decoz gives. Authors differ on details, such as which numbers they check and how they add the birth date.

## How Ulune calculates it

- Ulune looks for debts in five core numbers: the Life Path, the [[expression|Expression]], the [[soul-urge|Soul Urge]], the Personality number and the [[birthday|Birthday]]. It does not look in the Maturity, the Attitude, the pinnacles, the planes, the cycles or the Chaldean number.
- Every total on the way counts, from the first sum to the step before the last. The final number is never a debt, since it is 1 to 9 or a [[master-numbers|master number]].
- Life Path: the month, the day and the year are each reduced first, with 11, 22 and 33 kept, then added. Ulune looks in that sum and its steps, never inside one part.
- Expression, Soul Urge and Personality: each name of the birth name is reduced on its own, then the names are added. Ulune looks in that sum and its steps. A name of one word keeps its own steps (49 → 13 → 4).
- Birthday: the day itself. Birth on the 13th, 14th, 16th or 19th is a debt.
- Switching a Y by hand can add or remove a debt in the Soul Urge or the Personality.
- The number is written with its debt (13/4), and the reading adds a section on that debt.
