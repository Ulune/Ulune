## Meaning

33 is the third of the [[master-numbers|master numbers]], often called the master teacher. Where the rule says so, numerology keeps it whole instead of reducing it to 6 (3 + 3). It is written 33/6.

It carries the care and responsibility of [[number-6|6]] on a larger scale: guidance, healing and service given out of love rather than duty. Ulune’s keywords for it are compassion, teaching, healing and devotion.

On its light side, a 33 is warm, patient and deeply supportive. It teaches by example, stays calm beside people in difficulty, and can turn its own experience into practical help for others.

The shadow is a theme to grow with. A 33 can take on the problems of everyone nearby until it is worn out, or become the helper who cannot receive help. Clear limits on what it gives are part of doing the work well.

Some numerologists do not use 33 at all. Those who do usually read it as a 6 until the person has grown into its demands.

## In practice

- **[[life-path|Life Path]] 33**: the master form of the 6, a road of care on a larger scale, teaching and easing others by example. It is rare, and usually lived first as a 6.
- **[[expression|Expression]] 33**: the abilities of the 6 on a larger scale, teaching, guiding and inspiring by example.
- **[[soul-urge|Soul Urge]] 33**: a wish to ease and to teach, whose care reaches beyond one’s own family. Receiving matters as much as giving.
- **[[personality-number|Personality]] 33**: comes across as deeply caring, with a quiet wisdom that invites trust.
- **[[maturity|Maturity]] 33**: a second half of life of care on a larger scale, kept possible by clear limits.
- **[[pinnacle|Pinnacle]] 33**: a stage of teaching or guiding others, where looking after oneself is part of the work.
- **[[attitude-number|Attitude]] 33**, from 22 November alone: meeting life with open-hearted care, warm and ready to teach or help.

## Across traditions

- **Dante.** The Divine Comedy gives 33 cantos each to Purgatory and Paradise, and 34 to Hell, a hundred in all. Number was part of the poem’s architecture.
- **The [[chaldean|Chaldean]] number.** A name total of 33 is one of Cheiro’s compound numbers. Ulune shows it and reads the single number, 6, which Cheiro gives to Venus.

## Origin and history

The master numbers are modern. Mrs L. Dow Balliett, early in the 20th century, is usually credited with leaving 11 and 22 unreduced. 33 came later: Ariel Yvon Taylor’s Numerology Made Plain, in its revised edition of 1930, lists it with the other two. It has never been used by every school. Ulune follows Hans Decoz, who keeps it.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune keeps 33 in the Life Path, the three name numbers and the Maturity. It also keeps it in the Attitude, the pinnacles and the [[planes|planes]]. In practice it appears where several numbers are added. No [[period-cycle|period cycle]] reaches it, since no part of a birth date reduces to 33.

For a birth on 22 September 1991, the parts give 9 + 22 + 2 = 33: a Life Path 33. The 22nd stays whole, and 1991 gives 20 → 2. The first pinnacle ends at 36 − 6 = 30. A birth on 11 November 2009 gives 11 + 11 + 11 = 33.

Masters are added whole. Yolanda Mary Kyle, born 29 November 1984, has 11 for her day and 22 for her year. Her second and fourth pinnacles are both 11 + 22 = 33. Her third adds the first two: 22 + 33 = 55 → 10 → 1.

The [[birthday|Birthday]] keeps only 11 and 22, and no day reduces to 33. The [[essence|essence]] cannot reach it, since three letters add up to 27 at most.

Ulune reduces 33 to 6 in the [[personal-year|personal year]], month and day, the [[universal-year|universal year]], the [[balance-number|balance]] and the [[rational-thought|rational thought]]. The [[challenge|challenges]] and the [[bridge|bridges]] count it as 6.
