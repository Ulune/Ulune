## Meaning

The attitude number is one of the [[finer-numbers|finer numbers]] of a numerology chart, and the only one drawn from the birth date alone. It adds the month and the day of birth. No name is needed, and the year plays no part.

Numerology reads it as a stance: the way a person tends to meet life day to day. It is often the first thing people pick up about someone, before they know them well. Ulune describes it as the stance a person tends to take towards life.

Each number has its own colour. A 1 meets life head-on, confident and ready to start; a word of warmth keeps the directness from sounding abrupt. A 2 meets it gently, looking for common ground. A 7 is thoughtful and observant; a friendly sign now and then keeps its reserve from reading as distance. None of these is a verdict, only a first impression that deeper numbers fill in.

The attitude number uses two of the three parts of the [[life-path|Life Path]], leaving out the year. It also shares its day with the [[birthday|Birthday number]]. So it often echoes them, yet it can differ: the year can pull the Life Path elsewhere.

The same sum, month plus day, is also the first [[pinnacle|pinnacle]]. The pinnacle reads it as the theme of the first long stage of life, until an age set by the Life Path. The attitude number reads it as a stance that lasts through every stage.

## In practice

Take a made-up person, Yolanda Mary Kyle, born 29 November 1984.

- The month, 11: kept as a [[master-numbers|master number]].
- The day, 29: 2 + 9 = 11, kept as well.
- 11 + 11 = 22, and 22 is kept too.

Her attitude number is 22. Ulune shows it as 22/4, a [[number-22|22]] over its root 4, and gives it the 4’s short reading. She tends to meet life seriously: practical, dependable, careful before committing. Some lightness keeps the seriousness from weighing on her.

Her first pinnacle is the same 22. Her Life Path is 8, so that pinnacle runs until her 28th birthday (36 minus 8).
## How Ulune calculates it

- Ulune reads the date of birth as it stands on the chart, the local date, not the date in universal time.
- The month is reduced to one digit, except 11, which is kept. October gives 1, December 3.
- The day is reduced the same way, keeping 11 and 22. The 11th and the 29th give 11, the 22nd gives 22.
- The two are added. The sum is reduced again, and 11, 22 and 33 are kept. A 33 comes from 22 November alone.
- No [[karmic-debt|karmic debt]] is read. Born 3 November, someone has 11 + 3 = 14, which simply becomes 5.
- A master number is shown with its root (22/4). The short text Ulune gives is that of the root.
