## Meaning

The personal month is the middle layer of the [[personal-cycles|personal cycles]]. It takes the [[personal-year|personal year]] and adds the month of the calendar. Numerology reads it as the year’s theme lived one month at a time: the same background, with a changing foreground.

A month is read inside its year, never on its own. A 5 month in a 7 year is a moment of movement in a reflective year. The same 5 month in a 5 year doubles the theme of change. The year sets the direction; the month shows which side of it comes forward for a few weeks.

Ulune’s short readings give each number a practical turn:

- **1**: a month to start something, to take the first real step on a plan.
- **2**: patience and cooperation; listening, handling details, letting things develop.
- **3**: company and self-expression; sharing an idea or a piece of work.
- **4**: steady work; catching up, repairing, keeping to a routine.
- **5**: movement and change; plans may shift, so some room for the unexpected helps.
- **6**: home, family and commitments; time for the people who rely on one.
- **7**: a quieter month for thinking, reading and rest, and stepping back before deciding.
- **8**: practical results; money matters, negotiation, a decision long put off.
- **9**: finishing and clearing out; tying up loose ends.

The months echo the numbers [[number-1|1]] to [[number-9|9]], read at the scale of a month.

## In practice

Camille Marie Laurent, a made-up person born on 15 June 1990, has a personal year 4 in 2026. Her months follow from it:

- **January**, month 1: 4 + 1 = 5.
- **June**, month 6: 4 + 6 = 10, then 1.
- **October**, month 10, which reduces to 1: 4 + 1 = 5.
- **December**, month 12, which reduces to 3: 4 + 3 = 7.

Her whole year runs 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 from January to December. Two patterns follow from the arithmetic. The months climb by one, then wrap from 9 back to 1. And October, November and December repeat the numbers of January, February and March, because 10, 11 and 12 reduce to 1, 2 and 3.

In any year, January’s number is the personal year plus one. A personal year 9 therefore begins with a personal month 1: the new round is already stirring while the old one closes.

## Origin and history

The personal month comes from the same twentieth-century American numerology as the personal year. Juno Jordan’s *Numerology: The Romance in Your Name* (1965) sets the personal months beside the personal year and the personal days. The method is now standard in modern books, Hans Decoz’s among them. No older tradition is known for it, and its link to Pythagoras is a later attribution, like that of numerology as a whole.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune adds the personal year, a number from 1 to 9, to the calendar month reduced to one digit. October, November and December count as 1, 2 and 3. The total is reduced to a number from 1 to 9. No master number is kept: a November in a personal year 9 gives 9 + 2 = 11, read as 2.

The personal month follows the calendar. It runs from the 1st to the last day of the month, inside a personal year that runs from 1 January.

The Cycles lens, at /numerology/cycles, shows the twelve personal months of the year chosen, each opening its reading. The Calendar gives the year view a band of personal months, and each month a reading with its personal year beneath it. Ulune lists there any long cycle that changes in that month, on a birthday.
