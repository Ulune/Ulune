## Meaning

The personal year is the number numerology gives to one calendar year of one person’s life. It joins the birth date to the year on the calendar, and it changes every year. It is the best known of the [[personal-cycles|personal cycles]], and the base of the [[personal-month|personal month]] and the [[personal-day|personal day]].

Personal years run in a round of nine. Each number is read as a step in that round, with its own theme:

- **Year 1**, after [[number-1|the number 1]]: a year for starting things; choices made now are said to set the tone for the round.
- **Year 2**, after [[number-2|the number 2]]: slower and more relational; patience and cooperation matter more than force.
- **Year 3**, after [[number-3|the number 3]]: more social contact, creativity and visibility, at the risk of scattering.
- **Year 4**, after [[number-4|the number 4]]: work and foundations; effort made now tends to pay off later.
- **Year 5**, after [[number-5|the number 5]]: movement and change; change that is chosen is easier than change that is forced.
- **Year 6**, after [[number-6|the number 6]]: home, family and responsibility; relationships may deepen.
- **Year 7**, after [[number-7|the number 7]]: quieter and more inward; study and reflection more than expansion.
- **Year 8**, after [[number-8|the number 8]]: work, authority and the use of resources; earlier effort may come to recognition.
- **Year 9**, after [[number-9|the number 9]]: completion; finishing and letting go, to make room for the next 1.

None of these years is good or bad: each is read as the right season for some things.

## In practice

Camille Marie Laurent, a made-up person, was born on 15 June 1990. For 2026, her birth month gives 6 and her birth day 15 gives 1 + 5 = 6. The year 2026 gives 2 + 0 + 2 + 6 = 10, then 1. Added, 6 + 6 + 1 = 13, then 1 + 3 = 4. Her 2026 is a personal year 4, a year read as one of work and foundations.

Her following years step on one at a time: 2027 is a 5, 2028 a 6, and so on to a 9 in 2031. Her round opened in 2023, a personal year 1, and the next opens in 2032.

A second made-up person, Bernard Olivier Martin, was born on 3 November 1978. November is the eleventh month, and here 11 is not kept as a [[master-numbers|master number]]: it becomes 2. With the day 3 and 2026 reduced to 1, he has 2 + 3 + 1 = 6, a personal year 6.

## Origin and history

The personal year belongs to the numerology that grew in the United States in the twentieth century. Florence Campbell’s *Your Days Are Numbered* (1931) already treats the number that rules a personal year. Juno Jordan’s *Numerology: The Romance in Your Name* (1965) gives it a chapter of its own, beside the personal months and days. No ancient text describes it, whatever the later link to Pythagoras suggests.

Numerologists differ on when the year begins. Hans Decoz counts it from 1 January to 31 December. Many add that it is often felt from the autumn before. Other writers start it on the birthday instead, rather like an astrologer’s yearly chart for the return of the Sun.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune follows Decoz. The birth month, the birth day and the calendar year are each reduced to one digit, then added, and the total is reduced to a number from 1 to 9. No master number is kept: a total of 11 or 22 becomes 2 or 4. A 13, 14, 16 or 19 on the way is not read as a [[karmic-debt|karmic debt]].

The personal year starts on 1 January, not on the birthday. Every year whose personal year is 1 opens a new round of nine. Ulune also uses these years to time the first [[period-cycle|period cycle]], which ends on the birthday in the first personal year 1 from age 27.
