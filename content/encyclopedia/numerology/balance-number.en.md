## Meaning

The balance number is one of the [[finer-numbers|finer numbers]] of a numerology chart, the smaller numbers read beside the main ones. It comes from the initials of the full birth name: the first letter of each name, turned into numbers, added and reduced to one digit.

Numerology reads it as a guide for difficult times. When a situation turns tense or confusing, people tend to fall back on a familiar way of coping. The balance number names that way, and what helps it work well.

It does not measure how balanced someone is. A 1 is no less steady than a 9. Each number describes a different strength under pressure, with its own small trap. A 4 copes through method and a plan; letting feelings in keeps the plan humane. A 7 steps back to think; what helps is coming back to people afterwards.

Resting on a few letters only, it is a light, practical reading: advice rather than a portrait. It is often read with the [[subconscious-self|subconscious self]], the reaction to the unexpected, and the [[rational-thought|rational thought number]], the way a decision is reached.

## In practice

Take a made-up person, Camille Marie Laurent, born 15 June 1990. Her birth name has three names, so three initials.

- C is worth 3, M is worth 4 and L is worth 3.
- 3 + 4 + 3 = 10, and 10 reduces to 1 + 0 = 1.

Her balance number is 1. Ulune’s reading: in a hard moment, the strength is to stand firm and act. What helps is talking it through with one trusted person before deciding alone.

A middle name changes the result. Typed as Camille Laurent alone, she would have 3 + 3 = 6, a balance of care and responsibility. This is one reason Ulune asks for the full name at birth.

## Origin and history

The balance number belongs to modern name numerology. Ulune follows Hans Decoz, who presents it among the numbers of a complete chart in his book with Tom Monte (1994). Its letter values are the [[pythagorean|Pythagorean]] table, whose link to Pythagoras himself is a modern attribution.

## How Ulune calculates it

- Ulune uses the full name at birth, as typed for numerology, or else the chart’s own typed name. A name used now does not change it.
- The name is cut into names at each space. A hyphenated name such as Jean-Pierre counts as one, with one initial.
- Accents are dropped, so É counts as E. Each initial takes its Pythagorean value, 1 to 9.
- The values are added, and the total is reduced by adding its digits until one digit is left.
- No [[master-numbers|master number]] is kept: 11 becomes 2, 22 becomes 4. No [[karmic-debt|karmic debt]] is read.
