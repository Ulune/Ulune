## Meaning

The pinnacles divide a life into four stages. Each stage has a number drawn from the birth date, and numerology reads it as the theme of those years: something to grow into, and a kind of success within reach. Where the [[life-path|Life Path]] describes the road as a whole, the pinnacles describe its stretches.

The first pinnacle runs from birth to an age between 27 and 35, the stage of growing up. The second and third last nine years each, through the middle of life. The fourth runs for the rest of life.

Each pinnacle travels with a [[challenge|challenge]] over the same years: the pinnacle is what the stage offers, the challenge what it asks one to work on. The [[period-cycle|period cycles]] run beside them on their own timing.

The numbers keep their usual meanings, at the scale of a decade:

- **1**: independence and initiative; leading and relying on oneself.
- **2**: cooperation, patience and diplomacy; success with others.
- **3**: self-expression, creativity and a lively social life.
- **4**: hard work, method and foundations built step by step.
- **5**: change, freedom, travel and new directions.
- **6**: responsibility for home, family and community.
- **7**: study, specialising and inner growth.
- **8**: achievement and authority through effort and judgement.
- **9**: completion, broad interests and service to others.
- **11**, **22** and **33**: inspiration, building on a large scale, and care on a larger scale.

## In practice

Camille Marie Laurent, a made-up person, was born on 15 June 1990. Her month is 6, her day 15 reduces to 6, and her year 1990 reduces to 1. Her Life Path is 4, so her first pinnacle ends at 36 − 4 = 32.

- **First pinnacle**, month + day: 6 + 6 = 12, then 3. From birth to her 32nd birthday, on 15 June 2022: a stage of [[number-3|expression]].
- **Second**, day + year: 6 + 1 = 7. From 32 to 41: a stage of [[number-7|study and inner growth]].
- **Third**, the first two added: 3 + 7 = 10, then 1. From 41 to 50: a stage of [[number-1|initiative]].
- **Fourth**, month + year: 6 + 1 = 7. From her 50th birthday on.

Yolanda Mary Kyle, also made up, was born on 29 November 1984. Her month 11 and her day 29, which reduces to 11, are kept whole, and 1984 reduces to 22. Her first pinnacle is 11 + 11 = 22, her second 11 + 22 = 33, both [[master-numbers|master numbers]]. Her third, 22 + 33 = 55, reduces to 1. Her Life Path is 8, so her first pinnacle ends at 28.

## Origin and history

The pinnacles are a twentieth-century American method. Clifford Cheasley’s *Numerology at a Glance* (1932) credits the pinnacles to Juno Kapp Walton. She is better known as Juno Jordan, and her *Numerology: The Romance in Your Name* (1965) gives them a chapter on timing the future. Hans Decoz’s modern rules, which Ulune follows, keep the same four stages. Like numerology as a whole, the method is often linked to Pythagoras, but that link is a later attribution.

## How Ulune calculates it

The month, the day and the year are each reduced first, keeping 11, 22 and 33 whole: the 29th counts as 11, and 1984 as 22. The first pinnacle adds the month and the day; the second, the day and the year; the third, the first two pinnacles; the fourth, the month and the year. Each total is reduced, again keeping 11, 22 and 33. No [[karmic-debt|karmic debt]] is read in a pinnacle.

The first pinnacle ends on the birthday at 36 minus the Life Path’s single digit. A master Life Path counts as its root: 11 as 2, 22 as 4, 33 as 6. The second and third last nine years each, and the fourth runs for the rest of life. Every change falls on a birthday.

The Cycles lens, at /numerology/cycles, shows the pinnacle running in the year chosen, and its life line draws all four to scale. The Calendar marks the birthday on which a new pinnacle begins.
