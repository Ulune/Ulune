## Meaning

11 is the first of the [[master-numbers|master numbers]]. Where the rule says so, numerology keeps it whole instead of reducing it to 2 (1 + 1). It is written 11/2: the master number and its root.

It carries the receptive, cooperative qualities of [[number-2|2]] at a higher intensity: intuition, inspiration and a sense of purpose. An 11 is often described as someone whose ideas or example reach and move other people. Ulune’s keywords for it are intuition, inspiration, sensitivity and vision.

On its light side, an 11 is intuitive and idealistic, able to inspire through ideas, teaching or art. It often senses what a group needs before anyone has put it into words.

The shadow is a theme to grow with. The same sensitivity can leave an 11 tense, self-critical and overloaded by other people’s moods. It can also wait for inspiration instead of acting. Steady routines help to keep it grounded: sleep, movement, a practical task.

The tradition says many people live the 2 first, through patience and cooperation, and grow into the 11 over time.

## In practice

- **[[life-path|Life Path]] 11**: the master form of the 2, a road of insight and inspiration. The growing edge is to trust it and keep one’s feet on the ground.
- **[[expression|Expression]] 11**: the abilities of the 2 raised to inspiration, the power to move people through what one says or makes.
- **[[soul-urge|Soul Urge]] 11**: a wish to live by an ideal and share a vision.
- **[[personality-number|Personality]] 11**: comes across as inspired and a little out of the ordinary, with a fine sensitivity.
- **[[birthday|Birthday]] 11**, for the 11th or the 29th: a talent for sensing what goes unsaid.
- **[[maturity|Maturity]] 11**: a second half of life led by intuition and ideals.
- **[[pinnacle|Pinnacle]] 11**: a stage of inspiration and visibility, perhaps a public role.
- **[[period-cycle|Period cycle]] 11**: a time when inspiration comes more easily, and so does tension.
- **[[essence|Essence]] 11**: a year of sharpened intuition and ideals, its energy handled gently.

## Across traditions

- **Renaissance magic.** Agrippa (1533) saw eleven as one step past ten, the number of the commandments, and one short of twelve, the number of grace. He called it a number of sin and repentance.
- **The alphabet.** K is the 11th letter. Ulune counts it as 2, like any letter in its column, yet its reading of K speaks of intuition and ideals, the tone of 11.
- **The [[chaldean|Chaldean]] number.** Cheiro read name totals from 10 to 52 as compound numbers, 11 among them. Ulune shows the compound beside the name but reads the single number, here 2.

## Origin and history

The master numbers are modern. Mrs L. Dow Balliett, writing in the early 1900s, is usually credited with first leaving 11 and 22 unreduced. Ariel Yvon Taylor’s Numerology Made Plain, in its revised edition of 1930, already lists 11, 22 and 33. Ulune follows Hans Decoz, who keeps all three.

## How Ulune calculates it

A total of 11, or a total that reduces to 11, such as 29 or 38, stops at 11 where the rule keeps masters. Ulune keeps it in the core numbers: the Life Path, the three name numbers, the Birthday and the Maturity. It keeps it in the [[attitude-number|Attitude]], the pinnacles, the period cycles, the [[planes|planes]], the essence, the rational thought and the universal year.

It keeps it inside the parts too. In the Life Path, a November month stays 11, and so do the 11th, the 29th, or a year like 2009. In a name, each name keeps its own 11 before the names are added. Bernard Olivier Martin, born 3 November 1978, has 29 → 11 in the vowels of Olivier.

For a birth on 1 January 1980, the parts give 1 + 1 + 9 = 11: a Life Path 11. The first pinnacle ends at 36 − 2 = 34, because a master counts as its root there.

Ulune reduces 11 to 2 in the [[personal-year|personal year]], month and day, and the [[balance-number|balance]]. The year 2027 adds up to 11: an 11/2 [[universal-year|universal year]], read with its own text. The [[challenge|challenges]] and the [[bridge|bridges]] count 11 as 2. Yolanda Mary Kyle, born 29 November 1984, has a Birthday 11, but her personal year uses the 29th as 2.
