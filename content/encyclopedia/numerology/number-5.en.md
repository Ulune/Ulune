## Meaning

5 sits in the middle of the digits 1 to 9 and is linked to the five senses. Numerology therefore associates it with experience, movement and change. Its keywords in Ulune are freedom, change, curiosity and adaptability.

The tradition reads 5 as freedom: travel, variety, new people, learning by trying. A 5 tends to adapt quickly and handles sudden change better than most. It is quick-witted, at ease with strangers, and brings back ideas and contacts from outside the usual circle. It grows restless when life becomes too predictable or too tightly controlled.

Its growth theme is freedom with a direction. A commitment can be dropped once it turns routine, and stimulation can be chased to escape boredom. Building variety into a commitment usually works better than leaving it.

## Across traditions

- **Greek number lore.** The *Theology of Arithmetic*, attributed to Iamblichus, calls the pentad “marriage”, since it joins the first even and the first odd. It also finds it “highly expressive of justice”.
- **Renaissance magic.** Agrippa writes that five “consists of the first even, and the first odd”, and links it to the five senses. His scale of five places in the heavens the “five wandring Stars”: Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Venus and Mercury.
- **Chaldean numerology.** In Cheiro’s table E, H, N and X are worth 5, and he gives 5 to Mercury.
- **The Lo Shu square.** In this Chinese square of nine, 5 holds the centre. Every row, column and diagonal adds up to 15, three times the centre. Legend tells of a turtle that rose from the Luo river bearing the pattern on its shell.
- **The birth grid.** In [[birth-grid|Phillips’s grid]], 5 also sits in the centre, where four of the eight lines cross.

## Origin and history

Chinese tradition set 5 at the heart of the Lo Shu, and clear references to the square of numbers appear by the first century CE. Schuyler Cammann traced how it shaped early Chinese philosophy and religion. Modern numerology, from the early 20th century, read the number of the senses as the number of experience and change.

## How Ulune calculates it

A total whose digits come down to 5 gives a 5: 14, 23, 32, 41 and 50. No master number lies on the way to 5. A Life Path of 5 comes from one of these totals.

A 14 on the way to a Life Path, a [[name-numbers|name number]] or a Birthday is kept. It is the [[karmic-debt-14|karmic debt 14]], written 14/5. Ulune reads it as a lesson about freedom used well: moderation, and keeping commitments. Hugo Paul Mercier, born 6 June 1995, has two. His Soul Urge is 9 + 4 + 1 = 14 → 5, and his Personality 6 + 1 + 7 = 14 → 5.

The Birthday is 5 on the 5th, the 14th (14/5) and the 23rd. For a Life Path 5, the first [[pinnacle|pinnacle]] ends at 31. In the [[letter-cycle|letter cycles]], E, N and W each last five years. In the birth grid, a 5 in the date fills the centre.
