## Meaning

Numerology reads time as well as character. The core numbers, such as the [[life-path|Life Path]], stay the same for a whole life. The personal cycles move. They come from the birth date set against the calendar, and they change every year, every month and every day.

There are three of them, nested inside one another. The [[personal-year|personal year]] gives the theme of a calendar year. The [[personal-month|personal month]] shows how that theme is lived month by month. The [[personal-day|personal day]] is the finest grain, the colour of a single date. Each runs from 1 to 9, and each takes the one above it as its base.

The personal years turn in a round of nine. A personal year 1 opens the round, a time the tradition associates with beginnings; a 9 closes it, a time of completion. Each year is read as a step in this round: a 4 builds on what the 1 began, and a 9 clears space for the next 1.

Behind every personal year stands the [[universal-year|universal year]], the number of the calendar year itself, the same for everyone. The personal year is that collective year coloured by one birth date.

These are the short rhythms of numerology. The long ones change on birthdays and last for years: the four [[pinnacle|pinnacles]] and their [[challenge|challenges]], and the three [[period-cycle|period cycles]]. The name has its own rhythm, the [[letter-cycle|letter cycles]], whose letters add up to a yearly [[essence|essence]].

## In practice

Take a made-up person, Camille Marie Laurent, born on 15 June 1990.

- **Personal year.** For 2026, the birth month gives 6, the birth day 15 gives 1 + 5 = 6, and 2026 gives 2 + 0 + 2 + 6 = 10, then 1. Added, 6 + 6 + 1 = 13, then 1 + 3 = 4. Camille’s 2026 is a personal year 4.
- **Personal month.** October is the tenth month, which reduces to 1. Added to the year, 4 + 1 = 5: October 2026 is a personal month 5.
- **Personal day.** On 8 October 2026, the personal month 5 and the day 8 give 5 + 8 = 13, then 4: a personal day 4.
- **The round.** Camille’s last personal year 1 was 2023. Her round reaches its 9 in 2031, and a new one opens in 2032.

Read together, the numbers form layers. A 4 year is associated with work and foundations; inside it, a 5 month brings some movement, and a 4 day suits practical tasks. The year is the background, the month and the day its passing weather: themes to notice, never events fixed in advance.

## Origin and history

Reading a life in steady rhythms is an old idea. In *De die natali*, written in 238, Censorinus reports the belief in climacteric years. These were turning points counted in sevens and nines, with seven tied to the body and nine to the mind. The 63rd year, made of nine sevens or seven nines, drew special attention. Aulus Gellius quotes a letter from the emperor Augustus, glad to have passed it.

The personal year itself is much younger. It belongs to the numerology that took shape in the United States in the early twentieth century. Florence Campbell’s *Your Days Are Numbered* (1931) already speaks of the number that rules a personal year. Juno Jordan’s *Numerology: The Romance in Your Name* (1965) gives chapters to the personal year, the personal months and the personal days. Modern numerology names Pythagoras as its founder, but that link is a later attribution: no ancient text describes these cycles.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune follows the rules of Hans Decoz. The birth month, the birth day and the calendar year are each reduced to one digit, then added and reduced again. No master number is kept in the personal cycles: 11 and 22 become 2 and 4, so every result lies between 1 and 9. A 13, 14, 16 or 19 on the way is not read as a [[karmic-debt|karmic debt]] here.

The personal year runs from 1 January to 31 December, not from birthday to birthday. The personal month adds the reduced calendar month to the personal year. The personal day adds the reduced day of the month to the personal month. The long cycles, by contrast, change on the birthday.

Everything is worked out on the device, from the birth date alone. Ulune shows the personal cycles in the Cycles lens, at /numerology/cycles, with the year, its twelve months and today’s personal day. The Calendar marks the personal day on every date, the personal month on every month and the personal year on every year, and flags each new round of nine.
