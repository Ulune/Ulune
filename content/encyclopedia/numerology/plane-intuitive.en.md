## Meaning

The intuitive plane is one of the four [[planes|planes of expression]], the groups into which numerology sorts the letters of the birth name. It holds seven letters: C, F, K, Q, U, V and Y.

The tradition links this plane to insight, inspiration and a sense of what cannot be measured. Its number is read as the way a person uses intuition: hunches, first impressions, a feel for what lies behind things. Where the mental plane reasons step by step, the intuitive plane arrives all at once.

Two things are read. The count of letters shows how much of the name leans on intuition. The number shows its style, with the usual meaning of each number:

- A [[number-3|3]] here is read as an imaginative intuition, rich in images.
- A [[number-5|5]] is read as quick flashes, intuition fed by experience and movement.
- A [[number-6|6]] is read as an intuition for people, sensing what others need.
- A [[number-7|7]] is read as a deep inner knowing, drawn to the spiritual.
- A [[number-9|9]] is read as a wide, humane kind of insight.

A plane with few letters, or none, is not read as a closed door. Numerology reads it as a quieter channel. Simple habits, such as noting hunches and checking them later, are its classic way in.

## In practice

Take a made-up person, Yolanda Mary Kyle. Her name has 15 letters. Four fall on the intuitive plane: the Y of Yolanda, the Y of Mary, and the K and the Y of Kyle.

Ulune adds them in one total: 7 + 7 + 2 + 7 = 23 → 5. Her intuitive plane is a 5.

Her three Y’s play different parts elsewhere. In Yolanda, the Y comes before a vowel and is read as a consonant. In Mary and in Kyle, it is read as a vowel. On the intuitive plane none of that matters: each Y counts here.

Four letters out of fifteen is a real share. Her [[plane-mental|mental plane]] has six letters, her [[plane-physical|physical plane]] three and her [[plane-emotional|emotional plane]] two. Numerology would read her as someone who thinks with care and trusts her hunches too. The 5 gives those hunches their pace: quick, restless, sharpened by new experiences.

## Across traditions

- **Jung’s intuition.** C. G. Jung’s *Psychologische Typen* (1921) names intuition as one of four functions: perceiving possibilities and what lies behind the obvious. The word is the same, and the idea very close.
- **Fire.** Liz Greene’s *Relating* (1977) pairs Jung’s intuitive type with the element [[element-fire|fire]]. Through her pairing, the intuitive plane meets the fire signs’ vision and enthusiasm. This is a resemblance, not a rule of numerology.

## Origin and history

The planes of expression appear in twentieth-century Western numerology. Florence Campbell’s *Your Days Are Numbered* (1931) already speaks of them. Hans Decoz, whose rules Ulune follows, gives the letter groups Ulune uses, with C, F, K, Q, U, V and Y on the intuitive plane. The values of the letters are the [[pythagorean|Pythagorean]] ones.

## How Ulune calculates it

- Ulune takes the full name at birth and finds every C, F, K, Q, U, V and Y in it. Accents are dropped first, so Ç counts as C and Ü as U.
- Their values: K is worth 2, C and U 3, V 4, F 6, Y 7, Q 8.
- Y always counts here, whether the name reads it as a vowel or a consonant. Switching a Y by hand changes the [[soul-urge|Soul Urge]] and the Personality number, never this plane.
- All of these letters are added in one total for the whole name, not name by name.
- The total is reduced by adding its digits until one is left. 11, 22 and 33 are kept and written with their root (11/2).
- A total such as 13 or 16 on the way is not marked as a [[karmic-debt|karmic debt]]: debts belong to core numbers only.
- A name with none of these letters has no intuitive number. Ulune shows none, never a 0.
