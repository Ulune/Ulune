## Meaning

9 is the last single digit, and any multiple of 9 reduces back to 9: 18, 27, 36. Numerology therefore links it to completion, a number that holds all the others before it. Ulune’s keywords for it are compassion, completion, idealism and generosity.

It stands for a broad concern: humanitarian ideals, tolerance, art and letting go. A 9 tends to think beyond personal interest and is often drawn to causes larger than itself.

On its light side, a 9 is generous, tolerant and able to see the wider picture. It is often creative, and good at closing chapters, ending a project or a stage of life with grace when its time has come.

The shadow is a theme to grow with. A 9 can give to the world while overlooking the people closest to it, or stay disappointed when others fall short of its ideals. Accepting ordinary, imperfect help is part of the work.

9 closes the round of digits that [[number-1|1]] opens. After it the count begins again, and [[number-11|11]] is the first master number.

## In practice

- **[[life-path|Life Path]] 9**: a road of wide horizons, giving without keeping score and letting go of what is finished. The growing edge is to look after one’s own needs too.
- **[[expression|Expression]] 9**: broad talents, a feel for art, people and ideas, often at their best for a cause.
- **[[soul-urge|Soul Urge]] 9**: a wish to give and to make a difference. Choosing where to give helps.
- **[[personality-number|Personality]] 9**: comes across as generous, open and a little idealistic. Clear limits keep the openness from being taken for granted.
- **[[birthday|Birthday]] 9**, for the 9th, 18th or 27th: a talent for understanding people.
- **[[maturity|Maturity]] 9**: a second half of life with a wider heart, once old stories are let go.
- **[[personal-year|Personal Year]] 9**: it closes the nine-year round, a year to finish, clear out and make room for the 1 that follows.
- **[[pinnacle|Pinnacle]] 9**: a stage of generosity and broad interests. A 9 [[period-cycle|period cycle]] opens onto the wider world.
- **[[hidden-passion|Hidden passion]] 9**: most letters of the name fall on 9, a pull towards ideals and people everywhere.
- **[[karmic-lesson|Karmic lesson]] 9**: no letter falls on 9. A wider view grows by giving a little time to others.

## Across traditions

- **Renaissance magic.** Agrippa (1533) writes that nine is dedicated to the Muses, who were nine.
- **Dante.** In the Vita Nuova, Dante calls Beatrice a nine, a miracle whose root is three, the Trinity. The idea of a number with a root is old.
- **The [[chaldean|Chaldean]] values.** Cheiro gives no letter the value 9, yet a name’s total can reduce to it. He gives the number to Mars: energy, courage and determination.
- **The Lo Shu square.** In this Chinese magic square, 9 sits in the middle of the top row, across the central 5 from 1.

## Origin and history

Adding the digits of a number leaves the same remainder on division by 9. Medieval arithmetic used this to check sums, a method called casting out nines, which Fibonacci’s Liber Abaci (1202) teaches. Numerology’s reduction is the same arithmetic read as symbol. The name of Pythagoras came to modern numerology later, through writers of the early 20th century such as L. Dow Balliett.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reduces a number by adding its digits until one is left. Where no master number is kept, the result is always the remainder on division by 9, with 9 in place of 0. So every multiple of 9 gives 9.

Take Bernard Olivier Martin, born 3 November 1978. The vowels of each name are added and reduced apart: Bernard 6, Olivier 29 → 11, kept as a master number, Martin 10 → 1. Then 6 + 11 + 1 = 18 → 9 gives his Soul Urge. Seven of his letters fall on 9, four R and three I, so 9 is also his hidden passion. His fourth pinnacle is 11 + 7 = 18 → 9.

A 9 never appears as a [[challenge|challenge]] or a [[bridge|bridge]]. These are differences between two digits, and run from 0 to 8.

The [[personal-year|personal year]] of 9 ends each nine-year round. Ulune also uses the round for the [[period-cycle|period cycles]]: the first ends at the first personal year 1 on or after the 27th birthday.
