jupiter-in-house-1:
  summary: Jupiter in the 1st house: tradition’s greater benefic in the house of life, read as a confident, generous presence and an appetite for growth.
  opening: The 1st house begins at the [[ascendant|Ascendant]] and is angular, the strongest kind of place, so Jupiter here shows in manner and bearing. Set in what tradition called the house of life, the greater benefic is read as trust in the world, worn openly. Its lesson is measure: a confidence that knows where to stop.
jupiter-in-house-2:
  summary: Jupiter in the 2nd house: growth and trust brought to what one has and values, with an open hand and a question of what is enough.
  opening: Jupiter’s urge to widen things meets the 2nd, the succedent house where resources are built up and kept, and where self-worth sits. Tradition called it the house of livelihood, and the greater benefic here is read as an open hand and a confident sense of value. Its real question is less how much than what is worth having.
jupiter-in-house-3:
  summary: Jupiter in the 3rd house: a wide-ranging, talkative curiosity, with growth sought in everyday learning, siblings, neighbours and short trips.
  opening: In the cadent 3rd, a house of learning and exchange, Jupiter enlarges the everyday mind. Distant horizons, its natural ground in the [[house-9|9th]] opposite, are found close by here: a conversation, a book, a local road. Brothers, sisters and neighbours, the house’s traditional people, are often read as a source of encouragement.
jupiter-in-house-4:
  summary: Jupiter in the 4th house: growth rooted in home and family, read as a welcoming household and a private life with room to expand.
  opening: The 4th is angular but private: its cusp is usually the [[imum-coeli|IC]], the lowest point of the chart. Jupiter here turns its generosity towards family, roots and a home that welcomes others. Its ease shows more behind closed doors than in public view.
jupiter-in-house-5:
  summary: Jupiter in the 5th house: joy, play and creativity on a generous scale, in the house tradition called good fortune.
  opening: Tradition named the 5th the house of good fortune, and the greater benefic placed there doubles the note of delight. This succedent house builds through what is made and enjoyed, so Jupiter grows through creation, romance, children and play. Its one caution is its own: enthusiasm that outruns the hours there are.
jupiter-in-house-6:
  summary: Jupiter in the 6th house: meaning sought in daily work, routines and service, and a generous hand in ordinary tasks.
  opening: Once called the house of toil, the 6th is cadent, a place of upkeep and adjustment. Jupiter brings its wish for meaning down to the scale of a working day: a task done well, a colleague helped. Its gift is making the ordinary feel worthwhile; its trap, taking on more than a week can hold.
jupiter-in-house-7:
  summary: Jupiter in the 7th house: the planet of trust and growth in the house of partners, read as generous bonds and growth through others.
  opening: Angular and facing the self, the 7th begins at the [[descendant|Descendant]] and holds anyone met one to one. With Jupiter there, partners, collaborators and even open rivals are read as channels of growth. What it asks is that the trust given to others stays clear-eyed.
jupiter-in-house-8:
  summary: Jupiter in the 8th house: trust and meaning carried into intimacy, shared resources and times of crisis, where depth becomes growth.
  opening: Jupiter’s optimism meets the 8th, the succedent house of what is held jointly and of deep change. Old texts named it the house of death; modern readers see endings and renewal, and Jupiter here as faith that holds through them. Generosity and trust in close bonds are its strengths.
jupiter-in-house-9:
  summary: Jupiter in the 9th house: the planet of belief and learning in the house of study, long journeys and faith, where it speaks most naturally.
  opening: Few placements match so closely: the 9th covers higher learning, long journeys and belief, the very fields Jupiter signifies. Cadent, the house favours understanding over action, so growth comes through ideas, teachers and distant places. Its joy belongs to the Sun, yet Jupiter is read here as entirely at ease.
jupiter-in-house-10:
  summary: Jupiter in the 10th house: growth and opportunity in career and public life, at the top of the chart where it is most visible.
  opening: At the top of the chart, the angular 10th carries career, reputation and authority, and usually begins at the [[midheaven|Midheaven]]. Jupiter here is read as a public role that widens through mentors, trust and a sense of purpose. Being seen is part of the placement, and so is keeping promises within reach.
jupiter-in-house-11:
  summary: Jupiter in the 11th house, in its joy: the greater benefic in the house of the Good Spirit, read as generous friends, allies and hopes.
  opening: Here Jupiter rejoices: Hellenistic astrologers called the 11th the Good Spirit and made it the joy of the greater benefic. This succedent house gathers support through connections, and Jupiter widens it into friendship, networks and shared ideals. Its hopes for the future tend to be large, and held in common.
jupiter-in-house-12:
  summary: Jupiter in the 12th house: generosity and faith working quietly, through solitude, retreat and help given out of sight.
  opening: Just above the Ascendant lies the 12th, a cadent house of retreat, of the hidden and of the inner life. There Jupiter grows in private, through faith, rest, imagination and help offered unseen. Tradition gave the house hard names, yet the greater benefic in it is often read as a protection that works behind the scenes.
saturn-in-house-1:
  summary: Saturn in the 1st house: patience and structure at the heart of identity, a serious presence whose confidence is built over time.
  opening: The angular 1st, the house of life, brings Saturn into plain view, so its seriousness shows in manner and bearing. Tradition’s greater malefic is read here as a self shaped slowly by responsibility. What begins as reserve tends to mature into steadiness and quiet authority.
saturn-in-house-2:
  summary: Saturn in the 2nd house: patience and structure applied to resources and self-worth, where what is built is meant to last.
  opening: Saturn and the 2nd share a theme, since this succedent house is about building up and keeping. The planet of time asks for patience with resources, and with the sense of one’s own worth. Self-worth here is earned like a craft, and held all the more firmly for it.
saturn-in-house-3:
  summary: Saturn in the 3rd house: careful thinking and deliberate speech, with learning, siblings and daily exchanges taken seriously.
  opening: Saturn slows the everyday mind of the cadent 3rd and makes it exact. Words are weighed before they are spoken, and early lessons may have felt demanding. The patience gained there is read as a lasting skill in thought, study and writing.
saturn-in-house-4:
  summary: Saturn in the 4th house: duty and structure in home and family, where a solid base is built with care over the years.
  opening: Home, roots and a parent belong to the 4th, an angular house turned inward that usually begins at the IC. Saturn there is read as responsibility learnt early in the family, and as a home built deliberately rather than given. In time, that effort becomes a foundation others can lean on.
saturn-in-house-5:
  summary: Saturn in the 5th house: creativity and pleasure given form, where play becomes a craft and self-expression gains confidence with age.
  opening: Tradition’s greater malefic in its house of good fortune makes a sober pairing. In the succedent 5th, Saturn asks that play, romance and creation be given shape and time. Spontaneity may come slowly; what is made instead tends to endure.
saturn-in-house-6:
  summary: Saturn in the 6th house: discipline in daily work and routines, where steady habits and careful method build real competence.
  opening: Both Saturn and the cadent 6th deal in upkeep, method and repeated effort. In what tradition called the house of toil, the planet of time works with its own materials. The trap is a schedule with no slack; the gift, skill built one ordinary day at a time.
saturn-in-house-7:
  summary: Saturn in the 7th house: commitment and responsibility in partnership, where bonds are chosen with care and strengthen over time.
  opening: The 7th, angular and set at the Descendant, is where others are met one to one. Saturn makes partnership a serious matter there: slow to commit, loyal once committed. Its lessons are fairness and shared responsibility, and its bonds are read as ones that mature with the years.
saturn-in-house-8:
  summary: Saturn in the 8th house: care and responsibility around intimacy, shared resources and change, with trust built slowly and deeply.
  opening: Saturn approaches the succedent 8th, house of joint resources, intimacy and crisis, with caution. Trust is given slowly, and deep change is met with structure rather than haste. The old house of death, read now as transformation, becomes here a place where endurance is learned.
saturn-in-house-9:
  summary: Saturn in the 9th house: beliefs tested and built with care, and learning, travel and philosophy approached seriously.
  opening: Saturn wants beliefs that bear weight, and the cadent 9th, house of higher learning and faith, is where it looks for them. Ideas are examined before they are accepted, and long study suits it better than quick conviction. What it reaches is a philosophy earned through experience.
saturn-in-house-10:
  summary: Saturn in the 10th house: ambition, duty and authority at the top of the chart, where standing is earned slowly and kept.
  opening: Saturn knows the 10th well: angular and at the [[midheaven|Midheaven]], it is the house of rank, career and responsibility. Recognition tends to come late and step by step, and to last because of it. Astrologers read authority gained through competence rather than favour.
saturn-in-house-11:
  summary: Saturn in the 11th house: a careful, committed way with friends and groups, and long-term plans built one step at a time.
  opening: The 11th, a succedent house, gathers support through connections; Saturn makes that gathering slow and deliberate. Where tradition placed the Good Spirit, the planet of structure gives alliances a lasting framework. Responsibility within a group, as an organiser or a keeper of its rules, is a common expression.
saturn-in-house-12:
  summary: Saturn in the 12th house, in its joy: the planet of time in the house of retreat, read as quiet discipline and strength built in private.
  opening: Saturn rejoices in the 12th, the cadent house of retreat that ancient authors called the Bad Spirit. The fit is old: solitude, limits and long, unseen effort belong to both. Doubts tend to stay private here, and patient work with them is read as a source of depth.
uranus-in-house-1:

  opening: Uranus spends about seven years in a sign, but rising into the angular 1st it becomes personal and visible. The planet of independence and sudden change shapes manner and first impressions here. It is read as someone who needs room to be different, and who often surprises others, and sometimes themselves.
