mars-conjunction-jupiter:
  summary: Mars conjunct Jupiter: drive and confidence fused into one bold impulse, generous with energy and keen to aim high.
  opening: Tradition’s lesser malefic stands beside its greater benefic here, and Jupiter is read as widening and warming the fire of Mars. The two meet a little more than every two years, so the contact marks a person rather than a generation.

mars-sextile-jupiter:
  summary: Mars sextile Jupiter: an easy link between action and confidence, which opens once a bold first step is taken.
  opening: Two signs apart, Mars and Jupiter stand in elements that suit each other, so courage and faith in the outcome cooperate lightly. It is luck that answers effort: the door opens after Mars moves first.

mars-square-jupiter:
  summary: Mars square Jupiter: a restless push to do more, faster and bigger, which grows into well-measured boldness.
  opening: The warrior and the great benefic share a modality here but not an element, so both move at once in different directions. The square asks Mars to learn proportion from Jupiter, and Jupiter to learn timing from Mars.

mars-trine-jupiter:
  summary: Mars trine Jupiter: action and confidence in a single element, a natural ease in effort that grows when given a worthy aim.
  opening: With both planets in signs of one element, the will to act and the belief that it will work share a temperament. Tradition sees the benefic softening the malefic’s edge, leaving a courage that is generous rather than combative.

mars-opposition-jupiter:
  summary: Mars opposite Jupiter: a tug between acting now and trusting a larger plan, often lived through others who push or promise more.
  opening: Facing each other across the zodiac, action and conviction can each seem to belong to someone else: an impatient partner, an expansive mentor. The opposition settles when both are owned, and enthusiasm learns where its limits lie.

mars-conjunction-saturn:
  summary: Mars conjunct Saturn: drive and discipline fused in one place, a controlled force that ripens into rare endurance.
  opening: Heat and cold meet in this conjunction of tradition’s two malefics, which older texts handled with care. Modern readings stress what it builds, recalling that Mars is exalted in Capricorn, Saturn’s own sign: force does its best work within limits.

mars-sextile-saturn:
  summary: Mars sextile Saturn: effort and patience in quiet cooperation, a knack for steady work that shows once it is put to use.
  opening: At sixty degrees, Mars and Saturn are close enough to cooperate and far enough apart to keep their own pace. Caution turns into method rather than a brake, provided Mars takes the first step.

mars-square-saturn:
  summary: Mars square Saturn: stop and go between drive and caution, a friction that, worked with, forges real stamina and skill.
  opening: Saturn checks Mars at right angles rather than in step, so effort tends to come in bursts and pauses. Read kindly, it is a training ground: each blocked push teaches timing, and the strength that results tends to last.

mars-trine-saturn:
  summary: Mars trine Saturn: drive and discipline in step, the gift of stamina and of patient, well-timed effort.
  opening: The two planets tradition called malefics work together easily in this trine, which modern astrology reads as controlled strength. Drive and a sense of what is realistic share one element, so force knows both its limits and its moment.

mars-opposition-saturn:
  summary: Mars opposite Saturn: a pull between pushing ahead and holding back, often met in authorities or rivals, that teaches balance.
  opening: Across the zodiac, Mars wants to act while Saturn asks for proof. The two often appear as people: a boss who slows things down, a rival who forces patience. Owning both sides turns the standoff into measured, lasting courage.

mars-conjunction-uranus:
  summary: Mars conjunct Uranus: action charged with the unexpected, quick, independent and inventive, at its best when the surge has a purpose.
  opening: Mars catches up with Uranus roughly every twenty-three months, which makes this conjunction personal rather than generational. It fuses impulse with a need to break routine: action that arrives suddenly and on its own terms.

mars-sextile-uranus:
  summary: Mars sextile Uranus: a light, inventive link between action and change, ready for new methods when the chance is taken.
  opening: The sextile gives the drive of Mars a ready partner in the originality of Uranus, without the jolt of the harder contacts. Its inventiveness waits for an opening: a new tool to try, a habit to rework.

mars-square-uranus:
  summary: Mars square Uranus: bursts of energy and impatience with limits, a restless force that grows into nerve and quick thinking under pressure.
  opening: At right angles, Mars and Uranus both want to move at once and neither tolerates a leash, so tension builds and releases in jolts. Its gift, once known, is nerve: fast, original action when a situation suddenly changes.

mars-trine-uranus:
  summary: Mars trine Uranus: quick, original action in easy agreement with change, a gift for meeting the unexpected with skill.
  opening: In one element, the urge to act and the urge to innovate share a temperament. Change is used rather than resisted, like reflexes tuned to the new.

mars-opposition-uranus:
  summary: Mars opposite Uranus: one’s own push meeting sudden change from outside, a tension that teaches flexible, independent action.
  opening: Opposite each other, the push of Mars meets the unpredictability of Uranus, so plans often meet surprises, or people who refuse to follow. Its lesson is freedom learned in relationship: acting independently without breaking every tie.

mars-conjunction-neptune:
  summary: Mars conjunct Neptune: action tied to ideals and imagination, an inspired effort that thrives on a cause worth serving.
  opening: The sharpest planet meets the most diffuse, and the edge of Mars softens into motion guided by feeling or vision. Since Mars moves quickly, the contact usually lasts only a few weeks at a time, so it is a personal signature.

mars-sextile-neptune:
  summary: Mars sextile Neptune: imagination that gently guides action, an opening for inspired effort in art, care or a shared ideal.
  opening: Neptune suggests a direction while Mars still chooses the step, so inspiration does not drain the will. It is a quiet contact, easy to miss until a creative or caring project calls on it.

mars-square-neptune:
  summary: Mars square Neptune: drive and ideals pulling across each other, a haze around motivation that clears into honest, inspired action.
  opening: Mars wants a target and Neptune dissolves targets, so at right angles each keeps unsettling the other. Worked with, the square teaches a rare honesty about motives, and effort that serves something larger than winning.

mars-trine-neptune:
  summary: Mars trine Neptune: action and imagination in one element, a graceful, intuitive way of moving and of working for an ideal.
  opening: Effort follows intuition instead of fighting it when Mars and Neptune share an element. Astrologers often link this trine to grace in movement, and to energy given freely to people or causes.

mars-opposition-neptune:
  summary: Mars opposite Neptune: a see-saw between asserting oneself and yielding, which teaches clear aims and generous effort.
  opening: Assertion and surrender sit at the two ends of this opposition, so one may be lived while the other is met in someone else. Its work is to hold both: to act fully, and to stay open to what cannot be controlled.

mars-conjunction-pluto:
  summary: Mars conjunct Pluto: drive concentrated to its utmost, a willpower that does not let go and can transform whatever it takes on.
  opening: [[scorpio|Scorpio]] belongs to both planets, to Mars by tradition and to Pluto in modern astrology, so their conjunction concentrates one theme twice over. Will comes at full strength; the lesson lies in choosing where to aim it.

mars-sextile-pluto:
  summary: Mars sextile Pluto: a quiet reserve of concentrated energy, available for demanding work once deliberately called on.
  opening: Through the sextile, Mars draws on Pluto’s depth while keeping its own pace. Intensity here is a resource rather than a compulsion, waiting until a real challenge calls it up.

mars-square-pluto:
  summary: Mars square Pluto: intense drive meeting the need for control, a pressure that, worked with, becomes formidable resilience.
  opening: At ninety degrees, the will of Mars and the intensity of Pluto push against each other, raising the stakes of everyday disagreements. Mastered, the square gives power used cleanly: strength that no longer needs to prove itself.

mars-trine-pluto:
  summary: Mars trine Pluto: willpower and depth in one element, the stamina to work under pressure and change things through steady effort.
  opening: Force and intensity move as one in this trine, without strain. Modern astrologers speak of regenerative energy: the capacity to begin again, renewed, after a great effort.

mars-opposition-pluto:
  summary: Mars opposite Pluto: one’s will meeting strong wills in others, a contest that teaches when to stand firm and when to let go.
  opening: This opposition often plays out between people: one person’s drive meets another’s intensity, and both may escalate. It matures when strength is shared rather than contested.

jupiter-conjunction-saturn:
  summary: Jupiter conjunct Saturn: the great conjunction, growth and structure beginning a new twenty-year cycle together.
  opening: Astrologers call this meeting the great conjunction and long used it to time historical eras. The 2020 meeting, at 0° [[aquarius|Aquarius]], opened a series in [[element-air|air signs]] after nearly two centuries spent mostly in earth.

jupiter-sextile-saturn:
  summary: Jupiter sextile Saturn: hope and realism in light cooperation, an opening to grow things steadily and on solid ground.
  opening: The two social planets stand sextile twice in each twenty-year cycle, a few years after their meeting and a few before the next. This is practical optimism: plans that grow because someone keeps tending them.

jupiter-square-saturn:
  summary: Jupiter square Saturn: expansion and restraint taking turns, a friction that teaches sustainable growth.
  opening: In readings after Rudhyar, the waxing square tests what the great conjunction began, and the waning square asks for restructuring before the next one. The square of 2024–2025, four years after the 2020 meeting, was a waxing one.

jupiter-trine-saturn:
  summary: Jupiter trine Saturn: vision and patience in one element, an easy rhythm between growing and consolidating.
  opening: Expansion and the need for solid ground share one temperament when Jupiter and Saturn stand in signs of the same element. One such trine forms in 2026–2027, between Jupiter in Leo and Saturn in Aries.

jupiter-opposition-saturn:
  summary: Jupiter opposite Saturn: optimism and caution face to face, a tension that teaches when to grow and when to consolidate.
  opening: Midway between two great conjunctions, the opposition is read as the moment when what began at the meeting is seen in full. The last fell in 2010–2011; the next spans 2029–2031.

jupiter-trine-uranus:
