sun-conjunction-moon:
  summary: The Sun conjunct the Moon, a birth near the New Moon: will and needs moving as one, a fresh start gathered into a single impulse.
  opening: Here the two lights stand together and the Moon is dark, the moment the [[moon-phase|lunar cycle]] begins again. Tradition reads such a birth as a seed: an impulse that is new and whole, not yet seen from outside.

sun-sextile-moon:
  summary: The Sun sextile the Moon: aims and needs on friendly terms, an inner agreement that shows itself once it is acted on.
  opening: Sixty degrees part the lights, the Moon two signs from the Sun, waxing or waning. Purpose and feeling can cooperate, but the sextile only offers the opening; the agreement shows once a first step is taken.

sun-square-moon:
  summary: The Sun square the Moon, a birth near a quarter Moon: will and needs at right angles, a friction that keeps life moving.
  opening: Born at a quarter Moon, half lit, a person carries aims and needs that want different things at once. Readings after Rudhyar take the waxing square as a push to act, the waning one as a push to rethink: friction that moves things.

sun-trine-moon:
  summary: The Sun trine the Moon: wanting and needing in easy accord, a quiet inner harmony that grows into strength when put to use.
  opening: A third of the circle apart, the lights usually share an element, so purpose and feeling speak with one temperament. Such ease rarely draws attention to itself; it ripens when it is leaned on deliberately rather than taken for granted.

sun-opposition-moon:
  summary: The Sun opposite the Moon, a birth near the Full Moon: will and needs face to face, often met in a partner or close other.
  opening: At the Full Moon the two lights face each other across the sky, and the Moon’s disc is wholly lit. Tradition reads it as a birth turned towards others: one side of the self is often recognised first in someone close, then owned.

sun-conjunction-mercury:
  summary: The Sun conjunct Mercury, a frequent contact: thinking bound up with identity, ideas held as part of who one is.
  opening: Tradition looks first at the distance between them. Within 8° Mercury is [[combust]], its voice drawn into the Sun’s light. Within 17′ it is cazimi, “in the heart of the Sun”, and read as strengthened.

sun-sextile-mercury:
  summary: The Sun sextile Mercury cannot form: Mercury never strays more than about 28° from the Sun, so the pair is read through Mercury’s sign.
  opening: No birth chart holds this aspect. Seen from the Earth, Mercury always stands in the Sun’s sign or one of the two beside it, never two signs away. The advice on sextiles further down speaks of the aspect in general, for other pairs.

sun-square-mercury:
  summary: The Sun square Mercury never occurs in a birth chart: Mercury keeps close to the Sun, and only their conjunction is possible.
  opening: Ninety degrees lie far beyond Mercury’s reach, since its orbit runs inside the Earth’s and keeps it near the Sun in our sky. The friction a square describes has no geometry to stand on here, so the paragraphs below read the nearest real case.

sun-trine-mercury:
  summary: The Sun trine Mercury is impossible: a hundred and twenty degrees is over four times Mercury’s greatest distance from the Sun.
  opening: Mercury rises just before the Sun as a morning star, or sets just after it as an evening star, and never strays farther. A trine between them exists only on paper; what Mercury’s own sign adds to the self is read below.

sun-opposition-mercury:
  summary: The Sun opposite Mercury is out of reach: Mercury keeps company with the Sun, never more than about 28° away, so it never faces it.
  opening: To stand opposite the Sun, a planet must lie beyond the Earth’s orbit, as Mars and the outer planets do. Mercury never can, and the meeting of self and mind is read here through the signs instead.

sun-conjunction-venus:
  summary: The Sun conjunct Venus, a frequent contact: identity warmed by affection and taste, with relationships close to the heart of the self.
  opening: Each meeting of Venus with the Sun is one point of the five-pointed figure it traces around the zodiac every eight years. Within 8° tradition calls Venus [[combust]], its values bent to the will the Sun stands for.

sun-sextile-venus:
  summary: The Sun sextile Venus cannot form: Venus never strays more than about 47° from the Sun, short of the sixty degrees a sextile needs.
  opening: Venus is the morning or evening star, bright beside a Sun it never leaves for long. The nearest friendly link it can make is the [[semi-sextile|semisextile]], thirty degrees, which the reading below describes.

sun-square-venus:
  summary: The Sun square Venus is impossible: Venus stays within about 47° of the Sun, so the closest tension it can form is the semisquare.
  opening: A square asks ninety degrees, nearly twice the farthest Venus ever stands from the Sun. Its milder cousin, the [[semi-square|semisquare]] at forty-five, can form, and the reading below turns to it.

sun-trine-venus:
  summary: The Sun trine Venus never appears in a birth chart: Venus keeps within about 47° of the Sun, far short of a third of the circle.
  opening: Inside the Earth’s orbit, Venus swings from one side of the Sun to the other, never even half a trine away. Whatever ease joins self and affection comes from their signs and their quieter links, not from a trine.

sun-opposition-venus:
  summary: The Sun opposite Venus cannot occur: Venus never moves more than about 47° from the Sun, so it can never face it across the sky.
  opening: Only a planet whose orbit lies beyond the Earth’s can face the Sun, and the orbit of Venus lies within it. Between these two, any tension takes the smaller, nagging form described below.

sun-trine-mars:

sun-opposition-mars:

  opening: Mars, beyond the Earth’s orbit, can stand directly opposite the Sun, as it does when the Earth overtakes it. Born at that moment, a person tends to meet their own fire in others first: a rival, a challenger, a partner who pushes.
