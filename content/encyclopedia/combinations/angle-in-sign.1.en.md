ascendant-in-aries:
  summary: Aries rising: the cardinal fire of Mars on the eastern horizon, a manner that meets the world head-on and learns by doing.
  opening: Aries rising sets the first sign of the zodiac on the degree that opens the chart, so two beginnings coincide. Mars, ruler of Aries, becomes the chart ruler, and its sign and house show where that pioneering approach is aimed.

  opening: With Taurus on the Ascendant, the fixed earth of the second sign shapes the body, the bearing and the pace of first reactions. Venus becomes the chart ruler, so comfort, the senses and what someone values colour the whole approach to life.
