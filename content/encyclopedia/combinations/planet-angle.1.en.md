sun-and-ascendant:
  summary: The Sun tied to the Ascendant: identity and outward manner speak with one voice, most of all with the Sun on the rising degree.
  opening: When the Sun touches the Ascendant, the light of identity meets the degree of first appearance. The conjunction, a birth close to sunrise, is the strongest form: a life lived in plain view, its vitality worn on the surface. Trines, squares and the rest tie the two more quietly.
moon-and-ascendant:
  summary: The Moon tied to the Ascendant: feelings and needs rise to the surface, read most strongly when the Moon is conjunct the rising degree.
  opening: The Moon at the horizon brings the inner tide of feeling up to where others first meet it. Conjunct, the light of the night sits on the degree of body and bearing, and sensitivity becomes part of the visible self. Both points move quickly, the Ascendant a degree about every four minutes, so this link rests on an exact birth time.
mercury-and-ascendant:
  summary: Mercury tied to the Ascendant: the messenger on the horizon, a manner shaped by quick thought, speech and curiosity.
  opening: Mercury on the Ascendant puts the messenger at the door of the chart, so thinking and speaking become part of the bearing itself. The conjunction is the clearest case. A trine or sextile lets wit flow into manner, while a square asks words and first reactions to find a shared pace.
venus-and-ascendant:
  summary: Venus tied to the Ascendant: grace on the horizon, a manner of charm and ease, fullest with Venus on the rising degree.
  opening: Venus, the lesser benefic of tradition, joined to the Ascendant lends the outer self its pleasantness and taste. Close to the rising degree, the planet of beauty and of what someone values shapes appearance and manner directly. Opposite, on the [[descendant]], the same Venus turns towards partners instead.
mars-and-ascendant:
  summary: Mars tied to the Ascendant: drive and courage on the horizon, a direct, energetic bearing, strongest with Mars on the rising degree.
  opening: Mars meeting the Ascendant brings the force that acts and defends right to the surface of the personality. In conjunction, the tradition reads the bearing as charged with Mars: quick to move, ready to stand up. Square or opposite, the same energy asks to be channelled, and learning that turns it into a reliable strength.
jupiter-and-ascendant:

  opening: Jupiter close to the Ascendant sets the greater benefic of tradition at the door of the chart. Its growth and trust spread into the whole manner, which others often sense before a word is said. Every aspect counts, but the conjunction is the one people notice first.
saturn-and-ascendant:

  opening: Saturn, the planet of structure, limits and maturity, standing near the Ascendant gives the outer self a certain weight. The conjunction is the strongest reading; squares and oppositions are read as a call to build self-trust patiently. What starts as caution often becomes the steadiness others rely on.

pluto-and-ascendant:
  summary: Pluto tied to the Ascendant: intensity on the horizon, a presence charged with depth and quiet power, strongest in conjunction.

Other aspects link the self to its public direction, and like every contact with an angle they hang on an exact birth time.
