sun-in-aries:
  summary: The Sun in Aries, in its exaltation: a self found by beginning, daring and acting first.
  opening: Here the light of identity meets the first sign, the one the Sun enters at the March equinox. Tradition [[exaltation|exalts]] it at 19° of Aries and reads will at its most direct: a self that exists by starting.

sun-in-taurus:
  summary: The Sun in Taurus: a sense of self rooted in steadiness, the senses and what is built to last.
  opening: Fixed earth gives the Sun a slow, patient burn. Under Venus’s rule, identity grows by accumulation, measured more by what it tends and keeps than by what it proclaims.

sun-in-gemini:
  summary: The Sun in Gemini: a self that lives through curiosity, conversation and the links between ideas.
  opening: Gemini belongs to Mercury, so the Sun here borrows the messenger’s ways. Mutable air keeps identity in motion, defined less by one role than by the many connections it makes.

sun-in-cancer:
  summary: The Sun in Cancer: an identity centred on care, belonging and the people one calls one’s own.
  opening: The Sun enters Cancer at the June solstice, the longest day of the northern year, in the sign ruled by the Moon. Solar purpose takes on lunar themes here: shelter, memory and kin.

sun-in-leo:
  summary: The Sun in Leo, in its domicile: warmth, creative self-expression and a heart that wants to be seen.
  opening: Leo is the one sign the Sun rules, its [[domicile]], where the planet speaks in its own voice. Fixed fire keeps that light constant, and astrologers read generosity, pride and a gift for making things glow.

sun-in-virgo:
  summary: The Sun in Virgo: a sense of self tied to skill, usefulness and getting things right.
  opening: In Mercury’s earth sign, the wish to shine turns towards craft. Virgo’s Sun is understood as identity earned through discernment, where real service is its own form of recognition.

sun-in-libra:
  summary: The Sun in Libra, in its fall: a self discovered through others, with a gift for fairness and judgement.
  opening: The Sun reaches Libra at the September equinox, when day and night stand equal. Tradition counts the sign as its [[fall]]: solar self-assertion yields to Venus’s sign of partnership, and strength comes through balance.

sun-in-scorpio:
  summary: The Sun in Scorpio: a self forged in depth, intensity and the wish to understand what is hidden.
  opening: Fixed water, ruled by Mars and, for modern astrologers, Pluto, gives the Sun a concentrated, inward heat. What shines here is a will that transforms by going deep.

sun-in-sagittarius:
  summary: The Sun in Sagittarius: a self oriented by meaning, freedom and the widest possible horizon.
  opening: Jupiter’s fire sign carries the Sun towards the far view. Mutable and restless, the placement casts identity as a quest, shaped by belief, learning and the distance travelled.

sun-in-capricorn:
  summary: The Sun in Capricorn: an identity built through structure, responsibility and achievement that lasts.
  opening: At the December solstice, in the depth of the northern winter, the Sun enters Capricorn as the light begins to return. Saturn’s sign teaches solar will patience: a self that rises slowly and keeps what it climbs to.

sun-in-aquarius:
  summary: The Sun in Aquarius, in its detriment: a self that shines through ideas, independence and the group.
  opening: Aquarius faces Leo across the zodiac, which puts the Sun in its [[detriment]], far from its own sign. Saturn’s air sign turns the solar light outward, towards principle and community.

sun-in-pisces:
  summary: The Sun in Pisces: an identity drawn from empathy, imagination and a feeling of belonging to something vast.
  opening: The last sign of the zodiac, ruled by Jupiter and, for modern astrologers, Neptune, softens the Sun’s outline. Purpose often comes to light through compassion, art or devotion, in a self with porous edges.

moon-in-aries:
  summary: The Moon in Aries: feelings that flare fast and pass quickly, with a need for independence and action.
  opening: The planet of needs and habits meets Mars’s cardinal fire here. Emotional life takes on the sign’s speed, and comfort is found in movement rather than in stillness.

moon-in-taurus:
  summary: The Moon in Taurus, in its exaltation: steady emotions and a deep need for comfort, routine and the senses.
  opening: Venus’s earth sign holds the Moon’s [[exaltation]], at 3°. Calm ground suits the light of the night, and the pairing is read as emotional security made solid, through body, food and familiar places.

moon-in-gemini:
  summary: The Moon in Gemini: feelings processed through words, curiosity and a steady flow of contact.
  opening: With Mercury as its ruler, Gemini turns the Moon’s needs mental. Emotional life comes alive in exchange, where understanding a feeling is part of feeling safe.

moon-in-cancer:
  summary: The Moon in Cancer, in its domicile: strong instincts for nurture, home and emotional memory.
  opening: Cancer is the Moon’s own sign, its [[domicile]], where the luminary speaks its native language. Cardinal water gives that care initiative: a person who makes a home wherever they belong.

moon-in-leo:
  summary: The Moon in Leo: a warm, generous heart that needs loyalty, appreciation and room to shine.
  opening: The Sun’s sign hosts the Moon here, so the two lights share one theme. Fixed fire makes feeling open and constant, a need to give warmth and to see it returned.

moon-in-virgo:
  summary: The Moon in Virgo: comfort found in order, usefulness and the sense that things are properly handled.
  opening: Mercury rules this mutable earth sign, and the Moon in it turns care into something precise. Needs settle through tending and sorting, and competence becomes a form of comfort.

moon-in-libra:
  summary: The Moon in Libra: a need for harmony, companionship and fairness to feel emotionally at ease.
  opening: Placed in Venus’s cardinal air, the Moon looks to others for its balance. Astrologers describe a heart tuned to atmosphere and relationship, most at peace when the bond between people is even.

moon-in-scorpio:
  summary: The Moon in Scorpio, in its fall: feelings that run deep, loyal and private, with trust before closeness.
  opening: Across from Taurus, where the Moon is exalted, Scorpio is the sign of its [[fall]]. Fixed water holds feeling rather than releasing it: an emotional life of great depth, slow to open and slow to let go.

moon-in-sagittarius:
  summary: The Moon in Sagittarius: comfort found in freedom, humour, faith and an open road.
  opening: Jupiter’s mutable fire lends the Moon a restless optimism. Emotional security becomes a matter of horizon, with needs met by belief, learning and the sense that life is going somewhere.

moon-in-capricorn:
  summary: The Moon in Capricorn, in its detriment: feelings held with reserve, and security found in competence.
  opening: Opposite Cancer, its own sign, the Moon stands in [[detriment]] in Saturn’s earth. Feeling is given a structure here: needs carried with dignity, which soften as trust and time allow.

moon-in-aquarius:
  summary: The Moon in Aquarius: a need for freedom and friendship, and a habit of viewing feelings from a distance.
  opening: Fixed air, ruled by Saturn and, for modern astrologers, Uranus, cools the Moon’s tide. This emotional life belongs to the circle more than the couple, and shared ideals steady it.

moon-in-pisces:
  summary: The Moon in Pisces: deep sensitivity and empathy, with a need for quiet to recover from the world.
  opening: In the last water sign of the zodiac, the Moon is read as feeling without firm edges. Compassion comes easily, and imagination becomes a refuge as well as a gift.

mercury-in-aries:
  summary: Mercury in Aries: a fast, direct mind that learns by doing and speaks before others dare.
  opening: The messenger meets Mars’s fire, and thought becomes an act of will. Reasoning here is quick and decisive, more at ease with a first impulse than with long deliberation.

mercury-in-taurus:
  summary: Mercury in Taurus: slow, practical, thorough thinking, with opinions that hold once formed.
  opening: Venus’s fixed earth slows Mercury to the sign’s pace. Ideas are tested by the hands and the senses, and prized for being solid more than for being new.

mercury-in-gemini:
  summary: Mercury in Gemini, in its domicile: an agile, curious mind at ease with words, news and many conversations.
  opening: Gemini is the first of Mercury’s two homes, an air sign where its [[domicile]] gives the mind full range. Mutable and quick, it describes an intelligence that thrives on variety and links one fact to the next.

mercury-in-cancer:
  summary: Mercury in Cancer: a mind shaped by feeling and memory, attentive to what is meant beneath the words.
  opening: Within the Moon’s water sign, Mercury thinks in feelings and recollections. Its intelligence is one of tone and context, closer to listening than to abstract argument.

mercury-in-leo:
  summary: Mercury in Leo: confident, expressive thinking, with a gift for storytelling and holding an audience.
  opening: Leo’s fire, ruled by the Sun, gives Mercury a stage. Thought turns warm and authoritative, an intelligence that likes to convince and puts its pride into what it says.

mercury-in-virgo:
  summary: Mercury in Virgo, in domicile and exaltation: precise, analytical, practical thinking at its strongest.
  opening: Mercury is the only planet exalted in a sign it rules, and Virgo is that sign, with the [[exaltation]] at 15°. Earth gives the messenger method and craft: thought made useful.

mercury-in-libra:
  summary: Mercury in Libra: a mind that weighs, compares and finds the diplomatic phrase.
  opening: Venus’s air sign lends Mercury a sense of proportion. Reasoning becomes sociable and balanced, at its best in dialogue and always in search of the fair formulation.

mercury-in-scorpio:
  summary: Mercury in Scorpio: an investigative mind drawn to motives, secrets and what is left unspoken.
  opening: Mars rules this fixed water sign, and it gives Mercury a probing edge. Such a mind concentrates rather than scatters, patient enough to dig until it reaches the real question.

mercury-in-sagittarius:
  summary: Mercury in Sagittarius, in its detriment: a mind of broad views, beliefs and big ideas, less patient with detail.
  opening: Sagittarius faces Gemini, so Mercury is in [[detriment]] in Jupiter’s fire. The messenger works at the scale of meaning, where vision leads and precision becomes a skill worth cultivating.

mercury-in-capricorn:
  summary: Mercury in Capricorn: structured, goal-minded thinking that is concise, realistic and reliable.
  opening: In Saturn’s cardinal earth, Mercury thinks like an architect. Order and consequence guide it, and understanding is built step by step towards a practical end.

mercury-in-aquarius:
  summary: Mercury in Aquarius: original, logical thinking that sees patterns and questions the usual way.
  opening: Saturn’s fixed air, which modern astrologers also give to Uranus, lends Mercury both rigour and invention. Its signature is a mind at home with systems and principles, often a step ahead of its time.

mercury-in-pisces:
  summary: Mercury in Pisces, in detriment and fall: a mind of images, impressions and intuition more than lists.
  opening: Pisces is both Mercury’s [[detriment]] and its [[fall]], so the messenger works here without its usual tools. Meaning is gathered by resonance, an intelligence closer to the poet’s than the clerk’s.

venus-in-pisces:

mars-in-virgo:

mars-in-libra:

mars-in-scorpio:

mars-in-capricorn:

mars-in-aquarius:
