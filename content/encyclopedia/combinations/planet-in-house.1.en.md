sun-in-house-1:
  summary: The Sun in the 1st house: identity worn openly, with vitality and purpose that show in presence and manner.
  opening: Set in the house that begins at the [[ascendant]], the Sun lends its light to the personality itself. The 1st is angular, the strongest kind of house, and was called the house of life. Will and vitality are read here as plainly visible, in bearing as much as in choices.
sun-in-house-2:
  summary: The Sun in the 2nd house: a sense of self built through what one earns, keeps and values.
  opening: Resources, and the worth a person gives to themselves, are where the Sun’s purpose gathers in the 2nd. Succedent houses build up and keep. Hellenistic astrologers read this one as less active, since it makes no classical aspect to the rising sign. The classic reading is an identity grounded in values, skill and a solid base.
sun-in-house-3:
  summary: The Sun in the 3rd house: a self defined by curiosity, conversation, learning and the near world of siblings and neighbours.
  opening: The Goddess was the Hellenistic name of the 3rd, the joy of the Moon, facing the 9th where the Sun itself rejoices. Placed here, the light of the day shines on the near world rather than the far one. This cadent house turns its purpose towards learning, exchange and everyday movement.
sun-in-house-4:
  summary: The Sun in the 4th house: an identity rooted in home, family, place and private life.
  opening: Near the [[imum-coeli]], the lowest point of the chart, the light of the day turns inward. Angular houses give strength, but the 4th shows its planets more in private than in public. Tradition sees a self that grows from its roots and finds its centre where it belongs.
sun-in-house-5:
  summary: The Sun in the 5th house: a self that comes alive in creating, playing, loving and shining in its own way.
  opening: Modern practice pairs the 5th with Leo, the sign the Sun rules, so the light is at ease here. Its Hellenistic name was Good Fortune, and its joy belonged to Venus. Succedent, it builds through what is made and enjoyed: the Sun here wants to create, and to be seen creating.
sun-in-house-6:
  summary: The Sun in the 6th house: identity found in useful work, good routines and skill refined day after day.
  opening: Here the Sun’s will turns to the craft of ordinary days. Hellenistic astrologers named this cadent house Bad Fortune, a hard name that modern practice softens into work, care and maintenance. The placement suggests purpose found in being useful, in method, and in skills refined over time.
sun-in-house-7:
  summary: The Sun in the 7th house: an identity that comes alive in relationship, partnership and one-to-one meetings.
  opening: Close to the [[descendant]], the degree setting in the west, the Sun places its light in the house of the other. Angular and strong, the 7th is often lived through partners, collaborators and open rivals. The self discovers its shape in relationship here, and learns to keep its own centre there.
sun-in-house-8:
  summary: The Sun in the 8th house: a self shaped by depth, trust, what is shared with others and times of deep change.
  opening: Idle place was the Hellenistic name of the 8th, which makes no classical aspect to the rising sign. Modern astrology reads this succedent house as shared resources, intimacy and transformation. With the Sun here, identity gathers around depth, trust, and the strength found whenever life asks for renewal.
sun-in-house-9:
  summary: The Sun in the 9th house, in its joy: an identity found through study, travel, belief or a calling.
  opening: This is the Sun’s joy: Hellenistic astrologers called the 9th the God, and said the light of the day was happiest here. Being cadent, the house favours understanding over action. Purpose is read as widening with every journey, teaching or question of meaning.
sun-in-house-10:
  summary: The Sun in the 10th house: identity tied to vocation, public role and what one is known for.
  opening: Near the [[midheaven]], the highest point a planet reaches in its daily course, the Sun stands in full view. The 10th is angular, traditionally the house of rank and action. Astrologers read a self defined by its vocation, one that grows by taking on responsibility in the world.
sun-in-house-11:
  summary: The Sun in the 11th house: a self that shines among friends, groups and shared hopes for the future.
  opening: The 11th was the Good Spirit of Hellenistic astrology, house of benefactors and joy of Jupiter. Succedent, it builds support through connections. The Sun placed among friends and causes is understood as an identity that grows with the people it chooses to build with.
sun-in-house-12:
  summary: The Sun in the 12th house: a light that shines best behind the scenes, in solitude, inner work or quiet service.
  opening: Out of sight is where the light of the day works in the 12th. This cadent house, the joy of Saturn, was once named for confinement and self-undoing. Modern astrology reads it as retreat, rest and the inner life, where a self grows strong in quiet or in serving something larger.
