jupiter-in-aries:
  summary: Jupiter in Aries: faith that grows by daring, read as a confidence renewed each time a first step is taken.
  opening: Jupiter’s wish to widen life meets the first sign of the zodiac, the cardinal fire sign of Mars. Optimism here has to move: belief proves itself in action rather than in theory.
jupiter-in-taurus:
  summary: Jupiter in Taurus: abundance trusted when it can be touched, read as growth that comes slowly and is made to last.
  opening: In the fixed earth sign of Venus, the greater benefic slows down and takes root. Its faith rests on what can be grown, kept and enjoyed. Like every Jupiter sign, it is shared by those born in the same year or so.
jupiter-in-gemini:
  summary: Jupiter in Gemini, in its detriment: a search for meaning spread over many sources, read as wisdom gathered piece by piece.
  opening: Gemini lies opposite Sagittarius, Jupiter’s own sign, so the planet is in [[detriment]] here. The seeker of one great truth lands in the sign of many small ones, and understanding grows by comparing voices.
jupiter-in-cancer:
  summary: Jupiter in Cancer, exalted: a generosity that nurtures, read as growth rooted in home, care and the people one belongs to.
  opening: Traditional astrology honours Jupiter in Cancer with its [[exaltation]], placed at 15° of the sign. As the guest of the Moon’s water sign, the benefic of trust expands by sheltering: faith is first learned at home.
jupiter-in-leo:
  summary: Jupiter in Leo: a large-hearted confidence, read as growth that comes through creating, shining and helping others shine.
  opening: Two warm forces meet when Jupiter enters Leo, the Sun’s fixed fire sign. Faith turns generous and visible, and in a night chart Jupiter also rules the fire [[triplicity]], a modest dignity of its own here.
jupiter-in-virgo:
  summary: Jupiter in Virgo, in its detriment: large hopes put to work in careful steps, read as a faith proved by being of use.
  opening: Opposite Pisces, one of the two signs it rules, Jupiter is in [[detriment]] in Virgo. Here the great widener works in the sign of sorting and refining. Its faith learns to live in the small: the measured gesture, the promise kept to the letter.
jupiter-in-libra:
  summary: Jupiter in Libra: faith in fairness and in other people, read as growth that comes through partnership and agreement.
  opening: In Venus’s cardinal air sign, Jupiter’s sense of justice turns towards the other person. This is the believer in dialogue: what grows is shared, negotiated and balanced, and law and good manners serve the same ideal.
jupiter-in-scorpio:
  summary: Jupiter in Scorpio: growth through depth, read as a faith that is tested by crisis and comes out of it stronger.
  opening: Scorpio is the fixed water sign that Mars rules by tradition, with Pluto beside it for the moderns. Jupiter’s expansion goes down here rather than out, seeking meaning in what is hidden, intense or held in common.
jupiter-in-sagittarius:
  summary: Jupiter in Sagittarius, its domicile: the planet of faith in the sign of the seeker, read as optimism, study and wide horizons.
  opening: This is Jupiter on its own ground, for Sagittarius is its fire [[domicile]], the sign that most resembles it. Planet and sign speak one language of journeys, teaching and belief, so gifts and excesses both come out at full size.
jupiter-in-capricorn:
  summary: Jupiter in Capricorn, in its fall: optimism tempered by realism, read as growth that is built, earned and made to last.
  opening: Capricorn, Saturn’s cardinal earth sign and the place of Mars’s exaltation, is Jupiter’s [[fall]]. Faith meets limits here, and the tradition reads that meeting as a schooling: a hope tested by time tends to keep its promises.
jupiter-in-aquarius:
  summary: Jupiter in Aquarius: faith in the future and in the group, read as growth that comes through ideas, friendship and shared causes.
  opening: The traditional ruler of Aquarius is [[saturn]], and Jupiter enters its sign as a guest with ideals in hand. Its expansion turns humanitarian and inventive, a belief in progress, in fair systems and in what people can build together.
jupiter-in-pisces:
  summary: Jupiter in Pisces, its domicile: generosity without measure, read as a faith that flows through compassion, imagination and devotion.
  opening: Pisces is Jupiter’s water [[domicile]], the sign it ruled alone before modern astrologers gave it to [[neptune]]. Here the greater benefic is at its most mystical, and belief becomes trust in the unseen.
