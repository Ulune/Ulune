## Meaning

The 5th house is the house of what one does for joy rather than duty: play, pleasure, romance and love affairs, art, performance, games and sport, and children, both the children of the body and the children of the mind. Astrologers read it as the place where a person expresses themselves freely and makes something that carries their mark.

It is a succedent house, following the angle of the 4th, and it is read as what grows from a secure base: the home makes room for play and for the next generation. Its opposite is the [[house-11|11th house]], and the axis they form is that of personal and shared joys: one’s own creation and love on one side, friends, groups and common hopes on the other.

It asks to be read with a light hand: a planet in the 5th does not decide whether there will be children, and says nothing fixed about love affairs; it describes the spirit in which a person plays, loves and creates, and what makes them feel most alive.

## In practice

- **Venus in the 5th**, in its joy, is often read as pleasure in love, art and company; **Saturn in the 5th**, as creativity that grows through discipline, and joy that is learned as much as found; **Mars in the 5th**, as a love of competition, sport and bold play.
- **The [[sun]] in the 5th** is often read as a strong need to create and to shine in one’s own way.
- **The sign on the cusp** describes the style of play and affection, and its ruler shows where enjoyment is found.
- **An empty 5th house** rules nothing out, not creativity, romance or children; it is read through its cusp and its ruler.

## Across traditions

- **Jyotish** calls this house putra, children, and also reads it for intelligence, learning and the merit carried from the past; it is one of the most favoured houses there, a trikona and a house of dharma.
- **Zi Wei Dou Shu** has a palace of Children among its twelve palaces; it is found by another method and read on its own terms.

## Origin and history

Hellenistic astrologers called this place Good Fortune, agathē tychē, and made it the joy of [[venus]]. Lilly read the 5th for children and for a woman with child, for banquets, ale-houses, taverns and plays, and for messengers and ambassadors. Modern astrology, following the sign of the same number, Leo, made it above all the house of creativity, self-expression and the heart’s pleasures.

## How Ulune calculates it

The cusp of the 5th comes from Swiss Ephemeris in the chosen system: in Placidus, Ulune’s default, it divides by time the passage of degrees between the lowest point of the sky and the western horizon; in Porphyry, it lies a third of the way along the zodiac arc from the IC to the Descendant; in Equal houses it lies 120° after the Ascendant, and in Whole sign houses the 5th is the fifth sign from the rising sign. Its opposite cusp is always that of the 11th. A body counts in the 5th from this cusp to the cusp of the 6th.
