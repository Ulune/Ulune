## Meaning

A trine joins two bodies a third of the circle apart. Their signs are four apart and, as a rule, of the same element: Aries, Leo and Sagittarius for fire; Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn for earth; Gemini, Libra and Aquarius for air; Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces for water. The two bodies share a temperament, and their functions support each other without being asked.

The trine is the aspect of ease. What it joins tends to work smoothly: a talent that seems to have always been there, a combination of qualities that others find natural and pleasant to be around. It is the classic good aspect, called harmonious since Ptolemy.

Ease has its own risk. Because a trine never insists, it can stay a pleasant background rather than become a strength: a gift used without effort is seldom developed, and a chart rich in trines but poor in tension may lack the push that drives growth. A trine gives most when it is put to work on purpose, often with help from a square or an opposition elsewhere in the chart that supplies the push.

Three bodies each in trine with the other two close a triangle in one element, the [[grand-trine|grand trine]].

## In practice

- [[moon|Moon]] trine [[venus|Venus]]: feelings and affection agree. Warmth, tact and an easy charm.
- [[mars|Mars]] trine [[saturn|Saturn]]: drive and discipline in step. Steady effort, and the stamina for long projects.
- [[jupiter|Jupiter]] trine the [[midheaven|Midheaven]]: confidence and generosity flow into public life and work.
- A trine that reaches across the border of a sign, such as 29° Cancer to 1° Sagittarius, joins two elements instead of one: it is an [[out-of-sign|out-of-sign aspect]], easy in its degrees but mixed in temperament.

## Across traditions

- **Indian astrology** gives Jupiter special glances at the fifth and ninth signs from its own, both trines, and counts the fifth and ninth houses, with the first, as the *trikona* houses, among the most favourable of the chart.
- **Chinese astrology** groups the twelve branches into four triangles, the *three harmonies*: branches four apart, such as the Monkey, the Rat and the Dragon, combine into one of the five phases. The same triangle, read in another language.

## Origin and history

In the *Tetrabiblos* (2nd century), Ptolemy describes the trine as a triangular distance between two signs, containing one right angle and a third, four signs or 120°, and calls it harmonious, with the sextile, because it joins signs of the same kind. The four triangles of signs, one for each element, are the [[triplicity|triplicities]] of traditional astrology, each with planets of its own as rulers. The word trine comes from the Latin *trinus*, threefold, while the French *trigone* keeps the Greek word for a triangle.

## How Ulune calculates it

- The orb is 8° between two bodies, and 6° when one of them is an angle (the Ascendant, the Descendant, the Midheaven or the IC). In synastry it is 6°, or 7° when the Sun or the Moon is involved, and never more than 6° to an angle.
- A trine between signs that are not four apart is marked [[out-of-sign|out of sign]].
- Trines make three of the configurations Ulune looks for: three bodies each trine the other two form a grand trine; a fourth body opposite one corner and sextile the other two turns it into a [[kite|kite]]; two trines and two sextiles around two oppositions make a [[mystic-rectangle|mystic rectangle]].
