## Meaning

The 9th house is everything that widens a person’s view of the world: long journeys and foreign places, higher learning and teachers, beliefs, religion and philosophy, law, publishing and the exchange of ideas between cultures. Astrologers read it as the search for meaning, the question of what one believes and why.

It is a cadent house, falling away from the angle of the 10th, and it is read as understanding and preparation more than as action. Its opposite is the [[house-3|3rd house]], and the axis they form is that of near and far knowledge: the street and the horizon, the facts of the day and the frame that makes sense of them.

Planets here are often read as a compass. A 9th house that holds much tends to show in a life shaped by a calling, a faith or a long apprenticeship, in the need to go and see for oneself, and in a readiness to teach what one has found.

## In practice

- **The Sun in the 9th**, in its joy, is often read as an identity found through study, travel or a calling; **Saturn in the 9th**, as beliefs earned through doubt and serious, patient study; **Venus in the 9th**, as a love of travel, art and other cultures.
- **The sign on the cusp** describes the approach to study and belief, and its ruler shows where the search for meaning leads: with the ruler of the 9th in the 7th, perhaps through a partner from another background.
- **Planets passing through the 9th** by [[transit|transit]] tend to mark times of study, travel or a change of outlook.
- **An empty 9th house** says nothing against travel or ideas; it is read through its cusp and its ruler.

## Across traditions

- **Jyotish** calls this house dharma, right action, or bhagya, fortune, and reads it for the father and the teacher, luck, higher learning, religion and pilgrimage; it is the most favoured of the trikonas, a house of dharma.
- **Zi Wei Dou Shu** has a palace of Travel among its twelve palaces; it is found by another method and read on its own terms.

## Origin and history

Hellenistic astrologers called this place the God, theos, and made it the joy of the [[sun]]; facing it, the 3rd was the Goddess, the joy of the Moon. Lilly read the 9th for long voyages beyond the sea, religion and the clergy, dreams and visions, foreign countries, books and learning, and the relatives of one’s spouse. Modern astrology, following the sign of the same number, Sagittarius, and its ruler [[jupiter]], made it above all the house of worldview, meaning and wide horizons.

## How Ulune calculates it

The cusp of the 9th comes from Swiss Ephemeris in the chosen system and always lies exactly opposite the cusp of the 3rd: in Placidus, Ulune’s default, it is the second time division between the western horizon and the top of the sky; in Equal houses it lies 240° after the Ascendant, and in Whole sign houses the 9th is the ninth sign from the rising sign. Under Equal or Whole sign houses, the Midheaven itself can fall in the 9th. In the table’s balance, the bodies in the 9th count among the cadent houses.
