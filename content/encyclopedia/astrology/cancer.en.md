## Meaning

Cancer is the fourth sign of the zodiac, from 90° to 120°, opened by the June [[season|solstice]]. It is [[element-water|water]] in its [[modality-cardinal|cardinal]] form, ruled by the [[moon]], and astrologers read it as the sign of care and belonging: the instinct to protect, to feed, to make a place where people feel safe.

A planet in Cancer tends to act from feeling and memory. It reads moods before words, remembers what mattered to people, and moves to defend what it is attached to. As a cardinal sign it is not passive: it takes the initiative, but through attachment rather than conquest. Its gifts are tenderness, loyalty, tenacity and a deep sense of home.

Its difficulties are the same attachment held too tight: retreating into a shell when hurt, taking things personally, keeping the past closer than the present. The opposite sign, [[capricorn]], brings structure, duty and public life; the pair is read as the axis of home and the world, of private roots and the role one plays outside.

Tradition exalts [[jupiter]] here, at 15°, generosity at its warmest when it protects; [[saturn]] is in its [[detriment|detriment]] and [[mars]] in its [[fall|fall]], which says that cold rules and open force are not this sign’s native tools.

## In practice

- **The [[sun]] in Cancer**, from about 21 June to 22 or 23 July (the day shifts from year to year): a sense of self rooted in family, in chosen kin or in a place.
- **The Moon in Cancer** is in its [[domicile|domicile]], its only one: needs and feelings flow easily, and caring for others comes naturally.
- **Cancer rising**, on the [[ascendant|Ascendant]]: a gentle, watchful first impression. The Moon rules the chart, so its sign, house and phase say much about the whole person.
- **Jupiter in Cancer**, exalted, gives warmly and widely. **Mars in Cancer**, in fall, tends to fight to defend rather than to win, and indirectly before openly. **Saturn in Cancer** has to learn that dependence and strength can live together.
- **Cancer on a house cusp**: that area of life is approached protectively, guided by feelings and memories.

## Across traditions

- **Jyotish** calls the sign Karka, the crab, gives it to the Moon and exalts Jupiter there too; measured from the stars, it begins about 24° further on, and the Sun enters it around 16 July.
- **Human Design** places gates 52, 39, 53 and 62 wholly in Cancer, with the end of gate 15 and the start of gate 56.
- **The Chinese calendar** opens Cancer with Xiàzhì, the summer solstice, and sets Xiǎoshǔ, “minor heat”, at 15° Cancer, where the month of the Goat (wèi) begins.

## Origin and history

Babylonian astronomers already knew these faint stars as the Crab. The Greeks, who called it Karkinos, told how Hera sent a crab against Heracles while he fought the Hydra, and set it in the sky after he crushed it. At its heart lies the Beehive, a cluster just visible to the naked eye; precession has carried it to about 7° of the sign Leo.

The Tropic of Cancer keeps the name it took when the Sun reached its June solstice among the Crab’s stars. By the boundaries astronomers published in 1930, the solstice point left the constellation Cancer around the turn of the eras, and since 1990 it has fallen in Taurus; the Sun now crosses Cancer from about 21 July to 11 August. Ptolemy explained why the Moon rules Cancer and the Sun Leo: he counted them the warmest signs, the closest to the zenith, and gave them to the two lights.

## How Ulune calculates it

A body is in Cancer from 90° to 120° of tropical longitude, measured from the March equinox point. In the dignity table the Moon holds its domicile here and Jupiter its exaltation, at 15° by tradition; Saturn is in detriment and Mars in fall. The [[triplicity|triplicity]] rulers are [[venus]] by day, Mars by night and the Moon participating, after Dorotheus; the [[term|terms]] run Mars to 7°, Venus to 13°, [[mercury]] to 19°, Jupiter to 26° and Saturn to 30°, and the [[face|faces]] are Venus, Mercury and the Moon. The readings’ [[decan|decans]] are those of Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces, ruled by the Moon, Pluto and Neptune, the modern rulers of the last two.
