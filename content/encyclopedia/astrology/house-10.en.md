## Meaning

The 10th house lies at the top of the chart, around the point of the zodiac that was culminating overhead at birth, the [[midheaven|Midheaven]]. Astrologers read it as public life: career and vocation, reputation and standing, achievements, the relationship with bosses and authority, and what a person is known for, or wants to be.

It is an angular house, the most elevated of the four, and planets here are read as strong and conspicuous: they tend to shape a public role. Its opposite is the [[house-4|4th house]], and the axis they form is that of public and private life, of the role one plays in the world and the roots it grows from.

The 10th is read as a direction rather than a destination. It describes the kind of work and recognition that feel right, not a fixed career, and many people live its themes through several jobs, or through a role that has no job title at all.

## In practice

- **Saturn in the 10th** is often read as a slow, steady climb, responsibility taken early and authority earned over time; **Venus in the 10th**, as grace in public life and work touching art, beauty or diplomacy; **the Moon in the 10th**, as a visible life and a public role built on care.
- **The sign on the Midheaven** describes the reputation one aims for, and its ruler shows where professional effort tends to go: with the ruler of the 10th in the 3rd, through teaching, writing or trade.
- **Planets passing over the Midheaven** by [[transit|transit]] are often read as times of visibility or of a turn in one’s path.
- **An empty 10th house** means neither a lack of career nor of ambition; it is read through the Midheaven and its ruler.

## Across traditions

- **Jyotish** calls this house karma, action and work, and reads it for profession, status and public life; it is the strongest of the angular houses, the kendras, a house of artha and an upachaya, a house said to improve with effort.
- **Zi Wei Dou Shu** has a palace of Career among its twelve palaces; it is found by another method and read on its own terms.

## Origin and history

Hellenistic astrologers called this place the Midheaven, mesouranēma, the highest point a planet reaches in its daily course, and tied it to a person’s actions. Lilly read the 10th for kings and magistrates, for honour, office and preferment, for a person’s profession or trade, and for the mother, as he gave the father to the 4th. Modern astrology, following the sign of the same number, Capricorn, and its ruler [[saturn]], made it above all the house of vocation, achievement and public standing.

## How Ulune calculates it

In Placidus, Ulune’s default, and in the other quadrant systems, the cusp of the 10th house is the Midheaven itself. In Equal houses the cusp lies 270° after the Ascendant, which is generally not the Midheaven; in Whole sign houses the 10th is the tenth sign from the rising sign; in Morinus the cusp is not tied to the Midheaven. Ulune places the Midheaven by the cusps like any other point, so under Equal or Whole sign houses it can fall in the 9th, the 10th or the 11th, and the readings follow that house. Without a birth time the Midheaven and the house are only a stand-in for 12:00, and are marked ~.
