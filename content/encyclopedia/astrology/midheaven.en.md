## Meaning

The Midheaven, or MC from the Latin medium coeli, “middle of the sky”, is the degree of the zodiac crossing the meridian above the birthplace, shown at the top of the chart. It is not the point straight overhead: it is where the zodiac’s own circle meets the meridian, due south from the northern mid-latitudes and due north from the southern ones.

In most house systems it begins the [[house-10|10th house]]. Astrologers read it as vocation, reputation and public direction: what someone aims for in the wider world, wants to be known for, and how they stand with authority. Where the Ascendant is the way a life is entered, the Midheaven is where it is heading in public.

Opposite lies the [[imum-coeli]], the root of the chart; the axis between them joins private foundations to public aims.

## In practice

- **Its sign** colours the aim: a Midheaven in Capricorn often goes with a slow, deliberate climb towards a recognised position, one in Pisces with work in care, art or music.
- **A planet near the MC** often becomes what someone is known for.
- **The ruler of its sign**, by sign and house, shows where professional effort tends to go.
- **The progressed Midheaven** moves about a degree a year, and its aspects to natal planets are often read alongside changes in work or public role.

## Across traditions

- **Indian astrology** reads career and standing above all from the tenth sign counted from the lagna, the rising sign.
- **Human Design** does not use the Midheaven or the houses.

## Origin and history

Hellenistic astrologers called it the culmination, mesouranēma, the highest of the four pivots. With whole-sign houses, the tenth place, of action and reputation, was the sign where the culminating degree usually falls, but they knew it could also land in the ninth or eleventh sign. Later quadrant house systems made the Midheaven the cusp of the tenth house, and for William Lilly that house showed honour, profession and authority.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune takes the Midheaven from the Swiss Ephemeris’s house calculation: the point of the ecliptic on the upper meridian, which depends on the sidereal time and the tilt of the ecliptic but not on the latitude. It therefore changes sign every 1 hour 50 to 2 hours 10 wherever one is born.

It is the cusp of the 10th house in Placidus, Ulune’s default, and the other quadrant systems; with equal or whole-sign houses it floats free of the cusps and is often in the 9th or the 11th, so Ulune reads its house from the cusps rather than assuming the 10th. Aspects to it use 6° for the trine and 4° for the sextile, and with the Ascendant it can take part in aspect patterns such as the T-square. In progressions it advances at the Naibod rate, 0°59′08″ of right ascension a year; in the calendar, a transit to the IC is listed once, as the matching transit to the Midheaven. Without a birth time it is only a noon stand-in, marked approximate.
