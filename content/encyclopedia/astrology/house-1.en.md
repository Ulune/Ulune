## Meaning

The 1st house is the part of the sky that was rising in the east at birth. It begins at the [[ascendant|Ascendant]] in most house systems, and astrologers read it as the self: the body and its vitality, appearance and manner, the way a person meets the world and begins things. If a chart is a story, the 1st house is the point of view from which it is told.

It is an angular house, the first of the four, and planets here are read as strong and visible: they colour the temperament and are often what others notice first. Its opposite is the [[house-7|7th house]], and the axis they form is that of self and other, of standing on one’s own and meeting someone else.

Tradition called it the house of life. Whatever planet stands here is said to show in the person’s bearing and in the body’s way of moving through the world, and the ruler of the rising sign, the chart ruler, shows where that way of being is directed.

## In practice

- **Saturn in the 1st** often gives a serious, reserved first impression and a strength that grows with the years; **Jupiter in the 1st**, an open and generous presence; **Venus in the 1st**, grace and a care for appearance.
- **The sign on the cusp**, the rising sign, colours the whole manner, whatever the Sun sign.
- **The chart ruler** links the self to the house where it stands: with the ruler in the 10th, much of the person goes into work and public life.
- **A planet just above the horizon** stands in the [[house-12|12th house]] by the cusps, yet many astrologers read a planet within a few degrees of the Ascendant on either side as rising, and especially prominent.
- **An empty 1st house** is common and says nothing about a faint personality: the rising sign and its ruler carry the story.

## Across traditions

- **Jyotish** calls this house tanu, the body, or lagna, the rising sign itself, from which every other house is counted; it is both an angular house, a kendra, and a house of dharma, of purpose.
- **Zi Wei Dou Shu** sets at the head of its twelve palaces the palace of Life, found from the lunar month and the hour of birth; it is read on its own terms, beside the 1st house rather than as the same thing.

## Origin and history

Hellenistic astrologers called this place the Hour-marker, hōroskopos, the degree that marks the hour of birth by rising; the word became “horoscope”, first the name of the rising degree, later of the whole chart. It was the place where [[mercury]] was said to rejoice, its joy. Lilly read the 1st for the life of the person, their stature, complexion and form, and in a horary question for the one who asks it; modern astrologers read it more widely as identity, presence and the body as it is lived.

## How Ulune calculates it

In Placidus, Ulune’s default, and in the other quadrant systems, Koch, Porphyry, Regiomontanus, Campanus, Alcabitius and Topocentric, the cusp of the 1st house is the Ascendant itself. In Equal houses it is the Ascendant’s degree, and the house runs 30° from there; in Whole sign houses it is the whole rising sign, from its 0°; in Morinus it is not tied to the Ascendant. A body is in the 1st from this cusp up to the cusp of the 2nd, counted in the order of the zodiac; where the cusp is the Ascendant, a body that has just risen above the horizon is therefore in the 12th. The table notes any body within 8° of an angle, whatever its house. Without a birth time the Ascendant, and with it the whole house, is only a stand-in for 12:00, and is marked ~.
