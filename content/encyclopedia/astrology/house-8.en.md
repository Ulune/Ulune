## Meaning

The 8th house is what is held with others: joint money and debts, loans and taxes, inheritance and gifts, what a partner brings. Astrologers also read it as the deeper ties that come with sharing, intimacy and trust, and as the times when something has to end so that something else can begin: crises, turning points, transformation.

It is a succedent house, following the angle of the 7th, and it is read as what partnership builds and holds in common. Its opposite is the [[house-2|2nd house]], and the axis they form is that of what is one’s own and what is shared, of keeping and letting go.

Tradition called it the house of death. Modern astrologers read it more widely and without dread, as endings and renewals of every kind, and nothing in a chart, a planet in the 8th included, foretells anyone’s death. A busy 8th house often goes with a taste for depth, research and the psychology of what lies beneath.

## In practice

- **Venus in the 8th** is often read as deep, private bonds and resources shared generously; **Saturn in the 8th**, as care with debts and joint commitments, and trust built slowly; **Mercury in the 8th**, as a mind drawn to research, investigation and hidden causes.
- **The sign on the cusp** describes how trust, debts and crises are met, and its ruler shows where these questions arise.
- **Planets passing through the 8th** by [[transit|transit]] are often read as times of reckoning with shared resources, or of inner change.
- **An empty 8th house** still has intimacy and joint finances in it; it is read through its cusp and its ruler.

## Across traditions

- **Jyotish** calls this house randhra, the opening, and also ayu, the length of life, and reads it for inheritance, research and what is hidden; it is one of the dusthanas, the difficult houses, and a house of moksha, release.
- **Zi Wei Dou Shu** has no single palace for these themes; its twelve palaces divide life in another way.

## Origin and history

Hellenistic astrologers called this place Idle, argos, because it makes no classical aspect to the rising sign. Lilly read the 8th for death, for wills, legacies and testaments, and for a spouse’s dowry, the goods that come through a partner. In the 20th century, Dane Rudhyar and the psychological astrologers after him read it as the house of shared experience and deep change, in the light of Scorpio, the sign of the same number, and of Pluto, its modern ruler.

## How Ulune calculates it

The cusp of the 8th comes from Swiss Ephemeris in the chosen system and always lies exactly opposite the cusp of the 2nd: in Placidus, Ulune’s default, it divides by time the passage of degrees between the western horizon and the top of the sky; in Equal houses it lies 210° after the Ascendant, and in Whole sign houses the 8th is the eighth sign from the rising sign. A body counts in the 8th from this cusp to the cusp of the 9th, and the house’s reading names the planets inside it and the ruler of its sign.
