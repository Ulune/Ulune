## Meaning

Virgo is the sixth sign of the zodiac, from 150° to 180°, the end of northern summer and the time of harvest. It is [[element-earth|earth]] in its [[modality-mutable|mutable]] form, ruled by [[mercury]], and astrologers read it as the sign of discernment: sorting the useful from the useless, learning a craft, putting things in good order and caring through what one does.

A planet in Virgo tends to notice detail, to analyse before it acts and to improve things step by step. As a mutable sign it adapts its methods readily when a better one appears. Its gifts are precision, competence, modesty and a quiet generosity that shows itself in help rather than in words.

Its difficulties grow from the same exactness: a standard that keeps rising, worry about what could go wrong, criticism turned inward or outward, trouble letting a thing be finished. The opposite sign, [[pisces]], brings what analysis cannot hold, compassion, imagination and surrender; the pair is read as the axis of order and of letting go.

Virgo holds a rare double dignity in tradition: Mercury both rules it and is exalted in it, at 15°. [[venus]] is in its [[fall|fall]] here, the heart asked to pass through judgement, and [[jupiter]] is in its [[detriment|detriment]]; some modern astrologers add Neptune, the modern ruler of Pisces.

## In practice

- **The [[sun]] in Virgo**, from 22 or 23 August to 22 or 23 September (the day shifts from year to year): a sense of self built through skill, work well done and being of use.
- **The [[moon]] in Virgo**: feelings that settle through order and practical help; worry eases when there is something concrete to do.
- **Virgo rising**, on the [[ascendant|Ascendant]]: a composed, attentive first impression. Mercury rules the chart, so its sign, house and aspects show where that attention goes.
- **Mercury in Virgo**, in its [[domicile|domicile]] and exalted, is exact and methodical. **Venus in Virgo**, in fall, shows love through care and service and may have to learn to accept being loved as it is.
- **Virgo on a house cusp**: that area of life is handled carefully and methodically, with an eye for what needs fixing.

## Across traditions

- **Jyotish** calls the sign Kanya, the maiden, gives it to Mercury and exalts Mercury there too; measured from the stars, it begins about 24° further on, and the Sun enters it around 17 September.
- **Human Design** places five gates wholly in Virgo, 59, 40, 64, 47 and 6, with the edges of gates 29 and 46.
- **The Chinese calendar** opens Virgo with Chǔshǔ, “end of heat”, and sets Báilù, “white dew”, at 15° Virgo, where the month of the Rooster (yǒu) begins.

## Origin and history

Babylonian astronomers saw here the Furrow, with an ear of barley. The Greeks made the figure a maiden, Parthenos, and Aratus identified her with Justice, Dikē, who lived among people in the golden age and withdrew to the sky as they grew violent. She still holds the ear of grain: Spica, the constellation’s brightest star, whose Latin name means just that.

Spica is one of the six [[fixed-star|fixed stars]] in Ulune’s table; precession has carried it to about 24° of the sign Libra. Virgo is the largest constellation on the ecliptic: by the boundaries astronomers published in 1930, the Sun crosses it from about 17 September to 31 October, some 44 days. In the old doctrine of planetary houses, Virgo is Mercury’s night house and Gemini its day house.

## How Ulune calculates it

A body is in Virgo from 150° to 180° of tropical longitude, measured from the March equinox point. In the dignity table Mercury holds both its domicile and its exaltation here, so a Mercury in Virgo scores both; Venus is in fall and Jupiter in detriment. The [[triplicity|triplicity]] rulers are Venus by day, the Moon by night and [[mars]] participating, after Dorotheus; the [[term|terms]] run Mercury to 7°, Venus to 17°, Jupiter to 21°, Mars to 28° and [[saturn]] to 30°, and the [[face|faces]] are the Sun, Venus and Mercury. The readings’ [[decan|decans]] are those of Virgo, Capricorn and Taurus, ruled by Mercury, Saturn and Venus.
