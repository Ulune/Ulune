## Meaning

Environment is one of the four arrows of [[hd-variable|Variable]], the finest layer Human Design reads in a chart. It comes from the North Node of the Design, the sky about three months before birth, when the Sun stood 88° further back. In the tradition’s drawing it is the arrow at the bottom left.

The Nodes are the two points where the Moon’s path crosses the Sun’s. Human Design reads them as the setting and direction of a life, the South Node for its first half, the North Node for its second. In the Design, the layer read as the body, the Node’s finest shade becomes Environment: the kind of place where the body does well.

The arrow carries two things. Its colour, one of six, names a kind of setting with a single word, such as Markets or Mountains. Its tone, one of six within that colour, gives its direction.

- Pointing left, tones 1 to 3: Human Design reads a focused, active relation to place. It is at its best in a clear, specific, chosen setting.
- Pointing right, tones 4 to 6: a receptive, open relation, at its best taking in a place as it comes.

Neither side is better. The tradition reads both as styles of the same function. Feeling out of place is treated, as so often in Human Design, as information about what fits, never as a fault.

## In practice

Human Design offers Environment as something to notice over time. Teachers suggest working with it only after a long time of living by [[hd-type|type]], [[hd-strategy|strategy]] and [[hd-authority|authority]]. It points to a quality of place, not to a particular town, house or job.

- Someone might find they think and rest better in one kind of setting, and recognise in the arrow’s word a name for it.
- With a left arrow, the tradition would suggest choosing that setting deliberately and returning to it.
- With a right arrow, it would suggest staying open, letting the right places show themselves.
- Two people sharing a home may each have a different arrow, and the tradition reads both needs as equally valid.

Environment is read together with its partner on the same side, [[hd-arrow-determination|Determination]]. The two Design arrows describe the body: how it is fed and where it is placed. On the other side, [[hd-arrow-motivation|Motivation]] and [[hd-arrow-perspective|Perspective]] describe the mind.

## Across traditions

Astrology reads the [[north-node|North Node]] as a point of direction and growth, and the [[south-node|South Node]] as what is already familiar. Human Design uses the same two points but reads them through gates, lines and, here, the arrow. The two traditions share the astronomy, not the meanings.

## Origin and history

Ra Uru Hu founded Human Design in 1987. He divided each line of the wheel into colours, tones and bases, levels the I Ching does not have. Environment belongs to the later, advanced part of his teaching. His school teaches it, with Determination, in its course called Primary Health System.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune finds the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude before its place at birth. It takes the true North Node at that moment, as Jovian Archive’s charts do, not the mean node. It subtracts 302°, where [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] begins, and counts the result in bases of 18.75″. From that one count come the [[hd-gate|gate]], the [[hd-line|line]], the colour, the tone and the base.

The colour gives the word shown with the arrow. The tone sets the side: 1 to 3 left, 4 to 6 right. The Design South Node, exactly opposite, shares the colour and tone. Ulune computes the base but does not read it.

The arrow sits on the North Node row of the Design column, on the left of the bodygraph. Choosing that row opens its reading. In the Keys table, Environment is the second letter after D: in PLR DLL it points left.

The Node moves slowly, on average about 3′ of arc a day, backwards through the zodiac. A tone is about 1′34″, so the Node takes around half a day to cross one. Ulune still casts the chart half an hour before and after the birth time. If the colour or side changes there, the arrow is marked as unsteady. That seldom happens for this arrow. Without a birth time, Ulune shows no arrows.
