## Meaning

The channel of Community makes bargains and belonging a consistent part of a person. Work and support are given, and loyalty and care come back. Human Design reads it as the glue of families, teams and small communities: an agreement, often unspoken, that each side keeps its part.

[[hd-gate-40|Gate 40, Aloneness]], sits in the [[hd-centre-heart|Heart]], the motor of willpower. It brings the will to work for others, followed by a real need to be alone and rest. [[hd-gate-37|Gate 37, Friendship]], sits in the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], the centre of emotions, which moves in a wave. It brings warmth and friendship built on fair bargains.

The tradition places the channel in the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuitry]]. That circuitry is about support, touch and providing for one’s own. Community sits at its heart: the deal that holds a group together.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: both gates are coloured at birth. The person knows their sense of fairness and belonging.
- **Design**, in red: both gates are coloured about three months before birth. Others may feel held by this person before they see it themselves.
- **One gate from each layer**: the channel is just as defined; its halves come from two moments.
- **Gate 40 alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a will to provide, then withdraw, often drawn to people who carry gate 37.
- **Gate 37 alone**: warmth and a sense of family, which someone with gate 40 can back with steady work.

Day to day, someone with this channel defined often holds together a family, a club or a small business. The arrangement works when both sides keep their part. Rest is part of the deal too: Human Design reads the Heart’s need to withdraw after giving as healthy, not as a lack of love.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 40, Deliverance in Wilhelm’s version, places Thunder over Water, the storm that clears the air. Hexagram 37, The Family, places Wind over Fire, warmth rising from the hearth. Change every line of one and the other appears: release and the home, two sides of one bargain.
- **Astrology**: gate 40 spans 5°45′00″ to 11°22′30″ [[virgo|Virgo]], and gate 37 the same degrees of [[pisces|Pisces]]. The two gates face each other exactly across the wheel.

## How Ulune calculates it

The channel is defined when gates 40 and 37 each hold at least one of the thirteen activations of the [[hd-layers|Personality or the Design]]. The Heart and the Solar Plexus then become defined. Because Ulune places the Earth exactly opposite the [[sun|Sun]], a Sun in either gate puts the Earth in the other. That single Sun defines the channel, in its own layer. The [[north-node|North Node]] and [[south-node|South Node]], also opposite, do the same.

As a chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-projector|Projector]]: two motors are defined, but neither reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], and the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] stays undefined. The strategy is to wait for the invitation. With the Solar Plexus defined, the authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]], and the [[hd-definition|definition]] single. If another channel joins either centre to the Throat, the type becomes [[hd-manifestor|Manifestor]], or [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]] with a defined Sacral.

On the bodygraph, each half takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, red dashes over ink for both. A defined channel is drawn thicker and its centres are filled; a hanging gate colours only its half. Without a birth time, a channel another hour could change is drawn faded.
