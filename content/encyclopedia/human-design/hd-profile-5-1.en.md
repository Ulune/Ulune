## Meaning

A [[hd-profile|profile]] pairs two [[hd-line|lines]]. The first is that of the [[hd-layers|Personality]] Sun, at birth, which Human Design reads as conscious. The second is that of the Design Sun, about three months earlier, read as unconscious and often clearer to others. In the 5/1 the first is the [[hd-line-5|Heretic]], the second the [[hd-line-1|Investigator]]. Each is the line of the gate where that Sun stands, a hexagram of the I Ching counted from the bottom: lines 1 to 3 form its lower trigram, 4 to 6 its upper one.

Human Design teaches that the fifth line, at the centre of the upper trigram, draws projection. Others, strangers above all, see in it the one who can solve their problem. Its reputation rises when it brings a practical answer and can turn when it cannot. The first line, the base of the hexagram, wants a firm foundation and seeks it through study.

In the 5/1 the projection is conscious: the person feels what others expect and often knows what is being asked of them. The unconscious first line is what lets them meet it, a depth of knowledge they may not notice they have built. The tradition reads that foundation as protection. When a solution rests on solid ground the projection holds; when it is improvised, it can turn quickly.

The 5/1 opens the [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle]], which the tradition reads as a transpersonal destiny, a life that unfolds largely through meeting others. Its [[hd-cross|Incarnation Cross]] carries that angle.

## In practice

- A mediator is brought in by two strangers who have heard she can settle a dispute.
- A teacher who gives one good talk is asked to rework the whole programme.
- Checking the facts before promising a solution is, in this reading, what keeps a reputation steady.
- When expectations run high, saying clearly what one can and cannot do tends to soften the projection.

## Across traditions

In Richard Wilhelm’s commentary on the I Ching, the fifth line is the ruler’s place and the first the beginning, still low. Lines in the same place of each trigram correspond, and Human Design calls them harmonic. The 5 and the 1 are not a pair; their partners make the [[hd-profile-2-4|2/4]].

## How Ulune calculates it

The first number is the line of the Personality Sun, at birth; the second, the line of the Design Sun, when the Sun stood exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. From [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302° of tropical longitude, each [[hd-gate|gate]] spans 5.625° and each line 0.9375°. The Earth always shares the Sun’s line.

88° is about 93.87 lines. So the Design line usually falls two places after the Personality line, counting on from 6 to 1. It falls three places after only in the last 0.125° of a line. The 5/1 is one of the six common profiles, each about 14.4% of the zodiac, against 2.2% for each rare one. As the Sun moves, the profile steps through the twelve in a fixed order: [[hd-profile-4-1|4/1]], then the 5/1 for about 20 hours, then [[hd-profile-5-2|5/2]] for about three.

Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at noon and at every hour of that day. The Sun moves about a degree a day, more than a line, so the profile is always marked ~: another hour gives a neighbour, usually the 4/1 or the 5/2. The [[hd-cross|cross]] is marked too when its angle or gates could change.
