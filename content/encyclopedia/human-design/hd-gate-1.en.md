## Meaning

Gate 1 is the gate of Self-Expression. Its theme is a direction or a style that is genuinely one’s own, offered as it is rather than shaped to please. Human Design reads it as creative energy that wants a personal signature: the person who rewrites the template instead of filling it in.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], the centre of identity, direction and love in the bodygraph. Gate 1 gives that sense of self a creative outlet. The tradition places it in the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]], the heart of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuit group]]. Human Design teaches that this group brings mutation: new ways of doing things that arrive in their own time and empower others once they are accepted.

The theme carries a gentle signal too. Work that leaves no room for a personal touch can feel flat to someone with this gate. The tradition reads that flatness as information about what fits, not as a failing.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: a planet stood in gate 1 at birth. Human Design reads it as the side of the theme a person knows in themselves.
- **Design**, in red: a planet stood here about three months before birth, when the Sun was 88° earlier. It is the side others often see more clearly.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: gate 1 is coloured but its partner, [[hd-gate-8]], is not. The theme is steady and reaches towards the other half of its channel. Someone who carries gate 8 can complete it while they spend time together.
- **Completed**: with gate 8 coloured too, the [[hd-channel-8-1|channel of Inspiration]] is defined, joining the G centre to the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The personal style then has a consistent voice, and others tend to notice it and take it as an example.

When the Sun stands in gate 1, at birth or in the Design, the gate becomes a core theme of the chart, since Human Design gives the Sun the most weight. The Earth then stands in [[hd-gate-2]], exactly opposite.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 1, Qián 乾, which Wilhelm called The Creative. It is the only hexagram of six unbroken lines, the trigram of Heaven doubled. Its judgment, in Legge’s words, speaks of “what is great and originating” (Legge, 1882).
- **Astrology**: the gate covers the middle of [[scorpio|Scorpio]], a sign astrologers read as depth and intensity. The Sun crosses it each year roughly from 5 to 11 November.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] around the tropical zodiac from gate 41 at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius, each 5.625° or 5°37′30″ wide, in the wheel’s order. Gate 1 is the 51st, between gates 44 and 43, so it begins 50 × 5.625° = 281.25° after gate 41. It runs from 13°15′00″ to 18°52′30″ Scorpio, that is from 223.25° to 228.875°.

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 13°15′00″, line 2 at 14°11′15″, line 3 at 15°07′30″, line 4 at 16°03′45″, line 5 at 17°00′00″ and line 6 at 17°56′15″ Scorpio.

Any of the thirteen bodies Ulune places at birth and at the Design moment that falls in this span colours gate 1, with its line. Without a birth time, a gate or line that could differ at another hour of the day is marked.
