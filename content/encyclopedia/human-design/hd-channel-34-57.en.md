## Meaning

The channel of Power links the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] at [[hd-gate-34|gate 34]] to the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]] at [[hd-gate-57|gate 57]]. Ulune reads it as strong Sacral energy combined with intuition in the moment. The result is quick, instinctive responses to whatever happens.

Gate 34, Power, is raw and independent working energy that wants to be busy. Gate 57, Intuitive Clarity, is the Spleen’s quiet, instant hearing of what is safe or off. Joined, they let the body know what to do and give it the strength to do it, often before the mind has caught up.

The Sacral is the motor of life force and of work energy. The Spleen is the centre of instinct and of the body’s sense of safety; Human Design associates it with the immune system. Here a motor and an instinct work as one.

The tradition places this channel in the [[hd-circuit-integration|Integration circuit]], whose four channels join gates 10, 20, 34 and 57. Integration stands apart from the three large circuitry groups and is read as the circuit of self-empowerment and survival. Each of its gates belongs to three channels. Gate 34 also makes [[hd-channel-34-20|34–20]] and [[hd-channel-10-34|10–34]]; gate 57 also makes [[hd-channel-20-57|20–57]] and [[hd-channel-10-57|10–57]].

## In practice

- **Personality, Design or both.** Defined by the Personality (the planets at birth, drawn in ink), the quick reactions tend to feel like one’s own. Defined by the Design (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red), they are more bodily, and others may notice them first. With one gate in each [[hd-layers|layer]], the channel is just as defined.
- **One gate alone.** Gate 34 alone is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: power that comes in bursts, without a steady instinct to aim it. Gate 57 alone gives intuition that does not always have the energy behind it.
- **Day to day.** Someone with this channel often reacts fast and well in sport, emergencies or negotiations, sensing what to do before thinking it through.
- **Trusting the body.** Ulune’s reading notes that the body’s timing is usually more reliable than afterthoughts. Second-guessing a sound first reaction is read as a signal, not a fault.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Gate 34 is hexagram 34, which Wilhelm calls The Power of the Great: Thunder over Heaven, strong lines rising. Gate 57 is hexagram 57, The Gentle, the Penetrating: Wind doubled, a force that gets in everywhere by steady pressure. Great power and gentle penetration are the two sides of the channel.
- **Astrology.** Gate 34 spans 0°07′30″ to 5°45′00″ [[sagittarius|Sagittarius]]. Gate 57 spans 15°07′30″ to 20°45′00″ [[libra|Libra]].

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune casts 26 activations: 13 bodies at birth for the Personality, 13 for the Design, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 34 and gate 57 each hold at least one, from either layer. The Sacral and the Spleen then become defined.

Run as the chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-generator|Generator]]: the Sacral is defined and no motor reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The authority is [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral]], since the Solar Plexus is not defined, and the definition single. Add gate 20, and 34–20 and 20–57 join the Sacral to the Throat: the chart becomes a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]].

On the bodygraph, each half of the channel takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, ink dashed with red for both. Defined, the line is drawn thicker than a hanging half, and the two centres are filled in gold. In the Personality or Design view, only that layer’s bodies count.
