## Meaning

In Human Design, the authority, or inner authority, is the part of a person’s design that the tradition says to trust when a decision is due. It is the third of the bodygraph’s keys, after the [[hd-type|type]] and the [[hd-strategy|strategy]]. The strategy says how to meet life: to inform, to respond, to wait for the invitation or to wait a lunar cycle. The authority says where, in oneself, the yes or no comes from.

Most authorities have a seat, one of the nine [[hd-centres|centres]] of the bodygraph, the system’s chart of energy centres. A centre counts as defined when a complete [[hd-channel|channel]] reaches it. Human Design ranks the centres that can carry an authority, and the highest defined one decides. The rest of the chart still matters, but it does not have the final word.

The tradition distinguishes seven ways of deciding:

- [[hd-authority-emotional|Emotional authority]], seated in the Solar Plexus: clarity over time, once the emotional wave has settled. About half of all people have it.
- [[hd-authority-sacral|Sacral authority]], seated in the Sacral: an immediate gut response to a clear question, for Generators.
- [[hd-authority-splenic|Splenic authority]], seated in the Spleen: a quiet instinct that speaks once, in the moment.
- [[hd-authority-ego|Ego authority]], seated in the Heart: deciding from what one truly wants and will commit to.
- [[hd-authority-self-projected|Self-Projected authority]], seated in the G centre: hearing one’s own truth while talking a choice through.
- [[hd-authority-mental|Mental authority]], with no seat: Projectors without inner authority, who find clarity through places and trusted listeners.
- [[hd-authority-lunar|Lunar authority]], with no seat: Reflectors, who give a big decision a full cycle of the Moon.

Whatever the authority, Human Design holds that the mind is not built to make life decisions on its own. The [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]] and the [[hd-centre-head|Head]] never carry an authority. The mind stays valuable as an outer guide: it thinks, compares and explains, and its insight is often most useful to others.

## In practice

- **Finding it.** On Ulune, the authority appears among the keys above the bodygraph, next to the type and the strategy. Choosing it lights its seat on the chart, when it has one.
- **Small choices first.** The tradition suggests trying the authority on small daily decisions before trusting it with large ones.
- **Strategy and authority together.** A Generator waits for something to respond to, then listens to the Sacral or lets the emotional wave settle. A Projector waits for the invitation, then weighs it with the authority.
- **Signals, not faults.** When a decision is made against the authority, the [[hd-not-self|not-self]] theme of each type, such as frustration or bitterness, is read as a gentle signal to return to it.

## Origin and history

The idea of inner authority comes from the teaching of Ra Uru Hu, who founded Human Design after an experience on Ibiza in January 1987. His order of authorities runs from the Solar Plexus down to the lunar cycle of Reflectors. Teachers name the last cases in different ways: environmental authority, sounding boards, or no inner authority at all.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the authority from the defined centres, testing these rules in order and stopping at the first that holds:

1. The [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]] is defined: Emotional.
2. The [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] is defined: Sacral.
3. The [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]] is defined: Splenic.
4. The [[hd-centre-heart|Heart]] is defined: Ego.
5. The [[hd-centre-g|G centre]] and the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]] are both defined: Self-Projected.
6. The type is [[hd-reflector|Reflector]]: Lunar.
7. Otherwise: Mental.

A centre is defined when one of its channels has both gates active, from any of the thirteen bodies of the Personality or of the Design. A chart with no defined centre is a Reflector, so rule 6 applies exactly when no centre is defined; rule 7 then covers [[hd-projector|Projectors]] defined only in the Head, the Ajna and the Throat.

When the authority is selected, Ulune lights the centre it comes from: the Solar Plexus, the Sacral, the Spleen, the Heart or the G. Mental and Lunar authority light none. Ulune names the Ego’s two forms, manifested for a Manifestor and projected for a Projector, and adds “no inner authority” beneath Mental; it does not split the other authorities into finer kinds. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon and again at every hour of that day; if the authority differs at any hour, Ulune marks it as one that could differ.
