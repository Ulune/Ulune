## Meaning

Gate 52, Stillness, is one of the nine gates of the [[hd-centre-root|Root centre]], the centre of the pressure that gets things moving. Its theme is stillness: the pressure to stay put in order to concentrate.

Human Design reads it as the calm of a mountain. Given something clear to focus on, it can hold a person absorbed in one task for hours. Its stillness is not idleness: it gathers the energy that attention needs.

When nothing clear holds that attention, the same pressure can turn into restlessness, busy without moving. The tradition reads this kindly, as a signal: the pressure is asking for a worthy object, not for more activity.

The tradition places gate 52 in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], also called the Understanding circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit]] group. The Logic circuit tests patterns and improves them over time. Gate 52 gives it the steadiness to stay with a problem long enough.

## In practice

A gate is coloured when a planet stands in it at birth or in the Design, as [[hd-gate|Gate]] and [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]] explain.

- **By a Personality planet**, at birth: the person tends to know their need for quiet focus and can arrange their days around it.
- **By a Design planet**, about three months earlier: the stillness works from the body. Others may notice how settled the person becomes once absorbed.
- **By both**: the theme is doubled, known and lived.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: without gate 9, the pressure to focus looks for energy to work with. People with [[hd-gate-9|gate 9]], Focus, bring the energy to concentrate on details.
- **Completed into a channel**: with gate 9, the [[hd-channel-9-52|channel of Concentration]] is defined, joining the Root to the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]]. It gives steady energy to focus on one thing and stay with it, which serves research, crafts and detailed work.

When this is a chart’s only channel, Ulune finds a [[hd-generator|Generator]] with [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 52, *Kên*, doubles the trigram of the Mountain, also named *Kên*, Keeping Still. Wilhelm titles it Keeping Still, Mountain: rest that comes from knowing when to stop. Legge’s judgement opens with an image of quiet, “when one’s resting is like that of the back” (Legge, 1882). Turned upside down, it becomes hexagram 51, the image of [[hd-gate-51|gate 51]].
- **Astrology**: the gate spans 3°52′30″ to 9°30′00″ [[cancer|Cancer]], the sign of shelter and of the home one withdraws into.

## How Ulune calculates it

- Gate 41 opens the wheel at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of tropical longitude. Each gate then spans 5°37′30″ (5.625°) in a fixed order.
- Gate 52 is the 28th, from 3°52′30″ to 9°30′00″ Cancer (93.875° to 99.5°). Its six lines are 0°56′15″ wide and begin at 3°52′30″, 4°48′45″, 5°45′00″, 6°41′15″, 7°37′30″ and 8°33′45″ Cancer.
- Ulune reads thirteen bodies at birth and again when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The Sun, the Earth, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto each colour the gate they stand in.
- When gate 9 is coloured too, at either moment, the channel 9–52 is defined. Ulune notes whether its gates come from the Personality, the Design or both.
- With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by the profile. Profiles 1/3 to 4/6 give the Right Angle Cross of Service 2. Profile 4/1 gives the Juxtaposition Cross of Stillness. Profiles 5/1 to 6/3 give the Left Angle Cross of Demands 1.
