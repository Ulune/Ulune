## Meaning

A [[hd-profile|profile]] pairs two [[hd-line|lines]]. The first is that of the [[hd-layers|Personality]] Sun, at birth, which Human Design reads as conscious. The second is that of the Design Sun, about three months earlier, read as unconscious and often clearer to others. In the 3/5 the first is the [[hd-line-3|Martyr]], the second the [[hd-line-5|Heretic]]. Each is the line of the gate where that Sun stands, a hexagram of the I Ching counted from the bottom: lines 1 to 3 form its lower trigram, 4 to 6 its upper one.

Human Design teaches that the third line learns by trial and error: it finds what works by running into what does not. The name sounds heavy, yet the tradition reads it as resilience and discovery. The fifth line draws projection: others see in it the one who can fix their problem, and its reputation rises or falls with the practical answers it brings.

In the 3/5 experience feeds reputation. The person consciously learns by trying, falling short and adjusting; others then see someone who can sort things out, precisely because they have clearly been through it. The tradition reads the bumps as the source of credibility. It also notes the pressure: an expectation that is not met can turn into blame. Speaking plainly about what was tried, and what did not work, keeps the picture honest.

The 3/5 belongs to the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], which the tradition reads as a personal destiny, lived mostly through one’s own process. Its [[hd-cross|Incarnation Cross]] carries that angle.

## In practice

- Someone who has moved country twice becomes the friend everyone asks before a move.
- A mechanic whose early repairs sometimes went wrong now knows exactly why an engine fails.
- A change of plan is, in this reading, research rather than inconstancy.
- When people expect a miracle, naming the limits of a solution early tends to soften the projection.

## Across traditions

Richard Wilhelm’s commentary on the I Ching sees the third line as a precarious place of transition and the fifth as the ruler’s place. Lines in the same place of each trigram correspond, and Human Design calls them harmonic. The 3 and the 5 are not a pair; their partners make the [[hd-profile-6-2|6/2]].

## How Ulune calculates it

The first number is the line of the Personality Sun, at birth; the second, the line of the Design Sun, when the Sun stood exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. From [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302° of tropical longitude, each [[hd-gate|gate]] spans 5.625° and each line 0.9375°. The Earth always shares the Sun’s line.

88° is about 93.87 lines. So the Design line usually falls two places after the Personality line, counting on from 6 to 1. It falls three places after only in the last 0.125° of a line. The 3/5 is one of the six common profiles, each about 14.4% of the zodiac, against 2.2% for each rare one. As the Sun moves, the profile steps through the twelve in a fixed order: [[hd-profile-2-5|2/5]], then the 3/5 for about 20 hours, then [[hd-profile-3-6|3/6]] for about three.

Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at noon and at every hour of that day. The Sun moves about a degree a day, more than a line, so the profile is always marked ~: another hour gives a neighbour, usually the 2/5 or the 3/6. Ulune names the profiles that day can give under the profile, as in “that day also 3/6”. The [[hd-cross|cross]] is marked too when its angle or gates could change.
