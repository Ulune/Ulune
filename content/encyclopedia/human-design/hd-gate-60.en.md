## Meaning

Gate 60, Acceptance, sits in the [[hd-centre-root|Root]], the pressure centre and motor at the base of the bodygraph. Its theme is accepting limits as the ground from which something new can emerge. A frame or a rule is not a wall to break here, but the shape that lets change take form.

The tradition sees two sides in this gate. One is patience with constraint, a capacity to work well inside tight conditions without fighting each of them. The other is a pressure for something new, which builds in the background and comes through in its own time. Between the two there can be flat or melancholic stretches. Human Design reads them as waiting, not failure: the moment for change arrives, and it cannot be forced.

The tradition places gate 60 in the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]], part of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual]] group. There, energy moves in pulses, on and off, and brings something new that others may only understand later.

## In practice

- **Activated by a Personality planet**, at birth, the person usually knows their way with limits and can choose to work inside them. **By a Design planet**, from about three months earlier, the pattern is lived more than seen: others notice the long waits and sudden changes first. **By both**, it is a strong, familiar rhythm.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**, gate 60 does not define the Root. It looks for its partner, [[hd-gate-3|gate 3, Ordering]], in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]]. People who carry gate 3 tend to bring the energy that turns waiting pressure into something new.
- **Completed into the [[hd-channel-3-60|channel of Mutation]]**, it defines the Root and the Sacral, which makes the chart a [[hd-generator|Generator]] or a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]]. Ulune describes a steady ability to bring something new into form, in pulses rather than steadily. Accepting the quiet phases as part of the process helps.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 60, *Jie*, puts Kan, water, above Dui, the lake. Wilhelm called it Limitation, and its judgment warns, in Baynes’s English, “Galling limitation must not be persevered in” (Wilhelm, 1924). A lake holds only so much water, and a measure that helps can turn harmful when pushed too far.
- **Astrology.** The gate straddles two signs. Its first three lines close [[capricorn|Capricorn]], and line 4 crosses into [[aquarius|Aquarius]]. From the earth sign of structure to the air sign of innovation, the passage fits a gate where form gives way to the new.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune places gate 60 from 26°22′30″ Capricorn to 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 296.375° to 302° of tropical longitude. The wheel starts with [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius. Each gate takes 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the fixed order of the Human Design wheel, and gate 60 is the sixty-fourth. It is the last of the 64 and ends exactly where gate 41 begins. Its six [[hd-line|lines]] take 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 ends at 27°18′45″ Capricorn, line 2 at 28°15′00″ and line 3 at 29°11′15″. Line 4 ends at 0°07′30″ Aquarius and line 5 at 1°03′45″.

Ulune reads thirteen bodies: the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes, and Mercury to Pluto. It reads them at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. With the Sun here, the Earth is always in [[hd-gate-56|gate 56]]. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon, and positions that could change with the hour are flagged.
