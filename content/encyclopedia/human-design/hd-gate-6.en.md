## Meaning

Gate 6 is the gate of Friction. It regulates intimacy: the emotional mood decides whether someone opens up or keeps others at a distance. Human Design reads it as a gatekeeper of closeness, a clear yes or no felt about letting someone in, in love, in friendship or across a negotiating table.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], the centre of emotions in the bodygraph, which Human Design describes as moving in a wave, from high to low and back again. The tradition places gate 6 in the [[hd-circuit-defense|Defense circuit]], part of the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuit group]]. Human Design teaches that this circuit is about bonding, care and the continuation of the family, and gate 6 decides who comes close enough to share in it.

The friction this gate creates is often productive. The tradition suggests letting the mood settle before deciding, since clarity arrives over time rather than in the moment. A sudden coolness towards someone is read as part of the wave, not as a verdict.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: a planet stood in gate 6 at birth. Human Design reads it as the side of the theme a person knows in themselves.
- **Design**, in red: a planet stood here about three months before birth, when the Sun was 88° earlier. It is the side others often see more clearly.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: gate 6 is coloured but its partner, [[hd-gate-59]], is not. The emotional filter is there, and the drive to break barriers and get close comes through others who carry gate 59.
- **Completed**: with gate 59 coloured too, the [[hd-channel-59-6|channel of Mating]], also called Intimacy, is defined, joining the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] to the Solar Plexus. Ulune’s texts describe a steady drive to create closeness, filtered by mood: bonds form easily, and whether to go deeper is best decided once the wave has settled.

Once this channel is defined, the chart belongs to a [[hd-generator|Generator]] or a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]], and a defined Solar Plexus gives it [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 6, Sòng 訟, which Wilhelm called Conflict. Heaven stands over Water: heaven rises while water runs down, two movements that part ways. The text counsels caution over pressing a dispute; in Legge’s words, “if he cherish an apprehensive caution, there will be good fortune” (Legge, 1882).
- **Astrology**: the gate covers the last degrees of [[virgo|Virgo]], a sign astrologers read as discernment and careful sorting. The Sun crosses it each year roughly from 15 to 21 September.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] around the tropical zodiac from gate 41 at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius, each 5.625° or 5°37′30″ wide, in the wheel’s order. Gate 6 is the 42nd, between gates 47 and 46, so it begins 41 × 5.625° = 230.625° after gate 41. It runs from 22°37′30″ to 28°15′00″ Virgo, that is from 172.625° to 178.25°.

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 22°37′30″, line 2 at 23°33′45″, line 3 at 24°30′00″, line 4 at 25°26′15″, line 5 at 26°22′30″ and line 6 at 27°18′45″ Virgo.

Any of the thirteen bodies Ulune places at birth and at the Design moment that falls in this span colours gate 6, with its line. Without a birth time, a gate or line that could differ at another hour of the day is marked.
