## Meaning

Channel 31–7 is one of the 36 [[hd-channel|channels]] of the bodygraph, and Ulune calls it the channel of the Alpha. It runs from [[hd-gate-7|gate 7]], the Role of the Self, in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]] to [[hd-gate-31|gate 31]], Influence, in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. It is one of four channels between those two centres.

Its theme is leadership with a voice. Gate 7 senses where a group should go and what role guides it there. Gate 31 says it in a way people listen to. The direction is towards the future: what the group could become if it followed a sound plan.

Someone with this channel defined is often asked to lead, as a class representative, a team lead or a spokesperson. Human Design stresses how that works. This leadership is meant to be recognised or elected, not simply claimed. Taken without invitation, the same gift tends to meet resistance.

The tradition places the channel in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], one of the two [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective]] circuits. Logic channels look for patterns that can be trusted and built on. A leader in this circuit is meant to serve the group’s future, not personal power.

## In practice

- **Defined by the Personality** (both gates from the planets at birth): its owner knows people turn to them for direction. See [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]].
- **Defined by the Design** (both gates from the planets about three months earlier): the authority shows before its owner claims it. Others may choose them without being asked.
- **Defined by both, or by one gate from each layer**: the channel is just as defined. Only its colours change.
- **Gate 7 alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a sense of direction, waiting for a gate 31 in someone else to give it a voice.
- **Gate 31 alone**: a voice that people follow, best guided by someone with gate 7.
- **Day to day**: chairing a meeting when asked, speaking for a group of neighbours, setting the plan for a trip. The tradition suggests waiting to be chosen, then leading with the group’s good in view.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 31, *Hsien*, places the lake above the mountain, and Wilhelm called it Influence, or Wooing. Hexagram 7, *Shi*, places earth above water, and Wilhelm called it The Army. Legge asks for a leader “of age and experience” (Legge, 1882). Influence through openness, and a leader worth trusting.
- **Astrology**: both gates lie in [[leo|Leo]], the sign of presence and command. Gate 31 covers 2°00′00″ to 7°37′30″, and gate 7 13°15′00″ to 18°52′30″, with gate 33 between them.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune places thirteen bodies at birth, the Personality, and thirteen at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 31 and gate 7 each hold at least one of these 26 activations, from either layer. The Throat and the G centre then become defined.

If this were the chart’s only channel, the Throat would be joined to no motor, and the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] would be undefined. Ulune’s rules then give a [[hd-projector|Projector]]. With no defined Solar Plexus, Sacral, Spleen or Heart, a defined G and Throat give [[hd-authority-self-projected|self-projected authority]].

On the bodygraph, each half of the line takes its gate’s colour. Ink means the Personality, red the Design, ink dashed with red both. A defined channel is drawn thicker, and its centres are filled gold. The Personality and Design views keep one layer each, so a channel made of one gate from each vanishes there. Without a birth time, a channel that another hour could change is drawn faint.
