## Meaning

Gate 37 is the gate of Friendship. Human Design reads it as warmth built on fair bargains: loyalty given in return for support. It is the agreement sealed with a handshake and a shared meal. It is also the deep hurt felt when the other side does not keep its word.

The gate sits in the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], the emotional motor, whose energy the tradition describes as a wave. Here the wave is warm and relational. Feelings rise with belonging and promises kept, and fall when a bargain feels broken.

It belongs to the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]], at the heart of the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuitry]]. The tradition reads this circuitry as the world of support: family and community, held together by give and take.

## In practice

- **Personality or Design.** A Personality planet (in ink on Ulune’s bodygraph) makes the wish for warm, fair bonds something people value openly. A Design planet (in red) shows it first in how others are drawn into one’s circle. In both layers, the theme is doubly present. See [[hd-layers]].
- **Hanging.** Coloured without gate 40, gate 37 is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the longing for community lacks a steady partner in willpower. Someone with gate 40 can complete it for a while.
- **With gate 40**, it makes the [[hd-channel-40-37|channel of Community]], joining the Solar Plexus to the Heart. Work and support are then given in exchange for loyalty and care, and rest is part of the deal.
- That channel defines the Solar Plexus, so the authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]]. It links no motor to the Throat: with this channel alone, a chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 37, jiā rén, is Wind (xun) above Fire (li), warmth rising from the hearth. Wilhelm calls it The Family (The Clan). Legge reads it as “the regulation of the family”: each member in a clear place.
- **Astrology**: the gate falls in the first half of [[pisces|Pisces]], a sign read as compassion and the wish to belong. In most years the Sun crosses it from about 24 February to 1 March.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune’s gate wheel starts at gate 41, at 2°00′00″ Aquarius (302° of tropical longitude). The other gates follow in the order of the Rave I Ching, each 5°37′30″ (5.625°) wide. Gate 37 is the 7th on the wheel, between gates 55 and 63. It spans **5°45′00″ to 11°22′30″ Pisces** (335.75° to 341.375°).

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 5°45′00″ Pisces, line 2 at 6°41′15″ and line 3 at 7°37′30″. Line 4 starts at 8°33′45″, line 5 at 9°30′00″ and line 6 at 10°26′15″. A longitude exactly on a boundary belongs to the gate or line that begins there.

Ulune places thirteen bodies twice: at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. They are the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours gate 37.
