## Meaning

The channel of Money gives a consistent willpower to manage and direct material resources, and the ability to act on it. Human Design reads it as the drive to be in charge of the means a household, a team or a community lives on. The name speaks of stewardship, not of a promise of wealth.

[[hd-gate-21|Gate 21, the Hunter/Huntress]], sits in the [[hd-centre-heart|Heart]], the motor of willpower and self-worth. It wants to run its own territory. [[hd-gate-45|Gate 45, the Gatherer]], sits in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], the centre of speech and action. It is the voice that brings people together and decides how shared resources are used.

Because the Heart is a motor, this channel joins a motor straight to the Throat. Human Design reads that link as the power to say and do on one’s own initiative. The tradition places the channel in the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuitry]]. That circuitry is about support, bargains and providing for one’s own people.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: both gates are coloured at birth. The wish to be in charge is something the person knows well in themselves.
- **Design**, in red: both gates are coloured about three months before birth. Others may notice the manager in them first.
- **One gate from each layer**: the channel is just as defined; its halves come from two moments.
- **Gate 21 alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a wish to control one’s own domain, often drawn to people who carry gate 45.
- **Gate 45 alone**: a gathering voice, which someone with gate 21 can back with steady will.

Day to day, someone with this channel defined often wants to run their own business, or to hold the household budget. Being trusted with the reins suits them; being watched over every detail does not. The tradition also links the Heart with rest: willpower works in bursts, and keeping promises includes keeping time to recover.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 45, Gathering Together in Wilhelm’s version, places Lake over Earth, water collecting where the ground receives it. Hexagram 21, Biting Through, places Fire over Thunder, an obstacle bitten through with energy. Gathering people and acting firmly: the two halves of the channel.
- **Astrology**: gate 45 spans 17°00′00″ to 22°37′30″ [[gemini|Gemini]], gate 21 spans 9°30′00″ to 15°07′30″ [[aries|Aries]].

## How Ulune calculates it

The channel is defined when gates 45 and 21 each hold at least one of the thirteen activations of the [[hd-layers|Personality or the Design]]. The Throat and the Heart then become defined.

As a chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-manifestor|Manifestor]]: a motor reaches the Throat and the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] is undefined. The strategy is to inform before acting. With no [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], Sacral or [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]] defined, the authority is [[hd-authority-ego|ego]], in the form the tradition calls ego manifested. The [[hd-definition|definition]] is single. In any chart, this channel makes the type Manifestor, or [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]] when the Sacral is defined too.

On the bodygraph, each half takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, red dashes over ink for both. A defined channel is drawn thicker and its centres are filled; a hanging gate colours only its half. Without a birth time, a channel another hour could change is drawn faded.
