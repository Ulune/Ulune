## Meaning

A [[hd-profile|profile]] pairs two [[hd-line|lines]]. The first is that of the [[hd-layers|Personality]] Sun, at birth, which Human Design reads as conscious. The second is that of the Design Sun, about three months earlier, read as unconscious and often clearer to others. In the 1/3 the first is the [[hd-line-1|Investigator]], the second the [[hd-line-3|Martyr]]. Each is the line of the gate where that Sun stands, a hexagram of the I Ching counted from the bottom: lines 1 to 3 form its lower trigram, 4 to 6 its upper one.

Human Design teaches that the first line seeks security in knowledge. It studies, checks and prepares until the ground feels firm. The third line learns another way: by trying things for real and finding out what breaks. Its name sounds heavy, but the tradition reads it as resilience, the practical knowledge that only experience gives.

In the 1/3 the two meet in a loop. The person consciously wants to understand before acting, while life keeps testing that understanding in practice. A failure, in this reading, is not a verdict on the research but its second half. What survives both stages becomes knowledge the person can trust, and later teach.

Both lines of the 1/3 sit in the lower trigram, which Human Design reads as personal. The profile belongs to the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], which the tradition reads as a personal destiny, lived mostly through one’s own process. Its [[hd-cross|Incarnation Cross]] carries that angle.

## In practice

- A cook masters the classic recipes from books, then finds a personal style after ruining a few dinners.
- A new job is approached like a research project, then learned again through the first mistakes on the floor.
- When a plan falls apart, asking what it taught tends to help more than self-reproach. Human Design reads such moments as information, not as faults.
- In a team, the 1/3 often brings both the reading behind an idea and the experience of which parts actually hold.

## Across traditions

In Richard Wilhelm’s I Ching, the first line is the start of a situation, still unseen; the third is a delicate place of transition. It pairs lines in the same place of each trigram, which Human Design calls harmonic. The 1/3’s lines are not a pair; their partners make the [[hd-profile-4-6|4/6]].

## How Ulune calculates it

The first number is the line of the Personality Sun, at birth; the second, the line of the Design Sun, when the Sun stood exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. From [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302° of tropical longitude, each [[hd-gate|gate]] spans 5.625° and each line 0.9375°. The Earth always shares the Sun’s line.

88° is about 93.87 lines. So the Design line usually falls two places after the Personality line, counting on from 6 to 1. It falls three places after only in the last 0.125° of a line. The 1/3 is one of the six common profiles, each about 14.4% of the zodiac, against 2.2% for each rare one. As the Sun moves, the profile steps through the twelve in a fixed order: [[hd-profile-6-3|6/3]], then the 1/3 for about 20 hours, then [[hd-profile-1-4|1/4]] for about three.

Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at noon and at every hour of that day. The Sun moves about a degree a day, more than a line, so the profile is always marked ~: another hour gives a neighbour, usually the 6/3 or the 1/4. The [[hd-cross|cross]] is marked too when its angle or gates could change.
