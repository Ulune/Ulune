## Meaning

In Human Design, strategy is the simple rule of engagement that goes with each [[hd-type|type]]. It answers one practical question: how is this person’s energy best brought into contact with the world? Human Design teaches that following it lowers resistance, so that life meets a person halfway instead of pushing back.

There are four strategies for five types.

- **Inform before acting**, for the [[hd-manifestor|Manifestor]]. Before starting something that affects others, tell them what is about to happen. It is not a request for permission; it simply spares people the shock of a sudden move.
- **Wait to respond**, for the [[hd-generator|Generator]] and the [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]]. Instead of starting from the mind, let life bring something to react to: a question, an offer, an idea met by chance. Then notice the gut’s yes or no. Many teachers add, for the Manifesting Generator, a second step: once the response is clear, inform the people concerned.
- **Wait for the invitation**, for the [[hd-projector|Projector]]. Guidance works best once it has been recognised and asked for, above all for the large things: work, close relationships, where to live.
- **Wait a lunar cycle**, for the [[hd-reflector|Reflector]]. For an important decision, take about a month, the Moon’s full round of the gates, and see what stays constant.

Three of the four strategies involve some form of waiting. The tradition is clear that this is not passivity. It means letting the right moment, the right question or the right invitation arrive, and staying active in everything else meanwhile.

Strategy works together with [[hd-authority|authority]]. Strategy says how an opportunity should arrive; authority says how to decide about it once it has. A Generator with emotional authority, for example, waits for something to respond to, then waits again for the feelings to settle before saying yes.

Two feelings show how well strategy is working. Human Design uses them as signposts: the signature when things flow, the [[hd-not-self|not-self theme]] when they do not.

## In practice

Strategy is meant to be tested, not believed. Teachers often suggest an experiment over weeks or months: follow it in small things first and watch what happens.

- A Manifestor tells their household before rearranging the living room, and notices whether the usual friction drops.
- A Generator stops sending applications by principle and waits for offers that light them up.
- A Projector holds back an unasked opinion in a meeting. Later a colleague asks for it, and the same advice lands quite differently.
- A Reflector lives with the idea of a move for a month before signing a lease, talking it through with different friends.

The point is not to follow a rule rigidly, but to notice where life flows more easily.

## Origin and history

Strategy is part of the system Ra Uru Hu said he received on Ibiza in 1987. He set it out with Lynda Bunnell in *The Definitive Book of Human Design* (2011). Ra Uru Hu presented type, strategy and authority as the practical heart of the system, something every person could try. Chetan Parkyn’s *Human Design* (2009) gives the same four strategies for general readers.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune sets the strategy straight from the type. A Manifestor gets “Inform before acting”, a Projector “Wait for the invitation”, a Reflector “Wait a lunar cycle”. Generators and Manifesting Generators get “Wait to respond”. It is the second card above the bodygraph, after the type. Without a birth time, the strategy is marked ~ whenever another hour of that day would give a type with another strategy.
