## Meaning

Gate 44 is the gate of Alertness. Human Design reads it as an instinctive memory for patterns, above all in people. It is the person who sizes up a candidate or a new acquaintance in minutes, and is usually right. The same memory can let old wariness resurface where it no longer applies.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]], the centre of instinct, intuition in the moment and well-being. Human Design associates the Spleen with the immune system. Its voice is quiet and immediate: it speaks once and does not repeat itself. In gate 44, that instinct draws on patterns seen before.

It belongs to the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]] of the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuitry]], the circuitry of support and belonging. Gate 44 serves the group by sensing who fits where, and whom to trust.

## In practice

- **Personality or Design.** A Personality planet (in ink on Ulune’s bodygraph) makes the sense for people a known talent for first impressions. A Design planet (in red) makes it a quieter ease or unease, felt in the body. In both layers, the theme is doubly present. See [[hd-layers]].
- **Hanging.** Coloured without gate 26, gate 44 is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the knowledge of people lacks the willpower to persuade. Someone with gate 26 can complete it for a while, and together they make a strong team.
- **With gate 26**, it makes the [[hd-channel-44-26|channel of Surrender]], joining the Spleen to the Heart. Instinct for people meets the will to persuade, a gift often seen in sales or recruiting. Honesty about what is offered keeps it trusted.
- That channel defines the Spleen and the Heart. Without a defined Solar Plexus or Sacral, the authority is [[hd-authority-splenic|splenic]]. With this channel alone, a chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 44, gòu, is Heaven (qian) above Wind (xun): one yielding line arriving beneath five strong ones. Wilhelm calls it Coming to Meet. The classic texts read a meeting that comes of its own accord and asks for discernment.
- **Astrology**: the gate falls in the first half of [[scorpio|Scorpio]], a sign read as perception and intensity. In most years the Sun crosses it from about 31 October to 5 November.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune’s gate wheel starts at gate 41, at 2°00′00″ Aquarius (302° of tropical longitude). The other gates follow in the order of the Rave I Ching, each 5°37′30″ (5.625°) wide. Gate 44 is the 50th on the wheel, between gates 28 and 1. It spans **7°37′30″ to 13°15′00″ Scorpio** (217.625° to 223.25°).

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 7°37′30″ Scorpio, line 2 at 8°33′45″ and line 3 at 9°30′00″. Line 4 starts at 10°26′15″, line 5 at 11°22′30″ and line 6 at 12°18′45″. A longitude exactly on a boundary belongs to the gate or line that begins there.

Ulune places thirteen bodies twice: at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. They are the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours gate 44.
