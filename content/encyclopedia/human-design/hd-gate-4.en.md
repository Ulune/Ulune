## Meaning

Gate 4 is the gate of Formulisation. It produces answers: logical formulas that explain why something happens. Human Design is clear that these answers are not right by default. They are proposals, made to be tested against the facts.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]], the centre of mental awareness, where Human Design places the processing of ideas, concepts and opinions. The tradition places gate 4 in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], also called the Understanding circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit group]]. Human Design teaches that this circuit looks for patterns that can be repeated and checked, so that what is learned can be shared and can make the future safer for everyone.

Someone with this gate often has an explanation ready within seconds of hearing a problem. The tradition reads the restless need to find an answer as a signal of the gate at work. It suggests checking the formula before defending it, and keeping it for shared questions rather than every personal decision.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: a planet stood in gate 4 at birth. Human Design reads it as the side of the theme a person knows in themselves.
- **Design**, in red: a planet stood here about three months before birth, when the Sun was 88° earlier. It is the side others often see more clearly.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: gate 4 is coloured but its partner, [[hd-gate-63]], is not. Answers come readily, and the doubt that sharpens them arrives through others who carry gate 63.
- **Completed**: with gate 63 coloured too, the [[hd-channel-63-4|channel of Logic]] is defined, joining the [[hd-centre-head|Head]] to the Ajna. Ulune’s texts describe a constant rhythm of question and answer: doubt raises the question and the mind proposes a formula to test. It serves science and planning well, and can tire when turned on every private choice.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 4, Méng 蒙, which Wilhelm called Youthful Folly. Mountain stands over Water: a spring at the foot of a mountain, not yet knowing its course. The text is about teacher and pupil, and the pupil must ask; as Legge has it, “he comes and seeks me” (Legge, 1882).
- **Astrology**: the gate covers the second half of [[leo|Leo]], a sign astrologers read as confidence and self-expression. The Sun crosses it each year roughly from 11 to 17 August.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] around the tropical zodiac from gate 41 at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius, each 5.625° or 5°37′30″ wide, in the wheel’s order. Gate 4 is the 36th, between gates 7 and 29, so it begins 35 × 5.625° = 196.875° after gate 41. It runs from 18°52′30″ to 24°30′00″ Leo, that is from 138.875° to 144.5°.

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 18°52′30″, line 2 at 19°48′45″, line 3 at 20°45′00″, line 4 at 21°41′15″, line 5 at 22°37′30″ and line 6 at 23°33′45″ Leo.

Any of the thirteen bodies Ulune places at birth and at the Design moment that falls in this span colours gate 4, with its line. Without a birth time, a gate or line that could differ at another hour of the day is marked.
