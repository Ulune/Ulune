## Meaning

Gate 48, Depth, is one of the seven gates of the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]], the centre of instinct and intuition. Its theme is depth of knowledge.

Human Design pictures this gate as a well, a store of understanding gathered patiently. Someone with it often studies a subject thoroughly and gives solid answers.

Each gate of the Spleen carries a fear in the tradition’s teaching, and this one is the fear of not knowing enough. It can show as hesitation: waiting to feel ready before teaching, sharing or applying what one knows. Human Design reads this kindly, as a signal to notice. Often the person is ready well before they feel it.

The tradition places gate 48 in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], also called the Understanding circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit]] group. It tests patterns and improves them for everyone’s future; gate 48 gives them depth.

## In practice

A gate is coloured when a planet stands in it at birth or in the Design, as [[hd-gate|Gate]] and [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]] explain.

- **By a Personality planet**, at birth: the person tends to know their own depth, and may know their doubt about it just as well.
- **By a Design planet**, about three months earlier: others often see the depth first. They come for advice before the person thinks of themselves as an expert.
- **By both**: the theme is doubled, known and lived.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: without gate 16, the well waits for a way out into the world. People with [[hd-gate-16|gate 16]], Skills, bring the enthusiasm for practice that lets the knowledge be used.
- **Completed into a channel**: with gate 16, the [[hd-channel-16-48|channel of the Wavelength]] is defined, joining the Spleen to the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. It makes talent steady: deep understanding joined to the will to practise. Years of repetition often turn it into mastery.

When this is a chart’s only channel, Ulune finds a [[hd-projector|Projector]] with [[hd-authority-splenic|splenic authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 48, *Ching*, places Water above Wood. Its trigrams are *K’an*, the Abysmal, over *Sun*, the Gentle. Wilhelm titles it The Well: a wooden bucket lifting water up from below. Legge’s text stresses that the well serves everyone: “those who come and those who go can draw and enjoy the benefit” (Legge, 1882). Turned upside down, it becomes hexagram 47, the image of [[hd-gate-47|gate 47]].
- **Astrology**: the gate spans 9°30′00″ to 15°07′30″ [[libra|Libra]], the sign of weighing and fair judgement.

## How Ulune calculates it

- Gate 41 opens the wheel at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of tropical longitude. Each gate then spans 5°37′30″ (5.625°) in a fixed order.
- Gate 48 is the 45th, from 9°30′00″ to 15°07′30″ Libra (189.5° to 195.125°). Its six lines are 0°56′15″ wide and begin at 9°30′00″, 10°26′15″, 11°22′30″, 12°18′45″, 13°15′00″ and 14°11′15″ Libra.
- Ulune reads thirteen bodies at birth and again when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The Sun, the Earth, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto each colour the gate they stand in.
- When gate 16 is coloured too, at either moment, the channel 16–48 is defined. Ulune notes whether its gates come from the Personality, the Design or both.
- With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by the profile. Profiles 1/3 to 4/6 give the Right Angle Cross of Tension 3. Profile 4/1 gives the Juxtaposition Cross of Depth. Profiles 5/1 to 6/3 give the Left Angle Cross of Endeavour 2.
