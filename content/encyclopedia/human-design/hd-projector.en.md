## Meaning

The Projector is one of the five [[hd-type|types]] of Human Design, the one the tradition describes as made to guide. Projectors are said to see clearly how other people and whole systems work, and to know how their energy could be used better.

The type is what remains once the other three with defined centres are ruled out. A Projector has at least one defined centre, but the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]], the centre of steady work energy, is undefined. And no motor, whether the [[hd-centre-heart|Heart]], the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]] or the [[hd-centre-root|Root]], reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]] through defined channels. A Projector can still have defined motors; they simply do not connect to the Throat.

Without a defined Sacral, Human Design teaches, Projectors lack a steady, renewable supply of energy for work. They are not built to labour all day as a [[hd-generator|Generator]] does. Their gift lies elsewhere: in attention, insight and the ability to direct the energy of others.

That gift works best when it is wanted. The [[hd-strategy|strategy]] of the type is to wait for the invitation. This matters above all for the large things: work, close relationships and where to live. The tradition holds that guidance offered before it is recognised tends to be ignored, however accurate it is. Recognised and invited, the same insight can change a team or a family.

Human Design names success as the signature of the Projector: not fame, but the sense of being seen, used well and rewarded. Its [[hd-not-self|not-self theme]] is bitterness. The tradition reads it as a gentle signal of having worked unseen, or given guidance no one asked for.

The tradition describes the Projector’s aura as focused and penetrating. It reaches into one person at a time. Others may feel deeply seen by a Projector, and that attention lands best when it is welcome.

## In practice

- **Invitations** are mainly for the big turns of life. Daily choices, the tradition says, can be made freely by the Projector’s [[hd-authority|authority]].
- **Recognition** comes more easily through mastery. Many Projectors study a system, a craft or a field in depth, and are then sought out for it.
- **Rest** and time alone help, because Human Design teaches that undefined centres take in and amplify the energy around them. Leaving before exhaustion is often advised.
- **Bitterness** is worth noticing early; it often follows effort that was never acknowledged.
- **Guiding** and managing roles suit many Projectors: coaching, consulting, teaching, coordinating.

Projectors have the widest range of authorities. A defined Solar Plexus gives [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]] authority. Next in order, a defined [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]] gives [[hd-authority-splenic|splenic]] authority, then a defined Heart gives [[hd-authority-ego|ego]] authority. With the [[hd-centre-g|G]] joined to the Throat, it is [[hd-authority-self-projected|self-projected]]: clarity comes from hearing oneself speak. When the definition lies only in the [[hd-centre-head|Head]], the [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]] and the Throat, the authority is [[hd-authority-mental|mental]], or environmental. Clarity then comes from the right places and sounding boards.

## Origin and history

The type belongs to the system Ra Uru Hu said he received on Ibiza in 1987. He set it out with Lynda Bunnell in *The Definitive Book of Human Design* (2011). The Projector is one of the four types that book describes. Chetan Parkyn’s *Human Design* (2009) gives a similar account for general readers.

Jovian Archive, the company that publishes Ra Uru Hu’s work, puts Projectors at just over 20% of people, about one in five.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune casts the [[hd-layers|Personality and the Design]], colours their gates and defines every [[hd-channel|channel]] whose two gates are coloured, with the centres it joins. The type is Projector when at least one centre is defined but not the Sacral. No path of defined channels may lead from the Throat to a motor.

The authority is read in a fixed order: Solar Plexus, Spleen, Heart, then the G with the Throat. When none applies, it is Mental. The strategy shown is “Wait for the invitation”, and the Keys table adds “Success · Bitterness”. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon and at every hour of the day. If another hour gives another type, the type is marked ~.
