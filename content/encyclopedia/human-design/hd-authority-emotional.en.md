## Meaning

In Human Design, the [[hd-authority|authority]] is the part of the bodygraph, the system’s chart of nine centres, that the tradition says to trust when a decision is due. Emotional authority belongs to everyone whose [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]] is defined, that is, reached by at least one complete [[hd-channel|channel]]. About half of all people have it, which makes it the most common authority.

The Solar Plexus is the centre of emotions. Human Design counts it both as a motor, a source of energy, and as an awareness centre. Its feelings move in a wave: hope and disappointment, excitement and calm, rising and falling over hours or days. The tradition’s point is simple. A choice felt at the top of the wave looks different at the bottom, so neither moment alone tells the whole story.

Emotional authority therefore works with time. Human Design teaches that, for this design, there is no truth in the moment. Clarity is not a flash. It builds as the same question is felt through several moods, until the answer stops moving much. It is rarely total certainty; the tradition speaks of a calm, settled clarity that is enough to act on.

Because the Solar Plexus comes first in the tradition’s order, Emotional authority applies to every type that has it defined: [[hd-manifestor|Manifestors]], [[hd-generator|Generators]], [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generators]] and [[hd-projector|Projectors]]. Only [[hd-reflector|Reflectors]], with no defined centre, never have it. It then shapes how each type lives its [[hd-strategy|strategy]]. A Generator still waits for something to respond to, but lets the response ripen. A Manifestor still informs before acting, once the feeling has settled.

## In practice

- **A job offer.** The first evening it feels thrilling; two days later it feels heavy. The tradition suggests waiting until the offer has been felt at both ends. An offer that still feels right after a low mood and a high one is usually a real yes.
- **Sleeping on it.** For small choices, one night is often enough. For a move or a relationship, several days or weeks may be needed.
- **Telling others.** A plain “I’ll let you know tomorrow” helps everyone. Human Design holds that people with this authority are not slow, only accurate with time.
- **Pressure.** A seller pushing for an answer now is a familiar test. In the tradition’s reading, a choice made at the peak or the bottom of the wave, and regretted once it turns, is a [[hd-not-self|not-self]] signal worth noticing, never a fault.

## Origin and history

The authorities come from the teaching of Ra Uru Hu, who founded Human Design after an experience on Ibiza in January 1987. In that teaching the Solar Plexus heads the order of authorities: when it is defined, its wave takes precedence over the immediate signals of the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] and the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]]. The emotional wave that must run its course is one of the system’s best-known ideas.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the authority from the defined centres, in a fixed order (see [[hd-authority]]). The first rule is: if the Solar Plexus is defined, the authority is Emotional, whatever else is defined. The Solar Plexus is defined when one of its seven channels is complete: [[hd-channel-12-22|12–22]] or [[hd-channel-35-36|35–36]] to the Throat, [[hd-channel-40-37|40–37]] to the Heart, [[hd-channel-59-6|59–6]] to the Sacral, or [[hd-channel-19-49|19–49]], [[hd-channel-39-55|39–55]] and [[hd-channel-41-30|41–30]] to the Root. A channel is complete when both its gates are active, from the Personality, the Design or one of each.

Choosing the authority on the bodygraph lights its seat, the Solar Plexus. Ulune does not tell apart the different kinds of emotional wave the tradition describes for different channels. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon and again at every hour of that day; if the authority differs at any hour, Ulune marks it as one that could differ.
