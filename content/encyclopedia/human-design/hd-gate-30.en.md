## Meaning

Gate 30 sits in the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], the centre Human Design gives to emotions, read as a wave that rises and falls over time. Its theme is desire: intense feelings that push towards new experiences, and the recognition of those feelings as they move.

The tradition places it in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit group]]. Collective energy is read as sharing: what one person lives becomes a lesson for many. In the Sensing circuit, that lesson comes from living an experience fully, then looking back on it.

Human Design reads the gate with a gentle realism. The longing for a trip, a relationship or a new life is genuine, and it deserves to be lived. What usually disappoints is not the experience but the expectations attached to its outcome. The experience itself is the point.

## In practice

A planet colours the gate either at birth, in the [[hd-layers|Personality]], or about three months before, in the Design.

- **Personality.** The strength of desire is usually felt as one’s own, and named.
- **Design.** The gate is read as working out of sight, as a hunger for experience others may notice first.
- **Both.** The theme is marked twice and reads as especially steady.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]], it is half a channel. Its owner may feel their longings take shape near someone with gate 41, who brings the fantasy that starts them.
- **Completed** by [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]], Contraction, in the [[hd-centre-root|Root]], it forms the [[hd-channel-41-30|channel of Recognition]]. The Root and the Solar Plexus are then defined: steady pressure to imagine new experiences and desire them. With this channel alone, the chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]]’s, with [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional authority]]: clarity comes once the feelings have settled.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 30, *Li*, is the Clinging, Fire, in Richard Wilhelm’s version. The trigram of Fire is doubled: light that clings to what it burns, as a flame clings to wood. James Legge’s translation counsels to “nourish (a docility like that of) the cow” (1882). Wilhelm reads it as clarity that depends on what it rests upon.
- **Astrology.** The gate covers the last degrees of [[aquarius|Aquarius]], the fixed air sign, and just touches [[pisces|Pisces]]. The Sun crosses it for about six days in mid-February.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays a wheel of 64 gates over the tropical zodiac. The wheel starts with gate 41 at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of longitude. Each gate covers 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the order of the Human Design wheel. Gate 30 comes 5th, after gate 49 and before gate 55. It runs from **24°30′00″ Aquarius to 0°07′30″ Pisces**.

Each of its six [[hd-line|lines]] spans 0°56′15″:

| 2 | 25°26′15″ Aquarius | 26°22′30″ Aquarius |

| 4 | 27°18′45″ Aquarius | 28°15′00″ Aquarius |
| 5 | 28°15′00″ Aquarius | 29°11′15″ Aquarius |
| 6 | 29°11′15″ Aquarius | 0°07′30″ Pisces |

Thirteen bodies are read twice: at birth, and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. They are the Sun and the Earth, the Moon, the true North Node and the South Node, and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours the gate, with its line. With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by [[hd-profile|profile]]:

- from 1/3 to 4/6, the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle Cross of Contagion 1]];
- in 4/1, the [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition Cross of Fates]];
- from 5/1 to 6/3, the [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle Cross of Industry 1]].
