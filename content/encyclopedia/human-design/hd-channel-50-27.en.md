## Meaning

The channel of Preservation links the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]] at [[hd-gate-50|gate 50]] to the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] at [[hd-gate-27|gate 27]]. Ulune reads it as a consistent energy to care for others, guided by instinctive values about what keeps a group healthy.

Each gate brings half of that. Gate 50, Values, carries a sense of the rules and standards that protect the people in one’s keeping. Gate 27, Caring, gives the Sacral’s energy to feed, shelter and look after them. Joined, they make care a steady part of a person.

The Spleen is the centre of instinct and of the body’s sense of safety in the moment; Human Design associates it with the immune system. The Sacral is the motor of life force and of work energy. Here instinct sets the standard and the Sacral supplies the energy to keep to it.

The tradition places this channel in the [[hd-circuit-defense|Defense circuit]]. With the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]], Defense makes up the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuitry]], the channels of family, support and belonging. Defense is read as the circuit that protects and nurtures those who cannot yet look after themselves. Its only other channel is [[hd-channel-59-6|59–6]], the channel of Mating.

## In practice

- **Personality, Design or both.** Defined by the Personality (the planets at birth, drawn in ink), the channel tends to be known from the inside: one sees oneself as a carer. Defined by the Design (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red), it works more bodily, and others often notice it first. With one gate in each [[hd-layers|layer]], it is just as defined.
- **One gate alone.** Gate 50 alone is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a feel for standards, waiting for the energy to carry them. Gate 27 alone offers care that comes and goes, without a fixed rule for it.
- **Day to day.** Someone with this channel often looks after children, elders or colleagues, and sets the standard for how things are done.
- **Care that includes oneself.** Ulune’s reading notes that caring for oneself belongs in the same picture. Tiredness from looking after everyone is read as a signal, not a failing.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Gate 50 is hexagram 50, which Wilhelm calls The Caldron: Fire over Wood, a vessel that cooks and offers food. Gate 27 is hexagram 27, The Corners of the Mouth, or Providing Nourishment: the Mountain over Thunder, drawn like an open mouth. Both speak of feeding.
- **Astrology.** Gate 50 spans 26°22′30″ [[libra|Libra]] to 2°00′00″ [[scorpio|Scorpio]]. Gate 27 spans 2°00′00″ to 7°37′30″ [[taurus|Taurus]]. The channel joins the sign of fair measure to the sign of nourishment and steady comfort.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune casts 26 activations: 13 bodies at birth for the Personality, 13 for the Design, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 50 and gate 27 each hold at least one of them, from either layer. The Spleen and the Sacral then become defined.

Run as the chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-generator|Generator]]: the Sacral is defined and no motor reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The authority is [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral]], since the Solar Plexus is not defined, and the definition is single.

On the bodygraph, each half of the channel takes the colour of its gate. It is ink for the Personality, red for the Design, and ink dashed with red when both layers stand in that gate. A defined channel is drawn thicker than a hanging half, and its two centres are filled in gold. The reading names the layer: Personality, Design, or Personality + Design when both layers share the channel. In the Personality or Design view, only that layer’s bodies count.
