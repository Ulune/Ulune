## Meaning

The Centering circuit is the smaller of the two circuits in the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuitry]]. It has only two [[hd-channel|channels]], and both start in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], the centre Human Design links to identity, direction and love. Their theme is centering: becoming fully oneself, and in doing so showing others that they can do the same.

The tradition reads this as empowerment by example. A centred person does not need to persuade anyone. Their way of living, consistent and self-directed, gives others permission to find their own centre. Like all Individual energy, it carries something unusual, and it influences best when it is simply lived.

The two channels:

- [[hd-channel-10-34|Exploration]] (10–34), G centre to [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]]: Sacral power given to one’s own convictions. The tradition reads it as following one’s own path, with the energy to keep going.
- [[hd-channel-25-51|Initiation]] (25–51), G centre to [[hd-centre-heart|Heart]]: the drive to be first and to leap into the unknown. Shocks along the way are read as deepening the spirit rather than breaking it.

Each channel pairs a gate of the self with a motor. [[hd-gate-10|Gate 10, Behaviour of the Self]], meets [[hd-gate-34|gate 34, Power]], the Sacral’s independent working energy. [[hd-gate-25|Gate 25, The Spirit of the Self]], meets [[hd-gate-51|gate 51, Shock]], the Heart’s competitive courage. In both, the identity is backed by a source of energy, which is why the tradition reads the circuit as grounding.

Gates 10 and 34 are also two of the four gates of the [[hd-circuit-integration|Integration channels]]. Through its other channels, a person with gate 10 or 34 can link this circuit to that self-empowering group.

## In practice

- **Finding it.** Ulune draws the channels but does not group them by circuit. Exploration or Initiation defined in the chart means Centering energy is consistent.
- **Exploration.** A defined Sacral makes the chart a [[hd-generator|Generator]] or a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]]. The tradition advises letting the Sacral’s response choose which conviction to follow.
- **Initiation.** It defines the G centre and the Heart, the centre of willpower. With no other channel, Ulune reads a [[hd-projector|Projector]] with [[hd-authority-ego|Ego authority]]. Other channels can change both, for example into a [[hd-manifestor|Manifestor]] when the Heart reaches the Throat.
- **Living rather than telling.** Human Design suggests that this circuit influences most through example. Explaining one’s choices helps, but the life itself carries the message.
- **Love of self.** The tradition links the G centre to love, and this circuit to a kind of self-love that steadies others. It reads this kindly: knowing where one stands is a gift to a group, not a withdrawal from it.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** The circuit’s four hexagrams form a telling set. Wilhelm calls 10 Treading (Conduct) and 25 Innocence (The Unexpected). He calls 34 The Power of the Great and 51 The Arousing (Shock, Thunder). Conduct and innocence backed by power and thunder suit a circuit of grounded selfhood. Human Design did not group them for that reason, though.

## Origin and history

The Centering circuit belongs to Ra Uru Hu’s teaching on circuitry, described in The Definitive Book of Human Design (2011). Some teachers count its two channels apart from the Individual group, alongside the Integration channels. This encyclopedia follows the more usual placement inside the Individual circuitry.
