## Meaning

The channel of Synthesis links the [[hd-centre-root|Root]] at [[hd-gate-19|gate 19]] to the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]] at [[hd-gate-49|gate 49]]. Ulune reads it as a consistent sensitivity to needs and principles within relationships and groups.

Gate 19, Wanting, is the Root’s pressure to be close and to have basic needs met: food, shelter, touch, belonging. Gate 49, Principles, is the emotional sense of which bonds to keep and which to end. Joined, they sense what a group needs and decide, with feeling, who belongs in it.

The Root is both a pressure centre and a motor, the source of the adrenaline that gets life moving. The Solar Plexus is a motor too, the centre of emotions, moving in waves. Here the Root’s need is weighed by the emotional wave before it becomes a choice.

The tradition places this channel in the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]]. With the [[hd-circuit-defense|Defense circuit]], Ego makes up the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuitry]], the channels of family, support and belonging. The Ego circuit is read through the bargains that keep a community together: who provides, who is provided for. 19–49 holds the terms of those bargains.

## In practice

- **Personality, Design or both.** Defined by the Personality (the planets at birth, drawn in ink), the sensitivity tends to be known and owned. Defined by the Design (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red), it works more bodily, and others may feel it first. With one gate in each [[hd-layers|layer]], it is just as defined.
- **One gate alone.** Gate 19 alone is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a sensitivity to needs, without fixed principles to sort them. Gate 49 alone holds principles that come and go with the mood.
- **Day to day.** Someone with this channel often notices what a partner or a community needs before it is said. They tend to have firm views on who belongs.
- **Clarity before choosing.** Ulune’s reading notes that emotional clarity keeps their decisions about people fair. A rejection made in a low mood is read as a signal to wait, not as a final word.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Gate 19 is hexagram 19, which Wilhelm calls Approach: Earth over the Lake, the shore drawing close to the water. Gate 49 is hexagram 49, Revolution, or Molting: the Lake over Fire, a pelt changed with the seasons. Drawing near and changing the terms are the channel’s two movements.
- **Astrology.** Both gates lie in [[aquarius|Aquarius]], the sign of community and of the group. Gate 19 spans 7°37′30″ to 13°15′00″, and gate 49 spans 18°52′30″ to 24°30′00″.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune casts 26 activations: 13 bodies at birth for the Personality, 13 for the Design, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 19 and gate 49 each hold at least one, from either layer. The Root and the Solar Plexus then become defined.

Run as the chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-projector|Projector]]. Two motors are defined, but none reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], and the Sacral stays undefined. The authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]], since a defined Solar Plexus comes first in Ulune’s order of authorities. The definition is single.

On the bodygraph, each half of the channel takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, ink dashed with red for both. Defined, the line is drawn thicker than a hanging half, and the two centres are filled in gold. In the Personality or Design view, only that layer’s bodies count.
