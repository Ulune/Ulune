## Meaning

Gate 62, Details, sits in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], the centre of communication and action, where Human Design sees energy become something others can hear or see. Its theme is the voice of facts and details: naming things precisely, so that an idea can be checked and understood.

The tradition reads this gate as the one that brings an opinion down to earth. It asks for the figures, the dates, the exact wording. Someone with it may write clear reports, remember precise numbers or correct an imprecise statement. The skill lies in choice: too many details can lose the listener, and knowing which ones matter is the gate’s craft. Human Design sees the gift at its best when the details serve a picture that others can follow.

The tradition places gate 62 in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], also called the Understanding circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective]] group. That circuit tests patterns against the facts and shares what holds, so that everyone can rely on it. Gate 62 is where its findings are put into words.

## In practice

- **Activated by a Personality planet**, at birth, the person knows their taste for precision and may use it in writing, teaching or research. **By a Design planet**, from about three months earlier, it shows on its own: others hear the exact word before the person notices choosing it. **By both**, it is a strong and constant trait.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**, gate 62 does not define the Throat. It looks for its partner, [[hd-gate-17|gate 17, Opinions]], in the [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]]. People who carry gate 17 tend to offer the view that gate 62 then backs with facts.
- **Completed into the [[hd-channel-17-62|channel of Acceptance]]**, it defines the Ajna and the Throat. Ulune describes a steady ability to turn an opinion into an organised, detailed explanation, often the one who writes the proposal. It works best when others have asked for that view. With no motor in it, the channel shapes how a person explains, not how they decide.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 62, *Xiao Guo*, puts Zhen, thunder, above Gen, the mountain. Wilhelm called it Preponderance of the Small. Its judgment says, in Baynes’s English, “Small things may be done; great things should not be done” (Wilhelm, 1924). Care for the small, done well, is close to Ulune’s reading of a gate that lives in the details.
- **Astrology.** The gate lies in the late degrees of [[cancer|Cancer]], the water sign that astrology links to memory, care and belonging.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune places gate 62 from 20°45′00″ to 26°22′30″ Cancer, 110.75° to 116.375° of tropical longitude. The wheel starts with [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius. Each gate takes 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the fixed order of the Human Design wheel, and gate 62 is the thirty-first. Its six [[hd-line|lines]] take 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 ends at 21°41′15″ Cancer, line 2 at 22°37′30″ and line 3 at 23°33′45″. Line 4 ends at 24°30′00″ and line 5 at 25°26′15″.

Ulune reads thirteen bodies: the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes, and Mercury to Pluto. It reads them at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. With the Sun here, the Earth is always in [[hd-gate-61|gate 61]]. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon, and positions that could change with the hour are flagged.
