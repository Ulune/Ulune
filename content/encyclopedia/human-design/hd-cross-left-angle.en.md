## Meaning

The [[hd-cross|Incarnation Cross]] gathers four gates: the Sun and the Earth at birth, the Personality, and in the Design, about three months earlier. Human Design reads it as the theme of a life. Its angle, one of three, comes from the [[hd-profile|profile]]. About a third of all charts have a Left Angle.

Human Design describes a Left Angle cross as a transpersonal destiny. The life unfolds largely through meeting others: through the people it encounters, what passes between them, and what each brings to the other. Where a [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]] life follows its own process, a Left Angle life finds its purpose in the meeting itself. Some teachers speak of a life bound up with other people’s stories.

Four profiles give a Left Angle cross:

- [[hd-profile-5-1|5/1]] and [[hd-profile-5-2|5/2]], with the [[hd-line-5|Heretic]] at birth.
- [[hd-profile-6-2|6/2]] and [[hd-profile-6-3|6/3]], with the [[hd-line-6|Role Model]].

In each, the line of the Personality Sun is 5 or 6. Both belong to the upper trigram of the hexagram, which Human Design reads as its transpersonal half, turned towards others.

## In practice

As with every cross, Human Design teaches that the theme appears by itself when someone follows their [[hd-type|type]], [[hd-strategy|strategy]] and [[hd-authority|authority]]. For a Left Angle, the meetings that matter are read as part of that same unfolding, not as something to arrange.

- Someone may notice that their turning points came through particular meetings rather than through plans of their own.
- Others may seem to carry part of their purpose, and they part of others’. The tradition reads this as the nature of the angle.
- Feeling dependent on others is common here. Human Design reads it as fitting for a transpersonal life, not as weakness.

## Origin and history

The three angles belong to Ra Uru Hu’s system, founded in 1987. The Definitive Book of Human Design, written with Lynda Bunnell, ties them to the profiles. The names of the Left Angle crosses come from the same system.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the profile: the line of the Personality Sun, then that of the Design Sun. The Design Sun is the Sun exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. A Personality line of 5 or 6 gives Left Angle.

Lines 5 and 6 are the last third of every gate, so the Left Angle covers exactly a third of the Sun’s path. By then the Design Sun has crossed into the next gate, fifteen places behind the Personality Sun’s rather than sixteen. The four gates no longer form a square.

The Earth stands opposite the Sun in both layers. So two crosses share the same four gates, their Sun and Earth swapped: 2/1 | 49/4 and 1/2 | 4/49. Ulune gives the pair one name, followed by 1 or 2. Number 1 goes to the cross whose Personality Sun stands in the half of the wheel from [[hd-gate-13|gate 13]] to gate 33. Number 2 goes to the half from gate 7 to gate 19. So 2/1 | 49/4 is the Left Angle Cross of Defiance 1, and 1/2 | 4/49 is Defiance 2.

That gives 64 Left Angle crosses with 32 names. Each shares its four gates with a [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]] cross; only the profile tells them apart. In French, Ulune follows the French Human Design sources, which name this angle the “angle ouvert”, as in “Croix de l’angle ouvert du défi 1”. For the Cross of Upheaval, which has no published French name that Ulune could find, it gives its own translation. Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at every hour of that day. The cross is marked ~ when its gates or angle could differ.
