## Meaning

Gate 22 is one of the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] of the bodygraph, and Ulune calls it Openness. It sits in the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], the centre Human Design links with emotions, and both a motor and an awareness. Its theme is social grace and attentive listening, and both depend on mood.

When its owner feels good, they can be warm and charming at a dinner, and listen with real attention. When they do not, they need to be left alone. Human Design reads these swings as part of an emotional wave, not as moodiness to fix. Respecting them, rather than forcing sociability, keeps the grace genuine.

The tradition places gate 22 in the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]], part of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual]] circuitry. Human Design links that circuitry with what is new and personal, shared in a way that can move others.

## In practice

- **Personality** (the planets at birth, drawn in ink): the gate is conscious. Its owner tends to know their social ease comes and goes. See [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]].
- **Design** (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red): the gate works below awareness. Others may read the mood before its owner has named it.
- **Both**: the theme is coloured twice, and tends to be a familiar part of the person.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the grace is there, and it reaches for gate 12, its partner across the channel. People who carry gate 12 can give that feeling a voice.
- **Completed** by gate 12 in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], it forms the channel of [[hd-channel-12-22|Openness]]. Ulune reads it as emotional expression and social grace that are always there, always coloured by mood. The channel joins a motor to the Throat, which makes a [[hd-manifestor|Manifestor]] when the Sacral is undefined.
- **Defined** this way, the Solar Plexus gives [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional authority]]: clarity comes over time, not in the moment.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 22, *Bi*, places the mountain above fire, a flame lighting the mountain’s foot. Wilhelm called it Grace. His judgment opens: “Grace has success.” His commentary adds that grace adorns things without being their essence.
- **Astrology**: the gate falls in the second half of [[pisces|Pisces]], the sign of feeling, empathy and the arts.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the gates on the tropical zodiac, from gate 41 at 302° (2° Aquarius), each 5°37′30″ wide. Gate 22 is the 9th gate of that wheel, between gate 63 and gate 36. It covers 17°00′00″ to 22°37′30″ Pisces. Its six [[hd-line|lines]] of 0°56′15″ begin at 17°00′00″, 17°56′15″, 18°52′30″, 19°48′45″, 20°45′00″ and 21°41′15″.

Ulune places thirteen bodies on the wheel: the Sun and the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes, and Mercury to Pluto. It does so at birth and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. Any of these 26 positions in the span colours the gate. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon. Ulune then flags any body whose gate or line could change at another hour.

When the Personality Sun stands here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] after gate 22. The [[hd-profile|profile]] sets its angle: [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]] or [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle]].
