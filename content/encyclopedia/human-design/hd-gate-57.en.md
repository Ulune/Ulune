## Meaning

Gate 57, Intuitive Clarity, sits in the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]], the oldest awareness centre in Human Design. The tradition associates the Spleen with instinct, intuition and the immune system. It speaks in the present moment about what is safe. Gate 57 is that intuition at its finest, often felt as an inner hearing.

It shows as a quiet, one-time sense that something is off: do not sign that lease, call that friend now. Because it speaks once and softly, it is easy to dismiss; learning to hear it is the gate’s practice. The tradition gives each Spleen gate a fear of its own. Here it concerns what is to come, and is read as a passing signal, not a verdict.

Like gates 10, 20 and 34, gate 57 takes part in three channels, and the tradition places it in two circuits. Through Perfected Form and Power it belongs to the [[hd-circuit-integration|Integration]] channels, about self-empowerment and survival. Through the Brain Wave it belongs to the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]] of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual]] group.

## In practice

- **Activated by a Personality planet**, at birth, the intuition is usually known and can be trusted on purpose. **By a Design planet**, from about three months earlier, it works in the body and is often only recognised afterwards. **By both**, it is a strong, central sense.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**, gate 57 does not define the Spleen. It reaches for three possible partners, and people who carry one of them can close a channel with it for a while.
- **Completed into a channel**, it defines the Spleen. With [[hd-gate-20|gate 20, The Now]], in the Throat, it forms the [[hd-channel-20-57|Brain Wave]]: intuition put into words at once. With [[hd-gate-10|gate 10, Behaviour of the Self]], in the G centre, it forms [[hd-channel-10-57|Perfected Form]]: acting true to oneself while sensing what keeps one well. With [[hd-gate-34|gate 34, Power]], in the Sacral, it forms the [[hd-channel-34-57|channel of Power]]: quick, instinctive responses.
- A defined Spleen gives [[hd-authority-splenic|splenic authority]] when neither the Solar Plexus nor the Sacral is defined.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 57, *Xun*, doubles the trigram Xun, wind and wood. Wilhelm called it The Gentle, the Penetrating, and its image reads, in Baynes’s English, “Winds following one upon the other” (Wilhelm, 1924). Wind gets in everywhere by gentle, steady pressure, which fits an intuition that slips in quietly.
- **Astrology.** The gate lies in the middle of [[libra|Libra]], the air sign that astrology links to balance, judgement and relationship.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune places gate 57 from 15°07′30″ to 20°45′00″ Libra, 195.125° to 200.75° of tropical longitude. The wheel starts with [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius. Each gate takes 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the fixed order of the Human Design wheel, and gate 57 is the forty-sixth. Its six [[hd-line|lines]] take 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 ends at 16°03′45″ Libra, line 2 at 17°00′00″ and line 3 at 17°56′15″. Line 4 ends at 18°52′30″ and line 5 at 19°48′45″.

Ulune reads thirteen bodies: the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes, and Mercury to Pluto. It reads them at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. With the Sun here, the Earth is always in [[hd-gate-51|gate 51]]. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon, and positions that could change with the hour are flagged.
