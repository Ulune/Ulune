## Meaning

Gate 50, Values, is one of the seven gates of the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]], the centre of instinct. Its theme is values, and responsibility for the group’s wellbeing.

Human Design reads it as the guardian of a household’s standards. It sets the rules of the house, checks that everyone is safe and keeps a team’s work sound. These values are not abstract morals but a felt sense of what nourishes people.

Each gate of the Spleen carries a fear in the tradition’s teaching, and this one concerns responsibility. It can show as taking charge of people who did not ask. Human Design reads this kindly, as a signal: care works best when it is wanted.

The tradition places gate 50 in the [[hd-circuit-defense|Defense circuit]], part of the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuit]] group. This circuit protects and nurtures those who cannot yet care for themselves.

## In practice

A gate is coloured when a planet stands in it at birth or in the Design, as [[hd-gate|Gate]] and [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]] explain.

- **By a Personality planet**, at birth: the person tends to know their values and can say them aloud.
- **By a Design planet**, about three months earlier: the values work as a bodily instinct. The person simply feels when something in a home or a team is off.
- **By both**: the theme is doubled, known and lived.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: without gate 27, the values look for energy to put them into practice. People with [[hd-gate-27|gate 27]], Caring, bring the strength to nurture.
- **Completed into a channel**: with gate 27, the [[hd-channel-50-27|channel of Preservation]] is defined, joining the Spleen to the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]]. It gives steady energy to care for others, guided by instinctive values. Caring for oneself belongs in that picture too.

When this is a chart’s only channel, Ulune finds a [[hd-generator|Generator]] with [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 50, *Ting*, places Fire above Wood. Its trigrams are *Li*, the Clinging, over *Sun*, the Gentle. Wilhelm titles it The Caldron: the six lines draw a bronze vessel, with legs, belly, handles and rings. Legge’s judgement reads simply: “Ting gives the intimation of great progress and success” (Legge, 1882). Turned upside down, it becomes hexagram 49, the image of [[hd-gate-49|gate 49]].
- **Astrology**: the gate spans 26°22′30″ [[libra|Libra]] to 2°00′00″ [[scorpio|Scorpio]].

## How Ulune calculates it

- Gate 41 opens the wheel at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of tropical longitude. Each gate then spans 5°37′30″ (5.625°) in a fixed order.
- Gate 50 is the 48th, from 26°22′30″ Libra to 2°00′00″ Scorpio (206.375° to 212°). Its six lines are 0°56′15″ wide and begin at 26°22′30″ Libra, 27°18′45″ Libra, 28°15′00″ Libra, 29°11′15″ Libra, 0°07′30″ Scorpio and 1°03′45″ Scorpio. The change of sign falls inside line 4.
- Ulune reads thirteen bodies at birth and again when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The Sun, the Earth, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto each colour the gate they stand in.
- When gate 27 is coloured too, at either moment, the channel 50–27 is defined. Ulune notes whether its gates come from the Personality, the Design or both.
- With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by the profile. Profiles 1/3 to 4/6 give the Right Angle Cross of Laws 3. Profile 4/1 gives the Juxtaposition Cross of Values. Profiles 5/1 to 6/3 give the Left Angle Cross of Wishes 2.
