## Meaning

Self-Projected authority is the [[hd-authority|authority]] of people whose [[hd-centre-g|G centre]] is defined and joined to the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The four centres that rank above it must be undefined: the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]], the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]] and the [[hd-centre-heart|Heart]]. It is found only in [[hd-projector|Projectors]], and it is rare.

In Human Design the G centre sits in the middle of the bodygraph, the system’s chart of nine centres. It holds identity, direction and love: the sense of who one is and where one’s life is heading. The Throat is the centre of speech and action. When the two are joined, the tradition says, the self speaks directly. Identity has a voice of its own.

That is how this authority decides. The truth of a choice is not felt in the gut or in a wave of emotion; it is heard. Talking a decision through, out loud, lets one hear what one says and how it sounds. Does the voice come alive on one option and go flat on the other? Does this direction sound like me? The tradition treats the voice as the instrument, and its tone and energy as the signal, more than the arguments it makes.

Other people matter here, but not as advisers. Human Design suggests choosing listeners who can hold a space without steering: a friend, a partner, a notebook, a voice memo. Their advice is not the point. Hearing oneself is. Because Projectors wait for the invitation in their [[hd-strategy|strategy]], the conversation often begins once an invitation has come, and is about whether to accept it.

## In practice

- **Two job offers.** Describing both to a friend, one may notice halfway through which one one talks about with more life. That shift is the answer the tradition points to.
- **Speaking to oneself.** Recording a short voice note about a choice and listening back can work when no one is around.
- **Walking and talking.** Many people with this authority find the words come more freely on the move than across a table.
- **Advice that misses.** Feeling unheard when others jump in with solutions is common. In the tradition’s reading that is a sign to ask for listening, not for advice.

## Origin and history

In Ra Uru Hu’s order, the G centre’s authority comes fifth, after the Heart. Teachers in his line describe four channels that can carry it, each giving its own flavour to the voice: [[hd-channel-8-1|1–8]], the channel of Inspiration; [[hd-channel-31-7|7–31]], the Alpha; [[hd-channel-33-13|13–33]], the Prodigal; and [[hd-channel-10-20|10–20]], Awakening.

## How Ulune calculates it

Rule 5 of Ulune’s order: when the Solar Plexus, the Sacral, the Spleen and the Heart are undefined, and both the G centre and the Throat are defined, the authority is Self-Projected. Ulune checks that the two centres are defined, not the channel between them. In such a chart, though, the G can only be defined through one of its four channels to the Throat. Its other six lead to the Sacral, the Spleen or the Heart, so the two are always joined.

No motor can reach the Throat in such a chart, so the type is always Projector. The Head and the Ajna may be defined as well; they do not change the authority. When the Heart is defined too, Ulune gives [[hd-authority-ego|Ego authority]] instead. Choosing the authority on the bodygraph lights the G centre. Without a birth time, Ulune marks the authority as one that could differ if it changes at another hour of that day.
