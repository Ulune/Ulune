## Meaning

A [[hd-profile|profile]] pairs two [[hd-line|lines]]. The first is that of the [[hd-layers|Personality]] Sun, at birth, which Human Design reads as conscious. The second is that of the Design Sun, about three months earlier, read as unconscious and often clearer to others. In the 6/2 the first is the [[hd-line-6|Role Model]], the second the [[hd-line-2|Hermit]]. Each is the line of the gate where that Sun stands, a hexagram of the I Ching counted from the bottom: lines 1 to 3 form its lower trigram, 4 to 6 its upper one.

Human Design teaches that the sixth line, at the top of the hexagram, lives in three phases: experiment until about 30, observation until about 50, then a life that embodies what was learned. The tradition times these turns by the [[saturn|Saturn]] return and the [[chiron|Chiron]] return. The second line carries natural talents that grow best in private and are often noticed by others first.

In the 6/2 the person consciously aims for perspective and authenticity, and unconsciously carries a gift that ripens away from view. The tradition describes an intense early phase of trying things and a long middle stretch of stepping back. Then comes a return, in which the gift is offered as an example. Others are often drawn to the talent long before the person names it.

The 6/2 belongs to the [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle]], which the tradition reads as a transpersonal destiny, a life that unfolds largely through meeting others. Its [[hd-cross|Incarnation Cross]] carries that angle.

## In practice

- A photographer shoots everything in her twenties, withdraws to a small town for years, and later returns with work others take as a reference.
- Friends seek out someone’s calm judgement long before that person sees themselves as a guide.
- A quieter middle stretch is, in this reading, observation, not a loss of ambition.
- Being called out of retreat can be welcome; Human Design suggests answering the calls that truly fit.

## Across traditions

In Richard Wilhelm’s I Ching, the top line often stands beyond a situation. The second is the central place of the lower trigram, frequently favourable. Lines in the same place of each trigram correspond; Human Design calls them harmonic. The 6 and the 2 are not a pair; their partners make the [[hd-profile-3-5|3/5]].

## How Ulune calculates it

The first number is the line of the Personality Sun, at birth; the second, the line of the Design Sun, when the Sun stood exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. From [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302° of tropical longitude, each [[hd-gate|gate]] spans 5.625° and each line 0.9375°. The Earth always shares the Sun’s line.

88° is about 93.87 lines. So the Design line usually falls two places after the Personality line, counting on from 6 to 1. It falls three places after only in the last 0.125° of a line. The 6/2 is one of the six common profiles, each about 14.4% of the zodiac, against 2.2% for each rare one. As the Sun moves, the profile steps through the twelve in a fixed order: [[hd-profile-5-2|5/2]], then the 6/2 for about 20 hours, then [[hd-profile-6-3|6/3]] for about three.

Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at noon and at every hour of that day. The Sun moves about a degree a day, more than a line, so the profile is always marked ~: another hour gives a neighbour, usually the 5/2 or the 6/3. The [[hd-cross|cross]] is marked too when its angle or gates could change.
