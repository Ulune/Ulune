## Meaning

The channel of Transitoriness gives a consistent emotional drive for new experiences, and the ability to act on it. Human Design reads it as a hunger to taste life widely: new places, new work, new people. Each experience passes, and the next one calls. Over a lifetime, that movement adds up to a broad knowledge of how things feel.

[[hd-gate-36|Gate 36, Crisis]], sits in the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], the centre of emotions, which moves in a wave. It brings the inexperience that seeks the new, and learns through the turbulence that follows. [[hd-gate-35|Gate 35, Change]], sits in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], the centre of speech and action. It wants to move on, and turns what was lived into practical knowledge worth passing on.

The Solar Plexus is a motor, so this channel joins a motor straight to the Throat. The tradition places it in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]]. That circuit learns by living through experiences, then looking back to share what they taught.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: both gates are coloured at birth. The person knows their restlessness well.
- **Design**, in red: both gates are coloured about three months before birth. Others may see the appetite for change before they do.
- **One gate from each layer**: the channel is just as defined; its halves come from two moments.
- **Gate 35 alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a wish for change and progress, often drawn to people who carry gate 36.
- **Gate 36 alone**: emotional curiosity for the new, which someone with gate 35 can turn into action.

Day to day, someone with this channel defined may change jobs, cities or passions several times. That breadth is the gift, not a lack of focus. Human Design advises waiting for emotional clarity before jumping in, since the wave can make a new start look brighter than it is. Many crises are avoided that way, and the ones that come still teach.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 35, Progress in Wilhelm’s version, places Fire over Earth, the sun rising over the land. Hexagram 36, Darkening of the Light, places Earth over Fire, the light gone below the ground. Each is the other turned upside down: advance and eclipse, the two sides of every new experience.
- **Astrology**: gate 35 spans 11°22′30″ to 17°00′00″ [[gemini|Gemini]], gate 36 spans 22°37′30″ to 28°15′00″ [[pisces|Pisces]].

## How Ulune calculates it

The channel is defined when gates 35 and 36 each hold at least one of the thirteen activations of the [[hd-layers|Personality or the Design]]. The Throat and the Solar Plexus then become defined.

As a chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-manifestor|Manifestor]]: a motor reaches the Throat and the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] is undefined. The strategy is to inform before acting. With the Solar Plexus defined, the authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]], and the [[hd-definition|definition]] single. In any chart, this channel makes the type Manifestor, or [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]] with a defined Sacral.

On the bodygraph, each half takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, red dashes over ink for both. A defined channel is drawn thicker and its centres are filled; a hanging gate colours only its half. Without a birth time, a channel another hour could change is drawn faded.
