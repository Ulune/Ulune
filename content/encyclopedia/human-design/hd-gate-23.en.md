## Meaning

Gate 23 sits in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat centre]], where Human Design places speech and action. Its theme is assimilation: putting an individual insight into simple words that others can take in. The insight comes from elsewhere; this gate is where it meets language.

The tradition places the gate in the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]], part of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuit group]]. Individual energy is read as mutative: it brings something new, in its own time, without asking the group’s permission. Here that newness has to be said out loud.

Human Design reads two sides to it. Explained clearly and at the right moment, an unusual point sounds original. Said too fast or too abstractly, the same point sounds odd. The gift lies less in many words than in the right few.

## In practice

A planet colours the gate either at birth, in the [[hd-layers|Personality]], or about three months before, in the Design.

- **Personality.** The theme tends to be felt as one’s own: the urge to simplify, and the frustration of not being understood.
- **Design.** The gate is read as working more out of sight, often noticed first by others.
- **Both.** The theme is marked twice and reads as especially steady.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]], it is half a channel. Its owner may feel most fluent near someone who carries gate 43.
- **Completed** by [[hd-gate-43|gate 43]], Insight, in the Ajna, it forms the [[hd-channel-43-23|channel of Structuring]]. The [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]] and the Throat are then defined, and original insights find a consistent voice. With this channel alone, the chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]]’s, with [[hd-authority-mental|mental authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 23, *Po*, is Splitting Apart in Richard Wilhelm’s version. Keeping Still, the Mountain, sits over the Receptive, the Earth: five broken lines under one unbroken line, like a roof about to give way. James Legge’s translation counsels that “it will not be advantageous to make a movement in any direction whatever” (1882).
- **Astrology.** The gate covers the middle of [[taurus|Taurus]], the fixed earth sign. The Sun crosses it for about six days in mid-May.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays a wheel of 64 gates over the tropical zodiac. The wheel starts with gate 41 at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of longitude. Each gate covers 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the order of the Human Design wheel. Gate 23 comes 20th, after gate 2 and before gate 8. It runs from **18°52′30″ to 24°30′00″ Taurus**.

Each of its six [[hd-line|lines]] spans 0°56′15″:

Thirteen bodies are read twice: at birth, and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. They are the Sun and the Earth, the Moon, the true North Node and the South Node, and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours the gate, with its line. With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by [[hd-profile|profile]]:

- from 1/3 to 4/6, the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle Cross of Explanation 2]];
- in 4/1, the [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition Cross of Assimilation]];
- from 5/1 to 6/3, the [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle Cross of Dedication 1]].
