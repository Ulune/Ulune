## Meaning

Gate 53, Beginnings, is one of the nine gates of the [[hd-centre-root|Root centre]], the centre of the pressure that gets things moving. Its theme is starting: the pressure to begin something new.

Human Design reads it as the spark that opens every cycle: a garden planned, a course begun, a business sketched on a napkin.

Its starts can outnumber its finishes, and the tradition does not read this as a failing. It suggests choosing beginnings with care, or teaming up with people who enjoy finishing.

The tradition places gate 53 in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], also called the Abstract circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit]] group. Gate 53 is where each of its experiences begins.

## In practice

A gate is coloured when a planet stands in it at birth or in the Design, as [[hd-gate|Gate]] and [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]] explain.

- **By a Personality planet**, at birth: the person tends to know their love of fresh starts, and can watch how many they take on.
- **By a Design planet**, about three months earlier: the pressure works from the body. A project may start before it is decided.
- **By both**: the theme is doubled, known and lived.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: without gate 42, the urge to begin looks for the energy to carry it through. People with [[hd-gate-42|gate 42]], Growth, bring the stamina to bring a cycle to its end.
- **Completed into a channel**: with gate 42, the [[hd-channel-42-53|channel of Maturation]] is defined, joining the Root to the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]]. It gives steady energy to start a cycle and see it through, gaining experience at each stage. Lives with it often unfold in clear chapters.

When this is a chart’s only channel, Ulune finds a [[hd-generator|Generator]] with [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral authority]]. Its strategy, to wait to respond, helps choose which cycles to enter.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 53, *Chien*, places the Wind, or Wood, above the Mountain. Its trigrams are *Sun*, the Gentle, over *Kên*, Keeping Still. Wilhelm titles it Development, or Gradual Progress: a tree on a mountain that grows slowly and holds firm. Its lines follow a wild goose drawing nearer, stage by stage. Legge reads it through a marriage made by the proper steps, “the marriage of a young lady” (Legge, 1882). Turned upside down, it becomes hexagram 54, the image of [[hd-gate-54|gate 54]].
- **Astrology**: the gate spans 15°07′30″ to 20°45′00″ [[cancer|Cancer]], the sign of nurturing what is newly born.

## How Ulune calculates it

- Gate 41 opens the wheel at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of tropical longitude. Each gate then spans 5°37′30″ (5.625°) in a fixed order.
- Gate 53 is the 30th, from 15°07′30″ to 20°45′00″ Cancer (105.125° to 110.75°). Its six lines are 0°56′15″ wide and begin at 15°07′30″, 16°03′45″, 17°00′00″, 17°56′15″, 18°52′30″ and 19°48′45″ Cancer.
- Ulune reads thirteen bodies at birth and again when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The Sun, the Earth, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto each colour the gate they stand in.
- When gate 42 is coloured too, at either moment, the channel 42–53 is defined. Ulune notes whether its gates come from the Personality, the Design or both.
- With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by the profile. Profiles 1/3 to 4/6 give the Right Angle Cross of Penetration 2. Profile 4/1 gives the Juxtaposition Cross of Beginnings. Profiles 5/1 to 6/3 give the Left Angle Cross of Cycles 1.
