## Meaning

The Logic circuit, also called the Understanding circuit, is one of the two halves of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]], the group whose keynote is sharing. Its theme is the pattern. It looks at how things repeat, doubts what does not hold, proposes a formula, tests it and corrects it. Then it shares the result so that everyone can face the future more safely.

The tradition reads it as the scientific side of human nature. It is oriented to the future: its question is what will work next time. Where the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]] learns by living first and understanding after, the Logic circuit wants to understand first, and then repeat what works.

Its seven [[hd-channel|channels]] form a path through the bodygraph:

- [[hd-channel-63-4|Logic]] (4–63), Head to Ajna: doubt raises a question, and the mind offers a formula to test.
- [[hd-channel-17-62|Acceptance]] (17–62), Ajna to Throat: an opinion turned into an organised, detailed explanation.
- [[hd-channel-16-48|The Wavelength]] (16–48), Spleen to Throat: depth of understanding joined to enthusiasm for practice, the talent that grows by repetition.
- [[hd-channel-18-58|Judgement]] (18–58), Spleen to Root: the drive to see what is wrong and improve it, fuelled by a joy in making things better.
- [[hd-channel-9-52|Concentration]] (9–52), Sacral to Root: the energy to focus on one thing and stay with it.
- [[hd-channel-15-5|Rhythm]] (5–15), G centre to Sacral: attunement to natural rhythms, and a pace others fall into.
- [[hd-channel-31-7|The Alpha]] (7–31), G centre to Throat: a voice for leadership that points a group towards the future, when it is recognised.

Several gates carry the circuit’s voice. [[hd-gate-63|Gate 63, Doubt]], starts the process, and [[hd-gate-4|gate 4, Formulisation]], answers it. [[hd-gate-17|Gate 17, Opinions]], and [[hd-gate-62|gate 62, Details]], put the answer into words. [[hd-gate-18|Gate 18, Correction]], and [[hd-gate-58|gate 58, Vitality]], keep improving the pattern. [[hd-gate-48|Gate 48, Depth]], and [[hd-gate-16|gate 16, Skills]], turn it into mastery through practice. [[hd-gate-7|Gate 7, The Role of the Self]], and [[hd-gate-31|gate 31, Influence]], lead others along it.

## In practice

- **Finding it.** Ulune draws the channels but does not group them by circuit. Any defined channel from the list above means Logic energy is consistent in the chart.
- **Doubt as a tool.** The tradition reads logical doubt as useful when it is aimed at patterns, plans and ideas. Turned on one’s own life decisions, it can become tiring. That is one reason Human Design looks for decisions in an inner [[hd-authority|authority]] rather than in reasoning alone.
- **Correction with care.** Logic sees what could be better, and says so. The tradition notes that criticism lands best when someone has asked for it.
- **Repetition.** Logic channels mature through practice. The Wavelength in particular is read as a talent that needs years of rehearsal before it shows its depth.
- **Leadership.** The Alpha channel leads towards a safer future, yet the tradition reads that leadership as working only when it is recognised or chosen.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Gate 63 is hexagram 63, which Wilhelm calls After Completion. A circuit that checks whether things still hold begins, fittingly, with the hexagram of order just reached and soon to be tested. Its partner in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]] is hexagram 64, Before Completion.
- **Science.** The circuit’s steps echo the scientific method: question, hypothesis, test, revision, shared result. The likeness is Human Design’s own reading, not a historical link.
