## Meaning

A [[hd-profile|profile]] pairs two [[hd-line|lines]]. The first is that of the [[hd-layers|Personality]] Sun, at birth, which Human Design reads as conscious. The second is that of the Design Sun, about three months earlier, read as unconscious and often clearer to others. In the 4/1 the first is the [[hd-line-4|Opportunist]], the second the [[hd-line-1|Investigator]]. Each is the line of the gate where that Sun stands, a hexagram of the I Ching counted from the bottom: lines 1 to 3 form its lower trigram, 4 to 6 its upper one.

Human Design teaches that the fourth line works through its network, its openings coming from people it already knows. The first line is the foundation of the hexagram: it needs to understand how something works before it feels secure, and it builds that ground through study.

In Human Design the 4/1 is the only profile of the [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]], an angle that stands between the personal and the transpersonal. The tradition describes a fixed path: a life that keeps to its own track and does not bend easily. The person consciously lives through a circle of close relationships, and unconsciously stands on a foundation that rarely moves. Their influence on others comes through that steadiness rather than through persuasion.

Lines 4 and 1 hold the same place in the upper and lower trigrams, the two halves of the hexagram, so they are in harmony. The 4/1 mirrors the [[hd-profile-1-4|1/4]], the same two lines in the other order. Its [[hd-cross|Incarnation Cross]] is always a Juxtaposition cross.

## In practice

- Someone keeps the same values and the same friends for decades, and their circle grows steadier around them.
- A craftsman who has worked the same way for thirty years is sought out for exactly that.
- Forcing a change of course rarely works for the 4/1; in this reading, change comes when the foundation itself shifts.
- Feeling out of step with a trend is often, for this profile, a sign of keeping to its own track, not of being wrong.

## Across traditions

Richard Wilhelm’s commentary on the I Ching sees the fourth line as the minister’s place and the first as the beginning. The two correspond, as lines in the same place of each trigram do, and Human Design calls them harmonic. The name Juxtaposition belongs to Human Design alone.

## How Ulune calculates it

The first number is the line of the Personality Sun, at birth; the second, the line of the Design Sun, when the Sun stood exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. From [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302° of tropical longitude, each [[hd-gate|gate]] spans 5.625° and each line 0.9375°. The Earth always shares the Sun’s line.

88° is about 93.87 lines. So the Design line usually falls two places after the Personality line, counting on from 6 to 1. It falls three places after only in the last 0.125° of a line. That last stretch, about three hours of the Sun’s motion, gives the 4/1. It is one of the six rare profiles, each about 2.2% of the zodiac against 14.4% for a common one. They are exactly the six whose lines form a harmonic pair. In the Sun’s order it comes between [[hd-profile-4-6|4/6]] and [[hd-profile-5-1|5/1]].

Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at noon and at every hour of that day. The Sun moves about a degree a day, more than a line, so the profile is always marked ~: another hour gives a neighbour, usually the 4/6 or the 5/1. Ulune names the profiles that day can give under the profile, as in “that day also 5/1”. The [[hd-cross|cross]] is marked too when its angle or gates could change.
