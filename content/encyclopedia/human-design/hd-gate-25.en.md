## Meaning

Gate 25 sits in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], the bodygraph’s centre of identity, love and direction. Its theme is the spirit of the self: an unconditional love that reaches out to all living things. Hard experiences put its innocence to the test.

The tradition places it in the [[hd-circuit-centering|Centering circuit]], one of the circuits of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual group]]. Centering energy is read as empowering: it helps a person become more fully themselves, and in doing so shows others that they can too.

The love of this gate is impersonal in the best sense. It does not choose one person over another; it extends to a stranger, an animal, a landscape. Human Design reads the test of innocence kindly. Disappointment is part of the path, and the gift is to keep trusting after being let down, without becoming naive.

## In practice

A planet colours the gate either at birth, in the [[hd-layers|Personality]], or about three months before, in the Design.

- **Personality.** The theme is usually felt as one’s own: a deep bond with animals or nature, and a readiness to trust.
- **Design.** The gate is read as working out of sight, more visible to others than to oneself.
- **Both.** The theme is marked twice and reads as especially steady.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]], it is half a channel. Its owner may feel a sharper spirit of adventure near someone with gate 51.
- **Completed** by [[hd-gate-51|gate 51]], Shock, in the [[hd-centre-heart|Heart]], it forms the [[hd-channel-25-51|channel of Initiation]]. The G centre and the Heart are then defined: a drive to be first and to leap into the unknown. With this channel alone, the chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]]’s, with [[hd-authority-ego|ego authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 25, *Wu Wang*, is Innocence, or the Unexpected, in Richard Wilhelm’s version. The Creative, Heaven, sits over the Arousing, the Thunder: movement from below, guided by what is above. Wilhelm reads it as acting without hidden motives, in harmony with heaven.
- **Astrology.** The gate straddles the last degrees of [[pisces|Pisces]] and the first of [[aries|Aries]]. The March equinox, 0° Aries, falls in its 2nd line, so the Sun stands here for a few days around 20 March.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays a wheel of 64 gates over the tropical zodiac. The wheel starts with gate 41 at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of longitude. Each gate covers 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the order of the Human Design wheel. Gate 25 comes 11th, after gate 36 and before gate 17. It runs from **28°15′00″ Pisces to 3°52′30″ Aries**.

Each of its six [[hd-line|lines]] spans 0°56′15″:

| Line | From | To |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 28°15′00″ Pisces | 29°11′15″ Pisces |
| 2 | 29°11′15″ Pisces | 0°07′30″ Aries |
| 3 | 0°07′30″ Aries | 1°03′45″ Aries |
| 4 | 1°03′45″ Aries | 2°00′00″ Aries |
| 5 | 2°00′00″ Aries | 2°56′15″ Aries |
| 6 | 2°56′15″ Aries | 3°52′30″ Aries |

Thirteen bodies are read twice: at birth, and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. They are the Sun and the Earth, the Moon, the true North Node and the South Node, and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours the gate, with its line. With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by [[hd-profile|profile]]:

- from 1/3 to 4/6, the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle Cross of the Vessel of Love 1]];
- in 4/1, the [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition Cross of Innocence]];
- from 5/1 to 6/3, the [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle Cross of Healing 1]].
