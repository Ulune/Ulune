## Meaning

A [[hd-profile|profile]] pairs two [[hd-line|lines]]. The first is that of the [[hd-layers|Personality]] Sun, at birth, which Human Design reads as conscious. The second is that of the Design Sun, about three months earlier, read as unconscious and often clearer to others. In the 5/2 the first is the [[hd-line-5|Heretic]], the second the [[hd-line-2|Hermit]]. Each is the line of the gate where that Sun stands, a hexagram of the I Ching counted from the bottom: lines 1 to 3 form its lower trigram, 4 to 6 its upper one.

Human Design teaches that the fifth line draws projection: others expect it to bring practical solutions, and its reputation follows what it delivers. The second line carries natural talents that grow best in private; it would often rather be left alone, and is drawn out by those who notice the gift.

In the 5/2 others project solutions onto the person, while the unconscious second line keeps longing for retreat. The talent is real, and often unnoticed by its owner; the calls come anyway. The tradition stresses selection. Accepting the calls that fit the natural gift, and letting the others pass, keeps both the retreat and the reputation intact.

Lines 5 and 2 hold the same place in the upper and lower trigrams, the two halves of the hexagram, so they are in harmony. The 5/2 belongs to the [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle]], which the tradition reads as a transpersonal destiny, a life that unfolds largely through meeting others. It mirrors the [[hd-profile-2-5|2/5]].

## In practice

- Someone who restores old bicycles at home is suddenly asked to run the village repair café.
- A private person shares one idea at a meeting, and the room starts to expect a leader.
- Time alone is, in this reading, where the talent that others call on is renewed.
- Turning down a call that does not fit is, for the tradition, a way of honouring the projection rather than refusing it.

## Across traditions

Richard Wilhelm’s commentary on the I Ching gives the fifth line the ruler’s place and the second the central place of the lower trigram. These two central lines correspond, and their bond often carries a hexagram’s meaning. Human Design calls them harmonic, and the 5/2 is built on that pair.

## How Ulune calculates it

The first number is the line of the Personality Sun, at birth; the second, the line of the Design Sun, when the Sun stood exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. From [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302° of tropical longitude, each [[hd-gate|gate]] spans 5.625° and each line 0.9375°. The Earth always shares the Sun’s line.

88° is about 93.87 lines. So the Design line usually falls two places after the Personality line, counting on from 6 to 1. It falls three places after only in the last 0.125° of a line. That last stretch, about three hours of the Sun’s motion, gives the 5/2. It is one of the six rare profiles, each about 2.2% of the zodiac against 14.4% for a common one. They are exactly the six whose lines form a harmonic pair. In the Sun’s order it comes between [[hd-profile-5-1|5/1]] and [[hd-profile-6-2|6/2]].

Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at noon and at every hour of that day. The Sun moves about a degree a day, more than a line, so the profile is always marked ~: another hour gives a neighbour, usually the 5/1 or the 6/2. Ulune names the profiles that day can give under the profile, as in “that day also 6/2”. The [[hd-cross|cross]] is marked too when its angle or gates could change.
