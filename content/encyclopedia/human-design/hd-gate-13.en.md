## Meaning

Gate 13 is one of the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] of the bodygraph, and Ulune calls it the Listener. It sits in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], which Human Design reads as the seat of identity, love and direction. Its theme is listening: it collects other people’s experiences and secrets, and keeps them.

Strangers may tell its owner their life story on a train. Colleagues confide in them without quite knowing why. What it hears is held and understood over time, so discretion is part of the gift. The tradition reads this gate as a memory for what people have lived, waiting for the right moment to become a lesson.

The tradition places gate 13 in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], also called the Abstract circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective]] circuitry. Human Design links that circuit with experience: living something through, then looking back to share what it taught.

## In practice

- **Personality** (the planets at birth, drawn in ink): the gate is conscious. Its owner tends to know that people open up to them. See [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]].
- **Design** (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red): the gate works below awareness. Confidences arrive, and its owner may wonder why they always end up hearing them.
- **Both**: the listening is coloured twice, and tends to be a steady trait.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the stories are gathered, and the gate reaches for gate 33, its partner across the channel. People who carry gate 33 can help turn what was heard into something told.
- **Completed** by gate 33 in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], it forms the channel of [[hd-channel-33-13|the Prodigal]]. Ulune reads it as a consistent witness. Such a person absorbs experiences, retreats to digest them, then tells what they learned.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 13, *Tong Ren*, places heaven above fire, the flame rising towards the sky. Wilhelm called it Fellowship with Men. His judgment opens: “Fellowship with Men in the open. Success.” His commentary ties true fellowship to aims that concern everyone, not private interest.
- **Astrology**: the gate falls in the middle of [[aquarius|Aquarius]], the sign of friendship, groups and shared ideals.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the gates on the tropical zodiac, from gate 41 at 302° (2° Aquarius), each 5°37′30″ wide. Gate 13 is the 3rd gate of that wheel, between gate 19 and gate 49. It covers 13°15′00″ to 18°52′30″ Aquarius. Its six [[hd-line|lines]] of 0°56′15″ begin at 13°15′00″, 14°11′15″, 15°07′30″, 16°03′45″, 17°00′00″ and 17°56′15″.

Ulune places thirteen bodies on the wheel: the Sun and the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes, and Mercury to Pluto. It does so at birth and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. Any of these 26 positions in the span colours the gate. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon. Ulune then flags any body whose gate or line could change at another hour.

When the Personality Sun stands here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] after gate 13. The [[hd-profile|profile]] sets its angle: [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]] or [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle]].
