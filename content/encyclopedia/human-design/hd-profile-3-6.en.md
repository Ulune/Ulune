## Meaning

A [[hd-profile|profile]] pairs two [[hd-line|lines]]. The first is that of the [[hd-layers|Personality]] Sun, at birth, which Human Design reads as conscious. The second is that of the Design Sun, about three months earlier, read as unconscious and often clearer to others. In the 3/6 the first is the [[hd-line-3|Martyr]], the second the [[hd-line-6|Role Model]]. Each is the line of the gate where that Sun stands, a hexagram of the I Ching counted from the bottom: lines 1 to 3 form its lower trigram, 4 to 6 its upper one.

Human Design teaches that the third line learns through trial and error, finding what works by meeting what does not. The sixth line, at the top of the hexagram, lives in three phases. Until about 30 it experiments much like a third line; until about 50 it steps back to observe; afterwards it embodies what it learned, as an example for others. The tradition times these turns by the [[saturn|Saturn]] return and the [[chiron|Chiron]] return.

In the 3/6 trial and error comes twice: as the conscious method and as the first phase of the unconscious sixth line. Early life can feel eventful, with jobs, places or relationships tried and left. The later phases bring distance, then a quiet authority built on what was lived. Human Design reads the early turbulence as the raw material of that later perspective, not as a sign of going astray.

Lines 3 and 6 hold the same place in the lower and upper trigrams, the two halves of the hexagram, so they are in harmony. The 3/6 is the last profile of the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], which the tradition reads as a personal destiny, lived mostly through one’s own process. It mirrors the [[hd-profile-6-3|6/3]].

## In practice

- Someone changes direction several times in their twenties, then spends years watching from a quieter post.
- Later, the same person helps others through the mistakes they once made, with both experience and distance.
- A restless youth is, in this reading, research, not failure.
- A pause in mid-life is read as observation, not as standing still.

## Across traditions

In Richard Wilhelm’s commentary on the I Ching, the top line often stands at the end of a situation, or beyond it. In the first hexagram it is the dragon that rose too high: “Arrogant dragon will have cause to repent” (Wilhelm, in Cary F. Baynes’s translation). Human Design gives the place a gentler role, the observer who becomes an example.

## How Ulune calculates it

The first number is the line of the Personality Sun, at birth; the second, the line of the Design Sun, when the Sun stood exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. From [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302° of tropical longitude, each [[hd-gate|gate]] spans 5.625° and each line 0.9375°. The Earth always shares the Sun’s line.

88° is about 93.87 lines. So the Design line usually falls two places after the Personality line, counting on from 6 to 1. It falls three places after only in the last 0.125° of a line. That last stretch, about three hours of the Sun’s motion, gives the 3/6. It is one of the six rare profiles, each about 2.2% of the zodiac against 14.4% for a common one. They are exactly the six whose lines form a harmonic pair. In the Sun’s order it comes between [[hd-profile-3-5|3/5]] and [[hd-profile-4-6|4/6]].

Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at noon and at every hour of that day. The Sun moves about a degree a day, more than a line, so the profile is always marked ~: another hour gives a neighbour, usually the 3/5 or the 4/6. Ulune names the profiles that day can give under the profile, as in “that day also 4/6”. The [[hd-cross|cross]] is marked too when its angle or gates could change.
