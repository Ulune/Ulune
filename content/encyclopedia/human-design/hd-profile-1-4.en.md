## Meaning

A [[hd-profile|profile]] pairs two [[hd-line|lines]]. The first is that of the [[hd-layers|Personality]] Sun, at birth, which Human Design reads as conscious. The second is that of the Design Sun, about three months earlier, read as unconscious and often clearer to others. In the 1/4 the first is the [[hd-line-1|Investigator]], the second the [[hd-line-4|Opportunist]]. Each is the line of the gate where that Sun stands, a hexagram of the I Ching counted from the bottom: lines 1 to 3 form its lower trigram, 4 to 6 its upper one.

Human Design teaches that the first line needs a foundation. It researches and checks until it understands how something works, and only then feels secure. The fourth line opens the upper half of the hexagram, the half turned toward others. It works through its network: its chances come from people it already knows, and its influence travels through friendship rather than through strangers. The word opportunist is not meant as a judgement here; it names the openings a circle brings.

In the 1/4 the person consciously builds a body of knowledge, and that knowledge spreads, often without a plan, through the people around them. A former colleague passes on a name, a friend asks for advice, and a quiet expertise turns into an opening. The tradition reads the quality of the relationships as part of the work: the right circle carries the foundation further than any effort to reach strangers.

Lines 1 and 4 hold the same place in the lower and upper trigrams. The 1/4 is therefore one of the few profiles whose lines are in harmony. It belongs to the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], which the tradition reads as a personal destiny, lived mostly through one’s own process. It mirrors the [[hd-profile-4-1|4/1]], the same two lines in the other order.

## In practice

- A translator who knows one field in depth gets most of her work from people who have already worked with her.
- A researcher’s findings travel first through a few trusted friends before anyone else hears of them.
- Sharing what one knows inside one’s own circle is, in this reading, how the foundation becomes useful.
- When a long friendship ends, the ground can feel less steady. Human Design reads that unease as a signal about the network, not as a weakness.

## Across traditions

In Richard Wilhelm’s I Ching, the first line is the beginning, still below the surface; the fourth is the place close to the ruler. Lines in the same place of each trigram correspond; Human Design calls them harmonic, and the 1/4 is built on such a pair.

## How Ulune calculates it

The first number is the line of the Personality Sun, at birth; the second, the line of the Design Sun, when the Sun stood exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. From [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302° of tropical longitude, each [[hd-gate|gate]] spans 5.625° and each line 0.9375°. The Earth always shares the Sun’s line.

88° is about 93.87 lines. So the Design line usually falls two places after the Personality line, counting on from 6 to 1. It falls three places after only in the last 0.125° of a line. That last stretch, about three hours of the Sun’s motion, gives the 1/4. It is one of the six rare profiles, each about 2.2% of the zodiac against 14.4% for a common one. They are exactly the six whose lines form a harmonic pair. In the Sun’s order it comes between [[hd-profile-1-3|1/3]] and [[hd-profile-2-4|2/4]].

Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at noon and at every hour of that day. The Sun moves about a degree a day, more than a line, so the profile is always marked ~: another hour gives a neighbour, usually the 1/3 or the 2/4. Ulune names the profiles that day can give under the profile, as in “that day also 2/4”. The [[hd-cross|cross]] is marked too when its angle or gates could change.
