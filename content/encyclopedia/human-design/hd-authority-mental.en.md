## Meaning

Mental authority is Ulune’s name for the [[hd-authority|authority]] of [[hd-projector|Projectors]] defined only at the top of the bodygraph, the system’s chart of nine centres. Their definition lies in the [[hd-centre-head|Head]], the [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]] and the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], and nowhere else. The tradition also calls it environmental authority, or simply says there is no inner authority. People with this design are often called Mental Projectors.

The word “mental” names where the definition sits, not who decides. Human Design holds that the Ajna, the awareness centre of the mind, is very good at thinking, sorting and explaining, but is not built to make life decisions. With no defined centre below the Throat, there is no inner signal such as a gut response, an emotional wave or a splenic instinct to lean on. Clarity therefore comes from outside.

The tradition points to two sources. The first is the environment: the right place, the right room, the right company. People with this design are said to be sensitive to surroundings, and they often think much more clearly in one setting than another. The second is a circle of trusted listeners, sometimes called sounding boards. Talking with them is not about collecting opinions. It is a way to hear one’s own thinking from outside, see the whole picture, and notice what one keeps coming back to.

This takes time, and several conversations rather than one. Because these are Projectors, the [[hd-strategy|strategy]] is to wait for the invitation; the authority then guides how an invitation is weighed. The term “environmental” here should not be confused with the [[hd-arrow-environment|Environment arrow]] of the [[hd-variable|Variable]], a separate layer of the chart.

## In practice

- **A favourite place.** Noticing that one thinks more clearly about a big decision in one particular café, garden or room, and going there on purpose to think it through.
- **Several listeners.** Talking the same choice over with two or three trusted people, on different days, and noticing what one says each time.
- **Not deciding alone in the head.** Long nights of pros-and-cons lists tend to loop. Speaking the lists aloud to someone usually moves things on.
- **A sense of the setting.** If a place feels wrong, the tradition suggests taking that seriously; a decision reached there may feel wrong as well.

## Origin and history

In Ra Uru Hu’s teaching, Projectors defined only above the Throat are described as having no inner authority. They are asked to rely on their environment and on the people around them. Sources in his line rank this case after the G centre’s authority, and before the Reflector’s lunar cycle.

## How Ulune calculates it

Mental authority is Ulune’s last rule: when none of the rules above applies and the type is not Reflector, the authority is Mental. In practice that means no defined Solar Plexus, Sacral, Spleen or Heart, and no defined G centre, since a G defined with the Throat gives [[hd-authority-self-projected|Self-Projected authority]]. What remains is definition among the Head, the Ajna and the Throat, through the channels [[hd-channel-64-47|64–47]], [[hd-channel-61-24|61–24]] and [[hd-channel-63-4|63–4]] between Head and Ajna, and [[hd-channel-17-62|17–62]], [[hd-channel-43-23|43–23]] and [[hd-channel-11-56|11–56]] between Ajna and Throat.

No motor is defined, so the type is always Projector. This authority has no seat in Ulune: choosing it on the bodygraph lights no centre. Without a birth time, Ulune marks the authority as one that could differ if it changes at another hour of that day.
