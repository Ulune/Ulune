## Meaning

The Sacral is the square in the lower middle of the bodygraph, below the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]]. Human Design calls it the most powerful motor in the chart. It is the life force: the energy for sustained work, for sexuality and for fertility. It renews itself when it is used for what it truly responds to.

The Sacral does not speak through thought. It answers through the gut, with an immediate inner yes or no. The tradition says it often shows as a sound, a low “uh-huh” for yes and “uhn-uhn” for no, before the mind has formed an opinion.

It holds nine gates, as many as the [[hd-centre-root|Root]] and fewer only than the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. Among them, [[hd-gate-34|gate 34]] is raw power and [[hd-gate-5|gate 5]] fixed rhythms. [[hd-gate-14|Gate 14]] is the skill to use resources, [[hd-gate-29|gate 29]] the capacity to say yes and persevere. [[hd-gate-59|Gate 59]] carries sexuality and intimacy, [[hd-gate-27|gate 27]] caring, [[hd-gate-9|gate 9]] focus, [[hd-gate-3|gate 3]] ordering, [[hd-gate-42|gate 42]] growth through completing cycles.

Defined, the Sacral gives renewable energy for work one responds to, and clear answers from the gut. That energy runs down when it goes into things one did not really want. Human Design names frustration as the signal of that drift, and satisfaction as the sign of energy well used.

Undefined or open, the Sacral gives no steady work energy of its own. Around people with a defined Sacral one can work very hard, then crash. The key skill the tradition offers is knowing when enough is enough. Resting before exhaustion, and resting away from others, lets the borrowed energy drain off.

## In practice

- **Type**: a defined Sacral makes a [[hd-generator|Generator]]. With a motor also joined to the Throat, it makes a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]]. Every other type has an undefined Sacral.
- **Strategy**: both Sacral types are advised to wait to respond, letting life bring something to answer, as the page on [[hd-strategy|strategy]] explains.
- **Authority**: when the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]] is undefined, the Sacral gives [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral authority]]: deciding in the moment, from the first gut response. A yes-or-no question asked by a friend can help it speak.
- **Undefined, with active gates**: a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]] here shows where another person’s life force is felt most, such as sexuality with gate 59.
- **The Throat link**: the channel [[hd-channel-34-20|34–20]] joins the Sacral straight to the Throat. It is one of the clearest Manifesting Generator signatures.

## Across traditions

- **The chakras**: Human Design says the Sacral derives from Svadhisthana, the sacral chakra of yoga, whose name is often read as one’s own seat. In yoga it is tied to water, desire and creation. The Sacral keeps that generative force and gives its emotions to the Solar Plexus.
- **Life force elsewhere**: Daoist inner alchemy and qigong place a store of vital energy below the navel, the lower dantian. The comparison is a likeness of place and theme, not a shared doctrine.

## Origin and history

Ra Uru Hu presented the nine centres from 1987 as a change from the seven chakras, dated to 1781. By the tradition’s count, Generators and Manifesting Generators together are most people, so the Sacral’s way of answering shapes much of the system’s everyday advice. The tradition links the Sacral to the ovaries and the testes. That is a correspondence the system teaches, never a medical claim.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune colours a gate when any of the 26 activations stands in it, the 13 bodies of the Personality and the 13 of the Design. A channel is defined when both of its gates are coloured, and a centre is defined when a defined channel touches it.

The Sacral is defined exactly when one of its eleven channels is defined. Otherwise it is undefined when any of its nine gates is coloured, open when none is. For type, a defined Sacral gives Generator, or Manifesting Generator when a walk from the Throat along defined channels reaches any motor. For authority, Ulune gives sacral authority to a defined Sacral when the Solar Plexus is undefined.
