## Meaning

Channel 64–47 is one of the 36 [[hd-channel|channels]] of the bodygraph, and Ulune calls it the channel of Abstraction. It runs from [[hd-gate-64|gate 64]], Confusion, in the [[hd-centre-head|Head]] down to [[hd-gate-47|gate 47]], Realisation, in the [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]]. It is one of the three channels of the mind, the only paths between those two centres.

Its theme is a consistent way of thinking about the past. The Head presses with images and memories that do not yet make sense. The Ajna sorts them and slowly draws out a meaning. The result is a story: what happened, and what it taught.

Human Design treats the confusion as part of the process, not as a failure. Someone with this channel defined may feel muddled for weeks after a move or a breakup. Then, often while doing something else, the pieces fall into place.

The tradition places the channel in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], also called the Abstract circuit, one of the two [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective]] circuits. Sensing channels learn through experience and look back on it. What they understand is meant to be shared, so that others can learn from a life already lived.

## In practice

- **Defined by the Personality** (both gates from the planets at birth): the reflecting is conscious. Its owner knows they need to go over things to understand them. See [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]].
- **Defined by the Design** (both gates from the planets about three months earlier): the sorting runs below awareness. Others may notice the lessons before its owner does.
- **Defined by both, or by one gate from each layer**: the channel is just as defined. Only its colours change.
- **Gate 64 alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the images press, waiting for a gate 47 in someone else.
- **Gate 47 alone**: the gift of realisation, working on the images other people bring.
- **Day to day**: a love of looking back, of journals, photo albums and long talks about what an event meant. The tradition adds a kind caution: this mind is for understanding, not for deciding.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 64, *Wei Ji*, places fire above water, and Wilhelm called it Before Completion. Hexagram 47, *K’un*, places the lake above water, and Wilhelm called it Oppression, or Exhaustion. Both rest on water, and both describe a state not yet resolved.
- **Astrology**: the two gates sit side by side in [[virgo|Virgo]], gate 64 from 11°22′30″ to 17°00′00″, gate 47 from there to 22°37′30″. Virgo is the sign of sorting and careful review.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune places thirteen bodies at birth, the Personality, and thirteen at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 64 and gate 47 each hold at least one of these 26 activations, from either layer. The Head and the Ajna then become defined.

The gates are neighbours on the wheel, so the Sun crosses both in about eleven days. Mercury, never far from the Sun, can easily stand in the other.

If this were the chart’s only channel, Ulune would find no defined [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] and no motor joined to the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The type would be [[hd-projector|Projector]], with [[hd-authority-mental|mental authority]].

On the bodygraph, each half of the line takes its gate’s colour. Ink means the Personality, red the Design, ink dashed with red both. A defined channel is drawn thicker, and its centres are filled gold. The Personality and Design views keep one layer each, so a channel made of one gate from each vanishes there. Without a birth time, a channel that another hour could change is drawn faint.
