## Meaning

The channel of Rhythm makes a person consistently attuned to natural rhythms, and able to set a pace others fall into. Human Design reads it as being in the flow. The timing of a day, a season or a life is felt in the body rather than worked out. Where such a person keeps their rhythm, those around them often find theirs.

[[hd-gate-15|Gate 15, Extremes]], sits in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], the centre of identity, direction and love. It carries a love of humanity that welcomes every rhythm, even the extreme ones. [[hd-gate-5|Gate 5, Fixed Rhythms]], sits in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]], the strongest motor of the bodygraph. It holds the habits and timing that repeat, day after day.

The tradition places the channel in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]]. That circuit looks for reliable patterns and tests them, so that they can be shared and trusted. Here the pattern is a rhythm: a steady way of living that others can lean on.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: both gates are coloured at birth. The person knows their need for routine.
- **Design**, in red: both gates are coloured about three months before birth. Others may feel the steadying pace before the person notices it.
- **One gate from each layer**: the channel is just as defined; its halves come from two moments.
- **Gate 15 alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a wide range of moods and habits, which can settle around people who carry gate 5.
- **Gate 5 alone**: a fixed personal rhythm, which someone with gate 15 can open to the rhythms of others.

Day to day, someone with this channel defined often keeps regular routines that ground a family or a team. Meals, sleep and work tend to find their set hours. Disrupted schedules, night shifts or constant travel can throw them off more than most. Human Design reads that sensitivity as part of the gift, not a weakness: protecting the rhythm protects what it gives.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 15, Modesty in Wilhelm’s version, places Earth over Mountain, the high hidden under the low. Its image speaks of evening out what is too much and too little. Hexagram 5, Waiting, places Water over Heaven, clouds gathering until the rain comes in its time. Balance and timing: the two halves of the channel.
- **Astrology**: gate 15 spans 28°15′00″ [[gemini|Gemini]] to 3°52′30″ [[cancer|Cancer]], across the June solstice. Gate 5 spans 11°22′30″ to 17°00′00″ [[sagittarius|Sagittarius]].

## How Ulune calculates it

The channel is defined when gates 15 and 5 each hold at least one of the thirteen activations of the [[hd-layers|Personality or the Design]]. The G centre and the Sacral then become defined.

As a chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-generator|Generator]]: the Sacral is defined, but no motor reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The strategy is to wait to respond. The authority is [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral]], and the [[hd-definition|definition]] single. In any chart, this channel makes the type Generator, or [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]] when a motor also reaches the Throat.

On the bodygraph, each half takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, red dashes over ink for both. A defined channel is drawn thicker and its centres are filled; a hanging gate colours only its half. Without a birth time, a channel another hour could change is drawn faded.
