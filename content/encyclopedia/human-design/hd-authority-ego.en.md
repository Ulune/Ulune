## Meaning

Ego authority is the [[hd-authority|authority]] of people whose [[hd-centre-heart|Heart]] centre, also called the Ego, is defined, while the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] and the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]] are not. It is one of the rarest authorities. It belongs to two types: [[hd-manifestor|Manifestors]] and [[hd-projector|Projectors]].

In Human Design the Heart is the motor of willpower in the bodygraph, the system’s chart of nine centres. It makes promises and keeps them. It carries the drive to earn, to own and to prove one’s worth, and it is tied to the material world: work, money, and what one can offer others. Like other motors, the tradition says, it works in cycles of effort and rest.

Deciding with the Heart means deciding from what one truly wants and is willing to commit to. The tradition invites an honest question about one’s own interest: is this worth my effort, and what will it bring me? Human Design does not read that question as selfish. A promise made from real desire is one the Heart can keep; a promise made to please, or to prove something, tends to drain it.

The tradition names two forms of this authority. **Ego manifested** belongs to Manifestors whose Heart reaches the Throat. The decision tends to be heard as it is said, in the same breath, so listening to one’s own spontaneous words matters. **Ego projected** belongs to Projectors whose Heart joins the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]] but does not reach the Throat. Here, the tradition suggests waiting for the invitation, then talking it through and hearing what the will really wants.

## In practice

- **What one keeps saying.** Noticing that one keeps saying “I want to go back to studying” is the kind of signal this authority trusts, more than what one thinks one should do.
- **Before promising.** A short pause before agreeing helps: is this a real “I want”, or a way to impress? Human Design reads the urge to prove one’s worth as a [[hd-not-self|not-self]] signal, never a fault.
- **Fair terms.** Naming a price, a salary or a deadline that truly suits is part of deciding from the Heart, not a detour from it.
- **Rest built in.** Since the Heart works in cycles, commitments made with rest included are easier to keep.

## Origin and history

In Ra Uru Hu’s order of authorities, the Heart comes fourth, after the Solar Plexus, the Sacral and the Spleen. Teachers in his line tell the manifested and projected forms apart by the channel that defines the Heart. It is either the channel of Money, [[hd-channel-45-21|21–45]], to the Throat, or the channel of Initiation, [[hd-channel-25-51|25–51]], to the G centre.

## How Ulune calculates it

Rule 4 of Ulune’s order: when the Solar Plexus, the Sacral and the Spleen are undefined and the Heart is defined, the authority is Ego. The Heart has four channels. Two of them define a higher centre too: [[hd-channel-44-26|44–26]] the Spleen, so the authority is Splenic, and [[hd-channel-40-37|40–37]] the Solar Plexus, so it is Emotional. Ego authority therefore comes from 21–45 or 25–51.

Ulune shows a single label, Ego, and does not tell the manifested and projected forms apart; the chart shows which one applies. With 21–45, a motor reaches the Throat and the type is Manifestor. With 25–51 alone, the type is Projector, unless the G centre also reaches the Throat; then the Heart reaches it through the G, and the type is Manifestor. The Heart outranks the G, so such a chart reads Ego, never [[hd-authority-self-projected|Self-Projected]].

Choosing the authority on the bodygraph lights the Heart. Without a birth time, Ulune marks the authority as one that could differ if it changes at another hour of that day.
