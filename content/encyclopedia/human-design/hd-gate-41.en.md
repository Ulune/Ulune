## Meaning

Gate 41 is the gate of Contraction. Human Design reads it as the pressure of imagination: the daydream of another life or a long journey. That fantasy is not an escape. It is a seed, often the first step towards trying something new.

The gate is where the wheel begins. When the Sun reaches it each year, at 2° Aquarius, Human Design starts its year. Gate 41 is therefore read as a beginning, a pressure that opens cycles of experience.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-root|Root centre]], the bodygraph’s pressure centre, which the tradition associates with the adrenal glands. It belongs to the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]] of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]], the circuit of learning by living. Gate 41 gives the first push: the wish for an experience not yet had.

## In practice

- **Personality or Design.** A Personality planet (in ink on Ulune’s bodygraph) makes the rich inner life known and enjoyed. A Design planet (in red) may show it as a restlessness others notice before it has a picture. In both layers, the theme is doubly present. See [[hd-layers]].
- **Hanging.** Coloured without gate 30, gate 41 is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the imagination lacks a steady desire to carry it. Someone with gate 30 can complete it for a while, turning a shared dream into a wish to act.
- **With gate 30**, it makes the [[hd-channel-41-30|channel of Recognition]], joining the Root to the Solar Plexus. It gives steady pressure to imagine new experiences and to desire them strongly.
- That channel defines the Solar Plexus, so the authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]]. Letting feelings settle helps tell a lasting desire from a passing fantasy. With this channel alone, a chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 41, sǔn, is Mountain (gen) above Lake (dui): the lake below gives of itself to the mountain. Wilhelm calls it Decrease. Legge’s judgment notes that, with sincerity, even “two baskets of grain” make a fit offering.
- **Astrology**: the gate begins two degrees into [[aquarius|Aquarius]], a sign read as vision and the future. In most years the Sun crosses it from about 22 to 27 January.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune’s gate wheel starts here, at 2°00′00″ Aquarius (302° of tropical longitude). The other 63 gates follow in the order of the Rave I Ching, each 5°37′30″ (5.625°) wide. Gate 41 is the 1st on the wheel: gate 60 closes the circle before it, and gate 19 comes next. It spans **2°00′00″ to 7°37′30″ Aquarius** (302° to 307.625°).

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, line 2 at 2°56′15″ and line 3 at 3°52′30″. Line 4 starts at 4°48′45″, line 5 at 5°45′00″ and line 6 at 6°41′15″. A longitude exactly on a boundary belongs to the gate or line that begins there.

Ulune places thirteen bodies twice: at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. They are the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours gate 41.
