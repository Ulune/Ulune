## Meaning

The channel of Judgement links the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]] at [[hd-gate-18|gate 18]] to the [[hd-centre-root|Root]] at [[hd-gate-58|gate 58]]. Ulune reads it as a consistent drive to see what is wrong and improve it, fuelled by a real joy in making things better.

Gate 58, Vitality, is the Root’s pressure of aliveness: the pleasure of seeing how something could be livelier, fairer or more fit. Gate 18, Correction, is the Spleen’s instinct for what has gone wrong and needs repair. Together they make criticism that is not sour. It comes from love of the thing being improved.

The Root is both a pressure centre and a motor, the source of the adrenaline that gets life moving. The Spleen is the centre of instinct and of the body’s sense of what is healthy; Human Design associates it with the immune system. Here the Root’s zest meets the Spleen’s eye for the flaw.

The tradition places this channel in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]]. With the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], Logic makes up the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]], the channels of sharing with the wider group. Logic works by patterns, testing and correction over time. In that light, the judgement of 18–58 is meant for the common good: a skill, a system or a society improved for everyone.

## In practice

- **Personality, Design or both.** Defined by the Personality (the planets at birth, drawn in ink), the eye for flaws tends to be known and owned. Defined by the Design (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red), it works more bodily, and others may feel it before its owner names it. With one gate in each [[hd-layers|layer]], it is just as defined.
- **One gate alone.** Gate 58 alone is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: zest for life, looking for something to improve. Gate 18 alone spots what needs correcting, without the steady energy to keep at it.
- **Day to day.** Someone with this channel is often an excellent editor, tester or coach.
- **Invited critique.** Ulune’s reading notes that critique offered where it was invited helps; offered everywhere, it strains relationships. The tradition reads others’ irritation as a signal about timing, not about the worth of the insight.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Gate 18 is hexagram 18, which Wilhelm calls Work on What Has Been Spoiled: the Mountain over Wind, a stale situation that calls for repair. Gate 58 is hexagram 58, The Joyous: the Lake doubled, two lakes that feed each other. Repair and joy, side by side, are the two halves of the channel.
- **Astrology.** Gate 18 spans 3°52′30″ to 9°30′00″ [[libra|Libra]], the sign of fair measure. Gate 58 covers the same degrees of [[capricorn|Capricorn]], 3°52′30″ to 9°30′00″.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune casts 26 activations: 13 bodies at birth for the Personality, 13 for the Design, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 18 and gate 58 each hold at least one, from either layer. The Spleen and the Root then become defined.

Run as the chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-projector|Projector]]. The Root is a motor, but nothing joins it to the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], and the Sacral stays undefined. The authority is [[hd-authority-splenic|splenic]], and the definition single.

On the bodygraph, each half of the channel takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, ink dashed with red for both. Defined, the line is drawn thicker than a hanging half, and the two centres are filled in gold. In the Personality or Design view, only that layer’s bodies count.
