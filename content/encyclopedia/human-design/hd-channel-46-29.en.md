## Meaning

The channel of Discovery gives consistent energy to commit fully to an experience, and to discover things along the way. Human Design reads it as the power to see something through. Once such a person has said yes, they keep going, and the meaning of the experience often shows only at the end.

[[hd-gate-46|Gate 46, Determination of the Self]], sits in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], the centre of identity, direction and love. It carries love for the body and the knack of being in the right place at the right time. [[hd-gate-29|Gate 29, Saying Yes]], sits in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]], the strongest motor of the bodygraph. It is the energy of commitment, persevering through whatever follows.

The tradition places the channel in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]]. That circuit learns by living through experiences, then looking back on them to share what they taught. Discovery is where the experience is entered, body and soul.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: both gates are coloured at birth. The person knows their staying power.
- **Design**, in red: both gates are coloured about three months before birth. Others may count on this person’s perseverance before they notice it.
- **One gate from each layer**: the channel is just as defined; its halves come from two moments.
- **Gate 29 alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a readiness to say yes, often drawn to people who carry gate 46.
- **Gate 46 alone**: a gift for being in the right place, which someone with gate 29 can fill with commitment.

Day to day, someone with this channel defined may succeed where others gave up, simply because they keep going once committed. The key, Human Design says, is committing to the right things. They will follow through either way. The Sacral’s response, a gut yes or no, is how the tradition suggests choosing what to enter.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 46, Pushing Upward in Wilhelm’s version, places Earth over Wind, a tree growing out of the soil, slowly and without strain. Hexagram 29, The Abysmal, doubles Water: danger upon danger, a gorge that water fills and flows on through. Steady growth and perseverance: the two halves of the channel.
- **Astrology**: gate 46 spans 28°15′00″ [[virgo|Virgo]] to 3°52′30″ [[libra|Libra]], across the September equinox. Gate 29 spans 24°30′00″ [[leo|Leo]] to 0°07′30″ Virgo.

## How Ulune calculates it

The channel is defined when gates 46 and 29 each hold at least one of the thirteen activations of the [[hd-layers|Personality or the Design]]. The G centre and the Sacral then become defined.

As a chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-generator|Generator]]: the Sacral is defined, but no motor reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The strategy is to wait to respond. The authority is [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral]], and the [[hd-definition|definition]] single. In any chart, this channel makes the type Generator, or [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]] when a motor also reaches the Throat.

On the bodygraph, each half takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, red dashes over ink for both. A defined channel is drawn thicker and its centres are filled; a hanging gate colours only its half. Without a birth time, a channel another hour could change is drawn faded.
