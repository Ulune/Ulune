## Meaning

Gate 3 is the gate of Ordering. It is the energy that brings order to something new and still chaotic. Human Design reads it as working in bursts rather than steadily: setup after setup is tried on a messy process, nothing seems to move for a while, then the arrangement that works appears at once.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral centre]], the motor of life force and of the energy to work, which in Human Design answers what life brings rather than starting from nothing. The tradition places gate 3 in the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuit group]]. Human Design teaches that this group carries mutation, the arrival of something genuinely new, and that mutation keeps its own timing.

The stuck stretches belong to the process. The tradition reads them as the pause before a new order forms, a signal to wait for the right moment, not as a personal failure.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: a planet stood in gate 3 at birth. Human Design reads it as the side of the theme a person knows in themselves.
- **Design**, in red: a planet stood here about three months before birth, when the Sun was 88° earlier. It is the side others often see more clearly.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: gate 3 is coloured but its partner, [[hd-gate-60]], is not. The urge to order is present, and the steady acceptance of limits that grounds it is met in others who carry gate 60.
- **Completed**: with gate 60 coloured too, the [[hd-channel-3-60|channel of Mutation]] is defined, joining the Sacral to the [[hd-centre-root|Root]]. Ulune’s texts describe it as the power to bring something new into form, in pulses, with flat or melancholic stretches before sudden creative change. Accepting the quiet phases helps.

A defined Sacral makes the chart a [[hd-generator|Generator]] or a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]], whatever else it holds.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 3, Zhūn 屯, which Wilhelm called Difficulty at the Beginning. Water stands over Thunder: clouds and thunder before the storm clears, an image of growth pushing through confusion. For all its difficulty, Legge’s judgment promises “great progress and success” (Legge, 1882).
- **Astrology**: the gate straddles a border. It begins in the last degrees of [[aries|Aries]], the sign of beginnings, and ends in the first degrees of [[taurus|Taurus]], the sign of form and substance. The Sun crosses it each year roughly from 16 to 21 April.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] around the tropical zodiac from gate 41 at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius, each 5.625° or 5°37′30″ wide, in the wheel’s order. Gate 3 is the 16th, between gates 42 and 27, so it begins 15 × 5.625° = 84.375° after gate 41. It runs from 26°22′30″ Aries to 2°00′00″ Taurus, that is from 26.375° to 32°.

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 26°22′30″, line 2 at 27°18′45″, line 3 at 28°15′00″ and line 4 at 29°11′15″ Aries; line 5 starts at 0°07′30″ and line 6 at 1°03′45″ Taurus. Line 4 thus crosses from Aries into Taurus.

Any of the thirteen bodies Ulune places at birth and at the Design moment that falls in this span colours gate 3, with its line. Without a birth time, a gate or line that could differ at another hour of the day is marked.
