## Meaning

Sacral authority is the [[hd-authority|authority]] of [[hd-generator|Generators]] and [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generators]] whose [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] centre is defined while the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]] is not. Every Generator has a defined Sacral, since that is what makes the type. Those whose Solar Plexus is also defined follow [[hd-authority-emotional|Emotional authority]] instead, and take their time.

In Human Design the Sacral is the most powerful motor of the bodygraph, the system’s chart of nine centres. The tradition links it to the life force: sustained work, sexuality and fertility. It does not speak in words. It answers. Put a clear question to it and it gives an immediate pull towards or away, often heard as a sound: “uh-huh” for yes, “uhn-uhn” for no.

The tradition calls this the Sacral response, and it lives in the present moment. The answer comes before the mind has time to weigh pros and cons, and that first answer is the one the authority asks to be trusted. The mind keeps a useful role: it looks at the options, gathers facts and explains the choice afterwards. It is simply not the one that chooses.

This authority fits closely with the Generator’s [[hd-strategy|strategy]], to wait to respond. Life brings something, a request, an offer, a question, and the Sacral answers it. A Sacral yes, Human Design teaches, comes with the energy to carry the thing through. A Sacral no keeps that energy for something else. The [[hd-not-self|not-self]] signal for these types is frustration, which the tradition reads kindly, as a sign that energy has gone into things the gut never agreed to.

## In practice

- **Closed questions.** “Do you want to go to the concert tonight?” works better than “What do you want to do tonight?” Friends can help by asking the yes-or-no version and listening to the first sound.
- **The first reaction.** The practice is to notice the very first response, before any explanation starts. A long “hmm” is often taken as “not now” or “not yet”.
- **Small things first.** Trying it on meals, walks or which message to answer first builds trust before larger choices.
- **Manifesting Generators** follow the same authority. The tradition adds that, once the response comes, they may move fast and skip steps they later go back to. Informing others before acting smooths their way.
- **No answer at all.** When nothing rises, the tradition suggests that the question may be too vague, or the moment not yet right.

## Origin and history

In Ra Uru Hu’s order of authorities, the Sacral comes second, just below the Solar Plexus. Its response is trusted when no emotional wave needs to settle first. The gut sounds became one of the system’s best-known practical tools. Teachers often suggest that friends of a Generator phrase their questions so the Sacral can answer aloud.

## How Ulune calculates it

Rule 2 of Ulune’s order: when the Solar Plexus is not defined and the Sacral is, the authority is Sacral. The Sacral is defined by any of its eleven channels: [[hd-channel-34-20|34–20]] to the Throat; [[hd-channel-10-34|10–34]], [[hd-channel-15-5|15–5]], [[hd-channel-2-14|2–14]] and [[hd-channel-46-29|46–29]] to the G centre; [[hd-channel-50-27|50–27]] and [[hd-channel-34-57|34–57]] to the Spleen; [[hd-channel-59-6|59–6]] to the Solar Plexus; [[hd-channel-9-52|9–52]], [[hd-channel-3-60|3–60]] and [[hd-channel-42-53|42–53]] to the Root. With 59–6, the Solar Plexus is defined as well, so that channel always leads to Emotional authority.

In Ulune a defined Sacral always makes the type Generator or Manifesting Generator, so Sacral authority never appears with another [[hd-type|type]]. Choosing the authority on the bodygraph lights the Sacral centre. Without a birth time, Ulune marks the authority as one that could differ if it changes at another hour of that day.
