## Meaning

Gate 45, the Gatherer, is one of the eleven gates of the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat centre]], where Human Design places speech and action. Its theme is gathering: the voice that brings people together and decides how what they share is used.

Human Design reads it as stewardship: someone has to say where the group’s money, food or time will go. It works well when the people gathered also benefit from what is gathered. It works poorly when it slides into simple possession, keeping for its own sake.

The tradition places gate 45 in the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]], part of the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuit]] group. The Tribal circuits care for support, agreements and shared resources. Within them, gate 45 is the speaking end, where the group’s material life is put into words.

## In practice

A gate is coloured when a planet stands in it at birth or in the Design, as [[hd-gate|Gate]] and [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]] explain.

- **By a Personality planet**, at birth: the gathering role is something a person tends to recognise in themselves and may claim openly.
- **By a Design planet**, about three months earlier: others often notice it first. The person finds themselves holding the shared purse without quite having asked.
- **By both**: the theme is present twice, known and lived at once.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: without gate 21, the urge to gather looks for a partner. Human Design teaches that people with [[hd-gate-21|gate 21]], the Hunter/Huntress, often feel a strong pull, since together they complete the channel.
- **Completed into a channel**: with gate 21, the [[hd-channel-45-21|channel of Money]] is defined, joining the Throat to the [[hd-centre-heart|Heart]]. This gives steady willpower to manage material resources and to act on it. Being in charge suits it; being micromanaged does not.

The Heart is a motor. A motor reaching the Throat makes a [[hd-manifestor|Manifestor]], or a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]] when the Sacral is defined as well.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 45, *Ts’ui*, places the Lake above the Earth. Its trigrams are *Tui*, the Joyous, over *K’un*, the Receptive. Wilhelm titles it Gathering Together; water collects where the ground receives it. Legge’s text opens on a gathering for worship: “the king will repair to his ancestral temple” (Legge, 1882). Turned upside down, it becomes hexagram 46, the image of [[hd-gate-46|gate 46]].
- **Astrology**: the gate spans 17°00′00″ to 22°37′30″ [[gemini|Gemini]], the sign of speech and exchange.

## How Ulune calculates it

- Gate 41 opens the wheel at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of tropical longitude. Each gate then spans 5°37′30″ (5.625°) in a fixed order.
- Gate 45 is the 25th, from 17°00′00″ to 22°37′30″ Gemini (77° to 82.625°). Its six lines are 0°56′15″ wide and begin at 17°00′00″, 17°56′15″, 18°52′30″, 19°48′45″, 20°45′00″ and 21°41′15″ Gemini.
- Ulune reads thirteen bodies at birth and again when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The Sun, the Earth, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto each colour the gate they stand in.
- When gate 21 is coloured too, at either moment, the channel 45–21 is defined. Ulune notes whether its gates come from the Personality, the Design or both.
- With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by the profile. Profiles 1/3 to 4/6 give the Right Angle Cross of Rulership 2. Profile 4/1 gives the Juxtaposition Cross of Possession. Profiles 5/1 to 6/3 give the Left Angle Cross of Confrontation 1.
