## Meaning

Gate 55, Spirit, sits in the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], the centre Human Design associates with emotions, feelings and the emotional wave. Its theme is mood: a swing between fullness and emptiness that follows its own rhythm rather than events. With it comes a sense of abundance that does not depend on circumstances. 

Human Design reads the swing as part of the gate’s nature, not as a problem to solve. One week can feel full and the next empty, with little change outside. Knowing that the mood will turn again takes the edge off both ends. It also leaves room for the creative depth the tradition often links to this gate: music, poetry, any art that draws on feeling.

The tradition places gate 55 in the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]], part of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual]] group of circuits. There, energy moves in pulses and brings something new, which has to be explained before others can share it.

## In practice

- **Activated by a Personality planet**, at birth, the theme is usually conscious: the person notices their moods and can name them. **By a Design planet**, from about three months earlier, it is lived more than seen, and others may notice the swings first. **By both**, it is a strong and familiar thread.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**, gate 55 does not define the Solar Plexus. Human Design reads it as a fixed way of meeting emotion that looks for its partner, [[hd-gate-39|gate 39, Provocation]], in the Root. People who carry gate 39 tend to stir the mood, and with them the channel closes for a while.
- **Completed into the [[hd-channel-39-55|channel of Emoting]]**, it defines both the Root and the Solar Plexus. A defined Solar Plexus gives [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional authority]]. Big choices then go better over several days than at a peak or a low.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 55, *Feng*, puts Zhen, thunder, above Li, fire. Wilhelm called it Abundance, and its judgment advises, in Baynes’s English, “Be like the sun at midday” (Wilhelm, 1924). The old commentary adds that the sun at noon begins to set. A fullness that cannot stay is close to Ulune’s reading of a mood that turns.
- **Astrology.** The gate covers the first degrees of [[pisces|Pisces]], the mutable water sign that astrology reads as receptive, imaginative and without firm edges.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune places gate 55 from 0°07′30″ to 5°45′00″ Pisces, 330.125° to 335.75° of tropical longitude. The wheel starts with [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius. Each gate takes 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the fixed order of the Human Design wheel, and gate 55 is the sixth. Its six [[hd-line|lines]] take 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 ends at 1°03′45″ Pisces, line 2 at 2°00′00″ and line 3 at 2°56′15″. Line 4 ends at 3°52′30″ and line 5 at 4°48′45″.

Ulune reads thirteen bodies: the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes, and Mercury to Pluto. It reads them at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. With the Sun here, the Earth is always in [[hd-gate-59|gate 59]]. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon, and positions that could change with the hour are flagged.
