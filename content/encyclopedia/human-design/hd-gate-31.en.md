## Meaning

Gate 31 sits in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat centre]], where Human Design places speech and action. Its theme is influence: the voice of leadership, which works when others recognise it. It is the voice that says where a group should go next.

The tradition places it in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit group]]. Collective energy is read as sharing, and the Logic circuit as the part that looks ahead. It tests patterns, and proposes a safer, better future for everyone.

Human Design reads this leadership as something given rather than taken. In a meeting, the group turns to this person to hear what comes next. Leadership offered before that recognition tends to meet resistance, and the tradition reads that resistance as a signal of timing, not of worth.

## In practice

A planet colours the gate either at birth, in the [[hd-layers|Personality]], or about three months before, in the Design.

- **Personality.** The wish to guide is usually felt as one’s own, along with a sense of when it is welcome.
- **Design.** The gate is read as working out of sight: others ask for direction before the person thinks to offer it.
- **Both.** The theme is marked twice and reads as especially steady.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]], it is half a channel. Its owner may speak with more assurance near someone with gate 7.
- **Completed** by [[hd-gate-7|gate 7]], the Role of the Self, in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], it forms the [[hd-channel-31-7|channel of the Alpha]]. The Throat and the G centre are then defined: a consistent voice for leadership that points a group towards the future. With this channel alone, the chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]]’s, with [[hd-authority-self-projected|self-projected authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 31, *Hsien*, is Influence, or Wooing, in Richard Wilhelm’s version. The Joyous, the Lake, sits over Keeping Still, the Mountain: the youngest daughter above the youngest son, mutual attraction. James Legge’s translation compares its right conduct to the care taken “in marrying a young lady” (1882). Wilhelm reads it as influence that works through openness, not pressure.
- **Astrology.** The gate covers the opening degrees of [[leo|Leo]], the fixed fire sign. The Sun crosses it for about six days in late July.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays a wheel of 64 gates over the tropical zodiac. The wheel starts with gate 41 at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of longitude. Each gate covers 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the order of the Human Design wheel. Gate 31 comes 33rd, after gate 56 and before gate 33. It runs from **2°00′00″ to 7°37′30″ Leo**.

Each of its six [[hd-line|lines]] spans 0°56′15″:

Thirteen bodies are read twice: at birth, and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. They are the Sun and the Earth, the Moon, the true North Node and the South Node, and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours the gate, with its line. With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by [[hd-profile|profile]]:

- from 1/3 to 4/6, the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle Cross of the Unexpected 2]];
- in 4/1, the [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition Cross of Influence]];
