## Meaning

Gate 24 sits in the [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]], the centre Human Design gives to the thinking mind: processing, comparing, making sense. Its theme is rationalisation, in the old sense of making something rational. The mind returns again and again to a thought until it makes sense.

The tradition places it in the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuit group]]. Knowing here is not learned step by step. It tends to arrive whole, after a long circling, as a sudden and personal understanding.

Human Design reads both sides of the circling. Replaying a conversation for days can end in real insight. The same loop can also be used to justify an old habit rather than to see past it. The gate asks for patience with a mind that has not yet found its answer.

## In practice

A planet colours the gate either at birth, in the [[hd-layers|Personality]], or about three months before, in the Design.

- **Personality.** The returning thought is usually noticed from the inside, as a familiar inner rehearsal.
- **Design.** The circling is read as working more quietly, its results surfacing with little sense of effort.
- **Both.** The theme is marked twice and reads as a constant feature.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]], it waits for a question to work on. Someone with gate 61 can supply one, and the circling then feels purposeful.
- **Completed** by [[hd-gate-61|gate 61]], Mystery, in the [[hd-centre-head|Head]], it forms the [[hd-channel-61-24|channel of Awareness]]. The Head and the Ajna are then defined: a steady inner pressure to understand the unknown. With this channel alone, the chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]]’s, with [[hd-authority-mental|mental authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 24, *Fu*, is Return, or the Turning Point, in Richard Wilhelm’s version. The Receptive, the Earth, lies over the Arousing, the Thunder: a single unbroken line comes back at the bottom, under five broken ones. Wilhelm links it to the winter solstice, when the light begins to return. James Legge’s translation says simply: “In seven days comes his return” (1882).
- **Astrology.** The gate covers the early part of [[taurus|Taurus]], the fixed earth sign. The Sun crosses it around the turn of April and May.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays a wheel of 64 gates over the tropical zodiac. The wheel starts with gate 41 at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of longitude. Each gate covers 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the order of the Human Design wheel. Gate 24 comes 18th, after gate 27 and before gate 2. It runs from **7°37′30″ to 13°15′00″ Taurus**.

Each of its six [[hd-line|lines]] spans 0°56′15″:

| 6 | 12°18′45″ Taurus | 13°15′00″ Taurus |

Thirteen bodies are read twice: at birth, and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. They are the Sun and the Earth, the Moon, the true North Node and the South Node, and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours the gate, with its line. With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by [[hd-profile|profile]]:

- from 1/3 to 4/6, the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle Cross of the Four Ways 1]];
- in 4/1, the [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition Cross of Rationalisation]];
- from 5/1 to 6/3, the [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle Cross of Incarnation 1]].
