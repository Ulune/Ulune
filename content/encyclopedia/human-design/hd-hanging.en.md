## Meaning

A hanging gate is a coloured [[hd-gate|gate]] whose partner, at the other end of the [[hd-channel|channel]], is not coloured. It is half a channel. The theme of the gate is there, often strongly, but the link it reaches for is missing.

Every channel joins two gates in two centres. When both are coloured, the channel is defined: a steady flow runs between the centres, and both become defined. When only one is coloured, nothing flows yet. The gate stays open at its far end, like a hand held out.

Human Design reads this half as real and lasting. A hanging gate is part of the chart, not a gap in it. It names a theme that one carries all the time, in the colour of its [[hd-line|line]] and its planet. What it lacks is consistency in the link. The flow happens when someone or something supplies the other gate.

That is why the tradition sees hanging gates as points of attraction. People tend to be drawn to those who carry the missing gate. When two people each hold one end of a channel, the channel switches on while they are together. Human Design teachers call this an electromagnetic connection: often felt as spark, pull or fascination, and sometimes as friction.

A hanging gate can also be completed for a while by the sky. When a transiting planet stands in the partner gate, the channel is defined for as long as it stays there.

## In practice

- **A theme carried all the time.** Gate 34 alone, for instance, gives power that comes in bursts. Its partner gates, 20, 10 and 57, would give that power a steady direction. See [[hd-gate-34]].
- **Someone who completes it.** A person who always seems to bring out a certain energy may carry the gate across from one’s own. The tradition invites noticing this rather than chasing it.
- **Borrowed, not owned.** The completed channel belongs to the meeting, not to either person. When the other person leaves, the half returns to being a half. Human Design reads the difference as useful to know, not as a loss.
- **Undefined centres.** A centre with a hanging gate and no defined channel is undefined, not open. It takes in what others bring mainly through the themes of its active gates. See [[hd-undefined-open]].
- **One gate, several channels.** A gate can be part of a defined channel and still hang in another. Gate 34 with gate 57, but without gate 20, completes [[hd-channel-34-57|34–57]] and leaves its link to the Throat hanging.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune colours a gate when any of its 26 activations stands in it: 13 bodies for the [[hd-layers|Personality]] and 13 for the Design. A channel is defined only when both of its gates are coloured. Otherwise, with one gate coloured, it is drawn as a hanging half.

On the bodygraph, a hanging half is drawn from its gate towards the middle of the channel, in that gate’s colour. Ink means the Personality, red the Design, ink dashed with red both. The line is thinner than a defined channel’s, and the far half stays a faint line. Hanging halves are drawn above undefined channels, and defined channels above both.

When a hanging channel is chosen, its reading says “Half of it in your chart” and names the bodies on the coloured gate. It adds which gate is not coloured, and that the other half can come from another person or a transit. Its layer reads “one half”. In the Personality or Design view, only that layer’s bodies count, so a channel defined across both layers shows there as a half. Ulune does not compare two Human Design charts, so it does not list electromagnetic connections.
