## Meaning

Gate 35 is the gate of Change. Human Design reads it as a hunger for new experiences and the change they bring. It tries the new job, the new city or the new hobby, tires of it once it becomes routine, and moves on. Over the years, that variety becomes broad practical knowledge, worth passing on.

The gate sits in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat centre]], the centre of speech and action, where energy becomes something said or done. Its voice is that of someone who has been there: experience told, not theory.

It belongs to the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]] of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]]. The tradition reads this circuit as learning by living: experiences gone through, reflected on, then shared.

## In practice

- **Personality or Design.** A Personality planet (in ink on Ulune’s bodygraph) makes the appetite for novelty a restlessness people name in themselves. A Design planet (in red) shows it first in an eventful life that others describe. In both layers, the theme is doubly present. See [[hd-layers]].
- **Hanging.** Coloured without gate 36, gate 35 is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the hunger for change has no steady emotional engine. Someone with gate 36 can complete it for a while, often with strong feeling.
- **With gate 36**, it makes the [[hd-channel-35-36|channel of Transitoriness]], joining the Throat to the Solar Plexus. A motor then reaches the Throat. Without a defined Sacral, Ulune reads a [[hd-manifestor|Manifestor]]; with one, a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]]. The authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]].
- Boredom is read kindly here, as a signal rather than a fault: an experience may simply have given what it had to give.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 35, jìn, is Fire (li) above Earth (kun), the sun rising over the land. Wilhelm calls it Progress. In Legge’s judgment, a prince is “three times in a day received at interviews”: quick, bright advance.
- **Astrology**: the gate covers the middle of [[gemini|Gemini]], a curious and mobile sign. In most years the Sun crosses it from about 1 to 7 June.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune’s gate wheel starts at gate 41, at 2°00′00″ Aquarius (302° of tropical longitude). The other gates follow in the order of the Rave I Ching, each 5°37′30″ (5.625°) wide. Gate 35 is the 24th on the wheel, between gates 16 and 45. It spans **11°22′30″ to 17°00′00″ Gemini** (71.375° to 77°).

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 11°22′30″ Gemini, line 2 at 12°18′45″ and line 3 at 13°15′00″. Line 4 starts at 14°11′15″, line 5 at 15°07′30″ and line 6 at 16°03′45″. A longitude exactly on a boundary belongs to the gate or line that begins there.

Ulune places thirteen bodies twice: at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. They are the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours gate 35.
