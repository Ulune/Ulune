## Meaning

The channel of Maturation links the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] at [[hd-gate-42|gate 42]] to the [[hd-centre-root|Root]] at [[hd-gate-53|gate 53]]. Ulune reads it as a consistent energy to start a cycle and carry it through to completion, gaining experience at each stage.

Gate 53, Beginnings, is the Root’s pressure to start something new. Gate 42, Growth, is the Sacral’s energy to see a process through to its end. Alone, each has half a cycle. Joined, they hold the whole of it, from the first step to the last.

The Root is both a pressure centre and a motor, the source of the adrenaline that gets life moving. The Sacral is the motor of life force and work energy, which answers rather than starts. So the tradition reads this channel as best entered by responding to what life brings.

The tradition places this channel in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], also called Abstract. With the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], Sensing makes up the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]], the channels of sharing with the wider group. Sensing learns through lived experience and makes sense of it afterwards. The cycles of 42–53 are where that experience comes from.

## In practice

- **Personality, Design or both.** Defined by the Personality (the planets at birth, drawn in ink), the rhythm of cycles tends to be known and owned. Defined by the Design (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red), it works more bodily, and others may notice it first. With one gate in each [[hd-layers|layer]], it is just as defined.
- **One gate alone.** Gate 53 alone is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: many beginnings, waiting for the energy to finish them. Gate 42 alone can complete what others start, without the pressure to begin.
- **Day to day.** Someone with this channel tends to live in clear chapters: a degree, a job, a relationship. Each is finished before the next begins.
- **Entering by responding.** Ulune’s reading stresses entering cycles by responding rather than on impulse. A cycle started from the head may have to run its course anyway, which the tradition reads as a lesson, not a failure.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Gate 42 is hexagram 42, which Wilhelm calls Increase: Wind over Thunder, two moving forces strengthening each other. Gate 53 is hexagram 53, Development, or Gradual Progress: Wind over the Mountain, a tree that grows slowly and holds firm. Both speak of growth by stages.
- **Astrology.** Gate 42 spans 20°45′00″ to 26°22′30″ [[aries|Aries]], the sign of beginnings. Gate 53 spans 15°07′30″ to 20°45′00″ [[cancer|Cancer]].

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune casts 26 activations: 13 bodies at birth for the Personality, 13 for the Design, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 42 and gate 53 each hold at least one, from either layer. The Sacral and the Root then become defined.

Run as the chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-generator|Generator]]: the Sacral is defined and neither motor reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The authority is [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral]], since the Solar Plexus is not defined, and the definition single.

On the bodygraph, each half of the channel takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, ink dashed with red for both. Defined, the line is drawn thicker than a hanging half, and the two centres are filled in gold. In the Personality or Design view, only that layer’s bodies count.
