## Meaning

Gate 18 is one of the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] of the bodygraph, and Ulune calls it Correction. It sits in the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]], the centre Human Design links with instinct and the body’s awareness in the moment. The tradition also associates the Spleen with the immune system, as a correspondence rather than a medical claim. The gate’s theme is spotting, by instinct, what is wrong in a pattern so it can be improved.

Its owner can see the flaw in a process within minutes, a gift in quality control, editing or coaching. The same eye can weigh on loved ones when the correction was not asked for. Human Design reads the gate’s aim as care for the pattern, not blame for the person.

The tradition places gate 18 in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], also called the Understanding circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective]] circuitry. Human Design links that circuit with patterns that can be tested, corrected and shared for the good of many.

## In practice

- **Personality** (the planets at birth, drawn in ink): the gate is conscious. Its owner tends to know they notice flaws quickly. See [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]].
- **Design** (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red): the gate works below awareness. Others may feel corrected before its owner realises they spoke critically.
- **Both**: the theme is coloured twice, and tends to be a steady trait.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the eye for flaws is there, and it reaches for gate 58, its partner across the channel. People who carry gate 58 can bring the joy that turns critique into improvement.
- **Completed** by gate 58 in the [[hd-centre-root|Root]], it forms the channel of [[hd-channel-18-58|Judgement]]. Ulune reads it as a steady drive to improve things, fuelled by real joy in making them better. Offered where invited, the critique helps; offered everywhere, it strains relationships.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 18, *Gu*, places the mountain above the wind, which blows low and is turned back. Wilhelm called it Work on What Has Been Spoiled. His judgment asks for reflection “before the starting point, three days” and care after it.
- **Astrology**: the gate falls in the first third of [[libra|Libra]], the sign of balance and fair measure.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the gates on the tropical zodiac, from gate 41 at 302° (2° Aquarius), each 5°37′30″ wide. Gate 18 is the 44th gate of that wheel, between gate 46 and gate 48. It covers 3°52′30″ to 9°30′00″ Libra. Its six [[hd-line|lines]] of 0°56′15″ begin at 3°52′30″, 4°48′45″, 5°45′00″, 6°41′15″, 7°37′30″ and 8°33′45″. The same degrees of [[aries|Aries]] hold gate 17.

Ulune places thirteen bodies on the wheel: the Sun and the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes, and Mercury to Pluto. It does so at birth and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. Any of these 26 positions in the span colours the gate. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon. Ulune then flags any body whose gate or line could change at another hour.

When the Personality Sun stands here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] after gate 18. The [[hd-profile|profile]] sets its angle: [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]] or [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle]]. The three names are in the table below.
