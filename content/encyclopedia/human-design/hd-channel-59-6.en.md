## Meaning

The channel of Mating, also called the channel of Intimacy, links the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] at [[hd-gate-59|gate 59]] to the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]] at [[hd-gate-6|gate 6]]. Ulune reads it as a consistent drive to create closeness, filtered by emotional mood.

Gate 59, Sexuality, is the Sacral’s energy to break down the distance between people. Gate 6, Friction, is the emotional membrane that decides how close someone may come, and when. Joined, they make a steady pull towards intimacy, opened or held back by the feelings of the moment.

The Sacral is the motor of life force and work energy, which Human Design also associates with fertility. The Solar Plexus is the centre of emotions, moving in waves of hope and doubt, warmth and coolness. Here the Sacral’s pull is always passed through that wave.

The tradition places this channel in the [[hd-circuit-defense|Defense circuit]]. With the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]], Defense makes up the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuitry]], the channels of family, support and belonging. Defense is read as the circuit that keeps the next generation alive. Its only other channel is [[hd-channel-50-27|50–27]], Preservation: one channel brings people together, the other cares for those that bond produces.

## In practice

- **Personality, Design or both.** Defined by the Personality (the planets at birth, drawn in ink), the pull towards closeness tends to be known and owned. Defined by the Design (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red), it works more bodily, and others often feel it first. With one gate in each [[hd-layers|layer]], it is just as defined.
- **One gate alone.** Gate 59 alone is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: an open, inviting energy, without a steady filter. Gate 6 alone feels the friction of closeness, without the Sacral’s steady drive behind it.
- **Day to day.** Someone with this channel often builds bonds easily, in love, friendship or teamwork, and others feel drawn in. The tradition does not limit it to romance.
- **Waiting out the wave.** Ulune’s reading suggests deciding whether to go deeper once the emotional wave has settled. A yes given at a high, or a no at a low, is read as a signal to wait.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Gate 59 is hexagram 59, which Wilhelm calls Dispersion: Wind over Water, wind loosening what has stiffened. Gate 6 is hexagram 6, Conflict: Heaven over Water, two movements that part. Dissolving distance and guarding it meet in this channel.
- **Astrology.** Both gates lie in [[virgo|Virgo]]. Gate 59 spans 0°07′30″ to 5°45′00″, and gate 6 spans 22°37′30″ to 28°15′00″.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune casts 26 activations: 13 bodies at birth for the Personality, 13 for the Design, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 59 and gate 6 each hold at least one, from either layer. The Sacral and the Solar Plexus then become defined.

Run as the chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-generator|Generator]]: the Sacral is defined, and neither motor reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]], because a defined Solar Plexus comes first in Ulune’s order of authorities. The definition is single.

On the bodygraph, each half of the channel takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, ink dashed with red for both. Defined, the line is drawn thicker than a hanging half, and the two centres are filled in gold. In the Personality or Design view, only that layer’s bodies count.
