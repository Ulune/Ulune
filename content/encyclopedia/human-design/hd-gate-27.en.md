## Meaning

Gate 27 sits in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral centre]], the motor Human Design gives to life force, work and response. Its theme is caring: the energy to care for, feed and protect others. Like every Sacral gate, it is read as energy that answers rather than initiates.

The tradition places it in the [[hd-circuit-defense|Defense circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuit group]]. The Defense circuit is read as the one that keeps the next generation alive. Its themes are nurture, protection, and the bonds that keep children and elders safe.

Human Design reads this gate generously, and with one caution. The gift shows in the person who cooks for everyone or checks that colleagues are coping. It works best when the carer also counts themselves among the people who need looking after. Care that leaves the carer out tends to run dry.

## In practice

A planet colours the gate either at birth, in the [[hd-layers|Personality]], or about three months before, in the Design.

- **Personality.** The urge to look after others is usually felt as one’s own.
- **Design.** The gate is read as working more out of sight: others feel cared for before the carer notices.
- **Both.** The theme is marked twice and reads as especially steady.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]], it is half a channel. Its owner may find a clearer sense of what is worth protecting near someone with gate 50.
- **Completed** by [[hd-gate-50|gate 50]], Values, in the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]], it forms the [[hd-channel-50-27|channel of Preservation]]. The Spleen and the Sacral are then defined: consistent energy to care, guided by instinctive values. With this channel alone, the chart would be a [[hd-generator|Generator]]’s, with [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 27, *I*, is the Corners of the Mouth, or Providing Nourishment, in Richard Wilhelm’s version. Keeping Still, the Mountain, sits over the Arousing, the Thunder: two unbroken lines enclose four broken ones, like an open mouth. James Legge’s translation advises: “We must look at what we are seeking to nourish” (1882). The hexagram speaks of feeding the body and the character alike.
- **Astrology.** The gate covers the opening degrees of [[taurus|Taurus]], the fixed earth sign. The Sun crosses it for about six days in late April.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays a wheel of 64 gates over the tropical zodiac. The wheel starts with gate 41 at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of longitude. Each gate covers 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the order of the Human Design wheel. Gate 27 comes 17th, after gate 3 and before gate 24. It runs from **2°00′00″ to 7°37′30″ Taurus**.

Each of its six [[hd-line|lines]] spans 0°56′15″:

Thirteen bodies are read twice: at birth, and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. They are the Sun and the Earth, the Moon, the true North Node and the South Node, and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours the gate, with its line. With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by [[hd-profile|profile]]:

- from 1/3 to 4/6, the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle Cross of the Unexpected 1]];
- in 4/1, the [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition Cross of Caring]];
- from 5/1 to 6/3, the [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle Cross of Alignment 1]].
