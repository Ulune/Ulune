## Meaning

The Sensing circuit, also called the Abstract circuit, is the second half of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]], the group whose keynote is sharing. Its theme is experience. It does not ask first whether something will work. It goes in, lives the experience fully, and only afterwards looks back to see what it meant. The story it then tells becomes something others can learn from.

The tradition reads it as the circuit of human experience and memory. Where the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]] faces the future, Sensing faces the past. Its learning is not a formula to repeat but a meaning to remember. Two of its channels pass through the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], so feeling, desire and mood colour much of the process.

Its seven [[hd-channel|channels]] trace a cycle of experience:

- [[hd-channel-41-30|Recognition]] (30–41), Root to Solar Plexus: the pressure to imagine new experiences and to want them strongly.
- [[hd-channel-42-53|Maturation]] (42–53), Sacral to Root: the energy to start a cycle and carry it through to its end.
- [[hd-channel-46-29|Discovery]] (29–46), Sacral to G centre: committing fully to an experience and discovering things along the way.
- [[hd-channel-35-36|Transitoriness]] (35–36), Solar Plexus to Throat: the emotional drive for new experiences and the ability to act on it.
- [[hd-channel-64-47|Abstraction]] (47–64), Head to Ajna: sorting past images until they form a meaningful story.
- [[hd-channel-11-56|Curiosity]] (11–56), Ajna to Throat: ideas turned into stories that stimulate others.
- [[hd-channel-33-13|The Prodigal]] (13–33), G centre to Throat: the witness who gathers experiences, retreats, and then tells what was learned.

The cycle has clear key gates. [[hd-gate-41|Gate 41, Contraction]], holds the fuel of new desire. It is also where Ulune’s gate wheel begins. [[hd-gate-53|Gate 53, Beginnings]], starts a cycle, and [[hd-gate-42|gate 42, Growth]], brings it to completion. [[hd-gate-35|Gate 35, Change]], and [[hd-gate-36|gate 36, Crisis]], seek the new and live through its turbulence. [[hd-gate-64|Gate 64, Confusion]], and [[hd-gate-47|gate 47, Realisation]], make sense of it afterwards. [[hd-gate-13|Gate 13, The Listener]], and [[hd-gate-33|gate 33, Privacy]], keep and then share the memory.

## In practice

- **Finding it.** Ulune draws the channels but does not group them by circuit. Any defined channel from the list above means Sensing energy is consistent in the chart.
- **Experience before clarity.** The tradition reads it as natural for this circuit to enter things without knowing how they will turn out. Human Design adds that waiting for emotional clarity first spares many crises. With a defined Solar Plexus, [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional authority]] says the same.
- **Finishing cycles.** Maturation and Discovery are read as learning best when a cycle is carried to its end. Leaving early often means missing the lesson the experience was for.
- **Looking back.** Confusion is part of this circuit’s thinking. The tradition reads it as a stage on the way to meaning, which usually comes with time.
- **Telling the story.** The circuit’s gift is shared through stories, teaching and art. A memory told at the right moment can save someone else a hard lesson.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Gate 64 is hexagram 64, which Wilhelm calls Before Completion, the last hexagram of the book. A circuit always moving into the next experience opens with the image of things not yet done. Its partner in the Logic circuit is hexagram 63, After Completion.
- **Astrology.** The circuit’s starting gate, 41, opens the gate wheel at 2° [[aquarius|Aquarius]]. The Human Design year therefore begins with the gate of new desire, in late January.
