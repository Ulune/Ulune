## Meaning

Gate 39 is the gate of Provocation. Human Design reads it as the pressure to provoke a reaction that shows what someone really feels. It shows as teasing, a challenging question, a remark that tests where people stand. Used with care, it wakes people up; used carelessly, it simply picks fights.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-root|Root centre]], the bodygraph’s pressure centre and one of its four motors, which the tradition associates with the adrenal glands. In gate 39, the push is aimed at feeling: it presses until something real comes out.

It belongs to the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]] of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuitry]], the circuitry of what is new and different. The provocation serves that newness: it unsettles a mood so that a truer one can emerge.

## In practice

- **Personality or Design.** A Personality planet (in ink on Ulune’s bodygraph) makes the urge to provoke known, so its timing can be chosen. A Design planet (in red) lets it act unnoticed, and others may feel provoked without it being meant. In both layers, the theme is doubly present. See [[hd-layers]].
- **Hanging.** Coloured without gate 55, gate 39 is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the provocation looks for a feeling to meet. Someone with gate 55 can complete it for a while, and strong moods may follow.
- **With gate 55**, it makes the [[hd-channel-39-55|channel of Emoting]], joining the Root to the Solar Plexus. It gives a rich emotional life with marked moods, often feeding music, writing or art.
- That channel defines the Solar Plexus, so the authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]]: big decisions go better over several days. With this channel alone, a chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 39, jiǎn, is Water (kan) above Mountain (gen): a steep mountain ahead, a deep gorge beyond. Wilhelm calls it Obstruction. Legge’s judgment ends hopefully: “with firmness and correctness, there will be good fortune”.
- **Astrology**: the gate falls in the first half of [[cancer|Cancer]], a sign read as feeling kept behind a protective shell. In most years the Sun crosses it from about 1 to 7 July.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune’s gate wheel starts at gate 41, at 2°00′00″ Aquarius (302° of tropical longitude). The other gates follow in the order of the Rave I Ching, each 5°37′30″ (5.625°) wide. Gate 39 is the 29th on the wheel, between gates 52 and 53. It spans **9°30′00″ to 15°07′30″ Cancer** (99.5° to 105.125°).

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 9°30′00″ Cancer, line 2 at 10°26′15″ and line 3 at 11°22′30″. Line 4 starts at 12°18′45″, line 5 at 13°15′00″ and line 6 at 14°11′15″. A longitude exactly on a boundary belongs to the gate or line that begins there.

Ulune places thirteen bodies twice: at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. They are the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours gate 39.
