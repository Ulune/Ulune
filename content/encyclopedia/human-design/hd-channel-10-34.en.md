## Meaning

The channel of Exploration gives consistent Sacral power to follow one’s own convictions. Human Design reads it as a life spent finding out, by doing, what it means to be oneself. Such a person tends to go their own way with a lot of energy. Instructions they have not agreed to sit badly with them.

[[hd-gate-10|Gate 10, Behaviour of the Self]], sits in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], the centre of identity, direction and love. It is about behaving as oneself, and accepting that. [[hd-gate-34|Gate 34, Power]], sits in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]], the strongest motor of the bodygraph. It is raw, independent working power that wants to be busy.

The tradition places the channel in the [[hd-circuit-centering|Centering circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuitry]]. Centering is read as empowerment: being so fully oneself that others feel freer to be themselves too. Gate 10 and gate 34 also belong to the [[hd-circuit-integration|Integration channels]], through gates 20 and 57.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: both gates are coloured at birth. The person knows their independence as a conscious trait.
- **Design**, in red: both gates are coloured about three months before birth. Others may meet the stubborn streak before the person sees it.
- **One gate from each layer**: the channel is just as defined; its halves come from two moments.
- **Gate 10 alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a wish to live by one’s own convictions, often drawn to the energy of people with gate 34.
- **Gate 34 alone**: strong Sacral power, which someone with gate 10 can give a personal direction.

Day to day, this channel often shows as people who do things their way and put a lot of energy into it. Human Design adds a nuance that matters. The Sacral works by response, so the channel works best when that independence answers what life presents. Pushing against everything to prove a point tends to wear it out. Responding, it explores freely.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 10, Treading in Wilhelm’s version, places Heaven over Lake: right conduct between the strong and the joyful. Hexagram 34, The Power of the Great, places Thunder over Heaven, strength rising and needing to stay correct. Conduct and power: the two halves of the channel.
- **Astrology**: both gates sit in [[sagittarius|Sagittarius]]. Gate 34 spans 0°07′30″ to 5°45′00″ of the sign. Gate 10 spans 28°15′00″ Sagittarius to 3°52′30″ [[capricorn|Capricorn]].

## How Ulune calculates it

The channel is defined when gates 10 and 34 each hold at least one of the thirteen activations of the [[hd-layers|Personality or the Design]]. The G centre and the Sacral then become defined.

As a chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-generator|Generator]]: the Sacral is defined, but no motor reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The strategy is to wait to respond. The authority is [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral]], and the [[hd-definition|definition]] single. In any chart, this channel makes the type Generator, or [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]] when a motor also reaches the Throat.

On the bodygraph, each half takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, red dashes over ink for both. A defined channel is drawn thicker and its centres are filled; a hanging gate colours only its half. Without a birth time, a channel another hour could change is drawn faded.
