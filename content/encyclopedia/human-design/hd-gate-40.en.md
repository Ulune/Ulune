## Meaning

Gate 40 is the gate of Aloneness. Human Design reads it as the willpower to work and provide for others, followed by a real need to withdraw and rest. It is the person who carries a family or a team all week, then needs an evening alone. Without appreciation or rest, resentment builds; with both, the giving stays generous.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-heart|Heart centre]], also called the Ego, one of the bodygraph’s four motors. The tradition reads it as the centre of willpower, self-worth and promises kept. In gate 40, that will is offered as work, provided the bargain is fair.

It belongs to the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]], at the core of the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuitry]]. This is the world of support, of give and take between provider and community.

## In practice

- **Personality or Design.** A Personality planet (in ink on Ulune’s bodygraph) makes the drive to provide and the need for solitude known, and easy to ask for. A Design planet (in red) lets others notice the withdrawal first. In both layers, the theme is doubly present. See [[hd-layers]].
- **Hanging.** Coloured without gate 37, gate 40 is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the will to work for others lacks a steady emotional partner. Someone with gate 37 can complete it for a while, bringing warmth and belonging.
- **With gate 37**, it makes the [[hd-channel-40-37|channel of Community]], joining the Heart to the Solar Plexus. Work and support are then given in exchange for loyalty and care, with rest as part of the deal.
- That channel defines the Solar Plexus, so the authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]]. With this channel alone, a chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]].
- Wanting to be alone is read as a signal, not a fault: the effort has been given, and recovery is due.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 40, jiě, is Thunder (zhen) above Water (kan), the storm that clears the air. Wilhelm calls it Deliverance. Legge’s judgment finds “good fortune in coming back” once nothing more is called for.
- **Astrology**: the gate falls in the first half of [[virgo|Virgo]], a sign read as service, useful work and retreat. In most years the Sun crosses it from about 29 August to 3 September.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune’s gate wheel starts at gate 41, at 2°00′00″ Aquarius (302° of tropical longitude). The other gates follow in the order of the Rave I Ching, each 5°37′30″ (5.625°) wide. Gate 40 is the 39th on the wheel, between gates 59 and 64. It spans **5°45′00″ to 11°22′30″ Virgo** (155.75° to 161.375°).

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 5°45′00″ Virgo, line 2 at 6°41′15″ and line 3 at 7°37′30″. Line 4 starts at 8°33′45″, line 5 at 9°30′00″ and line 6 at 10°26′15″. A longitude exactly on a boundary belongs to the gate or line that begins there.

Ulune places thirteen bodies twice: at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. They are the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours gate 40.
