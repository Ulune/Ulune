## Meaning

Gate 5 is the gate of Fixed Rhythms. Its theme is keeping a consistent rhythm: habits, routines and a timing that repeats. Human Design reads it as a gate that finds its strength in regularity, the person who eats, trains and sleeps at the same hours and works well that way.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral centre]], the motor of life force and of the energy to work, which in Human Design answers what life brings. The tradition places gate 5 in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], also called the Understanding circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit group]]. Human Design teaches that this circuit works with patterns that repeat and can be relied on, and gate 5 gives that pattern a bodily pace.

A rhythm can be broken. Travel, a new job or a night shift can unsettle someone with this gate for days. The tradition reads that unease as a signal that the rhythm needs restoring, not as fragility.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: a planet stood in gate 5 at birth. Human Design reads it as the side of the theme a person knows in themselves.
- **Design**, in red: a planet stood here about three months before birth, when the Sun was 88° earlier. It is the side others often see more clearly.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: gate 5 is coloured but its partner, [[hd-gate-15]], is not. The private rhythm is steady, and the easy acceptance of many rhythms that gate 15 carries is met in others who have it.
- **Completed**: with gate 15 coloured too, the [[hd-channel-15-5|channel of Rhythm]] is defined, joining the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]] to the Sacral. Ulune’s texts describe a person attuned to natural rhythms, able to set a pace others fall into, and steadied by routines that ground a family or a team.

A defined Sacral makes the chart a [[hd-generator|Generator]] or a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 5, Xū 需, which Wilhelm called Waiting, with Nourishment as its second title. Water stands over Heaven: clouds have risen in the sky and the rain will come in its time. Patience here is active, and Legge’s judgment says that “with firmness there will be good fortune” (Legge, 1882).
- **Astrology**: the gate covers the middle of [[sagittarius|Sagittarius]], a sign astrologers read as the search for meaning and the long journey. The Sun crosses it each year roughly from 3 to 9 December.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] around the tropical zodiac from gate 41 at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius, each 5.625° or 5°37′30″ wide, in the wheel’s order. Gate 5 is the 56th, between gates 9 and 26, so it begins 55 × 5.625° = 309.375° after gate 41. It runs from 11°22′30″ to 17°00′00″ Sagittarius, that is from 251.375° to 257°.

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 11°22′30″, line 2 at 12°18′45″, line 3 at 13°15′00″, line 4 at 14°11′15″, line 5 at 15°07′30″ and line 6 at 16°03′45″ Sagittarius.

Any of the thirteen bodies Ulune places at birth and at the Design moment that falls in this span colours gate 5, with its line. Without a birth time, a gate or line that could differ at another hour of the day is marked.
