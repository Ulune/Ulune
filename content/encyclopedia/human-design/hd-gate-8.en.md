## Meaning

Gate 8 is the gate of Contribution. Its theme is making a personal contribution visible to the group. Human Design reads it as the wish to have distinctive work seen: publishing, presenting, signing an idea, offering an example of what one person’s way can look like.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], the centre of expression in the bodygraph, where speech and action come out into the world. The tradition places gate 8 in the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]], part of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuit group]]. Human Design teaches that this group brings mutation and empowers others by example, and gate 8 is one of the places where that example is shown.

Where individual input disappears into the team, someone with this gate can feel invisible. The tradition reads that feeling as a signal that the contribution wants a stage, not as vanity.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: a planet stood in gate 8 at birth. Human Design reads it as the side of the theme a person knows in themselves.
- **Design**, in red: a planet stood here about three months before birth, when the Sun was 88° earlier. It is the side others often see more clearly.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: gate 8 is coloured but its partner, [[hd-gate-1]], is not. The urge to show is there, and the creative material it presents often comes through others who carry gate 1.
- **Completed**: with gate 1 coloured too, the [[hd-channel-8-1|channel of Inspiration]] is defined, joining the Throat to the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]]. Ulune’s texts describe consistent creative self-expression: a recognisable touch that others notice and are influenced by. Recognition from others helps the contribution reach them.

When the Sun stands in gate 8, the Earth stands opposite, in [[hd-gate-14]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 8, Bǐ 比, which Wilhelm called Holding Together, with Union as its second title. Water stands over Earth: water on the ground flows together. The text asks whoever stands at the centre of a gathering to “re-examine himself” (Legge, 1882), to be sure the union is worth holding.
- **Astrology**: the gate covers the last degrees of [[taurus|Taurus]] and its sixth line just reaches into [[gemini|Gemini]], from the sign of substance into the sign of exchange. The Sun crosses it each year roughly from 15 to 21 May.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] around the tropical zodiac from gate 41 at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius, each 5.625° or 5°37′30″ wide, in the wheel’s order. Gate 8 is the 21st, between gates 23 and 20, so it begins 20 × 5.625° = 112.5° after gate 41. It runs from 24°30′00″ Taurus to 0°07′30″ Gemini, that is from 54.5° to 60.125°.

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 24°30′00″, line 2 at 25°26′15″, line 3 at 26°22′30″, line 4 at 27°18′45″, line 5 at 28°15′00″ and line 6 at 29°11′15″ Taurus; line 6 ends at 0°07′30″ Gemini.

Any of the thirteen bodies Ulune places at birth and at the Design moment that falls in this span colours gate 8, with its line. Without a birth time, a gate or line that could differ at another hour of the day is marked.
