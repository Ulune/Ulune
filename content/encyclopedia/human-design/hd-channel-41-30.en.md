## Meaning

The channel of Recognition links the [[hd-centre-root|Root]] at [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] to the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]] at [[hd-gate-30|gate 30]]. Ulune reads it as a consistent pressure to imagine new experiences and to feel a strong desire for them.

Gate 41, Contraction, is the Root’s pressure to have new experiences, beginning as a seed of fantasy. Gate 30, Recognition of Feelings, is the emotional fire that turns that seed into longing. Joined, they make a steady current of desire, always reaching towards the next experience.

The Root is both a pressure centre and a motor, the source of the adrenaline that gets life moving. The Solar Plexus is a motor too, the centre of emotions, moving in waves. Here the Root supplies the hunger for the new, and the Solar Plexus makes it felt.

The tradition places this channel in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], also called Abstract. With the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], Sensing makes up the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]], the channels of sharing with the wider group. Sensing learns through lived experience and makes sense of it afterwards. 41–30 supplies the wish that sets an experience in motion.

## In practice

- **Personality, Design or both.** Defined by the Personality (the planets at birth, drawn in ink), the desires tend to be known and named. Defined by the Design (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red), they work more bodily, and others may sense them first. With one gate in each [[hd-layers|layer]], the channel is just as defined.
- **One gate alone.** Gate 41 alone is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: rich fantasies, without a steady fire to make them felt. Gate 30 alone burns with feeling that comes and goes, without a constant source.
- **Day to day.** Someone with this channel often has vivid plans, such as a move abroad or a new career. They need to live them to learn from them.
- **Letting feelings settle.** Ulune’s reading notes that letting feelings settle before committing helps separate a lasting desire from a passing fantasy. A disappointed hope is read as part of the experience, not as a mistake.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Gate 41 is hexagram 41, which Wilhelm calls Decrease: the Mountain over the Lake, the lake giving of itself. Gate 30 is hexagram 30, The Clinging, Fire: Fire doubled, a flame that clings to what it burns. Something held back, then something burning bright, is the channel’s movement.
- **Astrology.** Both gates sit in [[aquarius|Aquarius]]. Gate 41 spans 2°00′00″ to 7°37′30″, and gate 30 spans 24°30′00″ Aquarius to 0°07′30″ [[pisces|Pisces]]. Gate 41 is the first gate of Ulune’s wheel.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune casts 26 activations: 13 bodies at birth for the Personality, 13 for the Design, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 41 and gate 30 each hold at least one, from either layer. The Root and the Solar Plexus then become defined.

Run as the chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-projector|Projector]]. Two motors are defined, but none reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], and the Sacral stays undefined. The authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]], since a defined Solar Plexus comes first in Ulune’s order of authorities. The definition is single.

On the bodygraph, each half of the channel takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, ink dashed with red for both. Defined, the line is drawn thicker than a hanging half, and the two centres are filled in gold. In the Personality or Design view, only that layer’s bodies count.
