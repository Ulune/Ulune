## Meaning

Channel 17–62 is one of the 36 [[hd-channel|channels]] of the bodygraph, and Ulune calls it the channel of Acceptance. It runs from [[hd-gate-17|gate 17]], Opinions, in the [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]] to [[hd-gate-62|gate 62]], Details, in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. It is one of three channels that let the mind speak.

Its theme is a consistent ability to turn a view into a clear, organised explanation. Gate 17 forms an opinion about how things should go. Gate 62 gives it names, facts and steps. Together they make a plan that others can follow and check.

Someone with this channel defined can lay out a project step by step, with the evidence to back it. They are often the one who writes the proposal or the report. Human Design adds that this gift lands best when the view has been asked for. An unrequested opinion, however well argued, tends to meet resistance.

The tradition places the channel in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], one of the two [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective]] circuits. Logic channels look for patterns that can be trusted and repeated. Here the pattern is put into words, so that a whole group can understand it and correct it.

## In practice

- **Defined by the Personality** (both gates from the planets at birth): its owner knows they think in structured arguments. See [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]].
- **Defined by the Design** (both gates from the planets about three months earlier): the organising runs below awareness. Others may be struck by the detail before its owner notices it.
- **Defined by both, or by one gate from each layer**: the channel is just as defined. Only its colours change.
- **Gate 17 alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: opinions without the facts to support them yet, waiting for a gate 62 in someone else.
- **Gate 62 alone**: a love of precise facts and names, ready to serve someone else’s view.
- **Day to day**: lists, outlines, careful wording and a taste for getting things right. The tradition’s kind advice is to wait to be asked, and to accept that a perfect plan may still be changed by others.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 17, *Sui*, places the lake above thunder, and Wilhelm called it Following. Hexagram 62, *Xiao Guo*, places thunder above the mountain, and Wilhelm called it Preponderance of the Small. A view others can follow, built from small things done well.
- **Astrology**: gate 17 covers 3°52′30″ to 9°30′00″ [[aries|Aries]], and gate 62 20°45′00″ to 26°22′30″ [[cancer|Cancer]].

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune places thirteen bodies at birth, the Personality, and thirteen at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 17 and gate 62 each hold at least one of these 26 activations, from either layer. The Ajna and the Throat then become defined.

If this were the chart’s only channel, the Throat would be defined but joined to no motor, and the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]] would be undefined. Ulune’s rules then give a [[hd-projector|Projector]], with [[hd-authority-mental|mental authority]], since nothing below the Throat is defined. Its [[hd-definition|definition]] would be single.

On the bodygraph, each half of the line takes its gate’s colour. Ink means the Personality, red the Design, ink dashed with red both. A defined channel is drawn thicker, and its centres are filled gold. The Personality and Design views keep one layer each, so a channel made of one gate from each vanishes there. Without a birth time, a channel that another hour could change is drawn faint.
