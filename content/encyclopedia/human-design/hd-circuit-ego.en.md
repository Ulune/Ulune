## Meaning

The Ego circuit is the larger of the two circuits in the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuitry]], the group whose keynote is support. Its name comes from the [[hd-centre-heart|Heart centre]], which Human Design also calls the Ego centre. Three of its five channels pass through it. The Heart is read as the centre of willpower, self-worth and the material world.

The tradition reads this circuit as the working life of the Tribe. It is about providing: earning, managing, trading, making agreements and keeping them. It also holds the Tribe’s sensitivity to needs, its principles and its sense of who belongs. Work and support go one way, loyalty and care come back. In Human Design’s reading, a fair bargain is what keeps a community fed.

Its five [[hd-channel|channels]]:

- [[hd-channel-40-37|Community]] (37–40), Heart to Solar Plexus: bargains and belonging, work and support given in exchange for loyalty and care.
- [[hd-channel-45-21|Money]] (21–45), Heart to Throat: the willpower to manage and direct material resources, and to act on it.
- [[hd-channel-44-26|Surrender]] (26–44), Spleen to Heart: an instinctive memory for people and patterns joined to the willpower to persuade.
- [[hd-channel-19-49|Synthesis]] (19–49), Root to Solar Plexus: sensitivity to needs and principles within relationships and groups.
- [[hd-channel-32-54|Transformation]] (32–54), Root to Spleen: the drive to rise, paired with an instinct for what will last.

Its key gates tell the Tribe’s story. [[hd-gate-37|Gate 37, Friendship]], and [[hd-gate-40|gate 40, Aloneness]], hold the bargain between care and rest. [[hd-gate-21|Gate 21, The Hunter/Huntress]], wants control of its own resources, and [[hd-gate-45|gate 45, The Gatherer]], speaks for them. [[hd-gate-26|Gate 26, The Egoist]], sells and persuades, guided by [[hd-gate-44|gate 44, Alertness]]. [[hd-gate-19|Gate 19, Wanting]], senses needs, and [[hd-gate-49|gate 49, Principles]], decides who stays. [[hd-gate-54|Gate 54, Ambition]], rises, and [[hd-gate-32|gate 32, Continuity]], checks what will endure.

## In practice

- **Finding it.** Ulune draws the channels but does not group them by circuit. Any defined channel from the list above means Ego energy is consistent in the chart.
- **Willpower in measure.** Human Design reads the Heart as a motor that works and then needs rest. The tradition counsels promising only what the will can truly carry. Gate 40’s wish for time alone is read as part of the deal, not a withdrawal from it.
- **Clear terms.** Community and Money work best with clear terms. The tradition suggests naming what is given and what is expected, so the bargain stays fair.
- **Authority.** With the Solar Plexus defined, as with Community or Synthesis, the chart has [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional authority]]. Surrender defines the Spleen, which comes before the Heart in the order of authorities. With Money and no Solar Plexus, Sacral or Spleen defined, the Heart gives [[hd-authority-ego|Ego authority]].
- **Worth.** The Heart is linked to self-worth. The tradition notes that a person strong in this circuit gives most freely when their own worth is recognised.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 37 is The Family in Wilhelm’s title, 45 is Gathering Together, and 21 is Biting Through. Hexagram 54, The Marrying Maiden, concerns a union and its proper terms. These titles fit a circuit of households and agreements, though Human Design did not group the gates for that reason.
