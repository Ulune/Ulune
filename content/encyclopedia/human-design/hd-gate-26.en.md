## Meaning

Gate 26 sits in the [[hd-centre-heart|Heart centre]], also called the Ego, one of the four motors of the bodygraph. Human Design gives it willpower, self-worth and the making of promises. The gate’s theme is persuasion: memory, presentation and the ability to sell.

The tradition places it in the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuit group]]. Tribal energy is read as the energy of support and bargains: family, community, work, and the give and take that keeps them fed. Gate 26 is the voice that presents what the tribe has to offer.

Its name, the Egoist, sounds harsher than its meaning. Human Design reads it as a talent for making something look its best, using what one remembers of people and what they respond to. The tradition also names its pitfall kindly: overpromising, or bending the story too far. Honesty about what is really offered keeps the gift trusted.

## In practice

A planet colours the gate either at birth, in the [[hd-layers|Personality]], or about three months before, in the Design.

- **Personality.** The knack for pitching an idea or a product is usually felt as one’s own.
- **Design.** The gate is read as working more out of sight, often noticed first by others.
- **Both.** The theme is marked twice and reads as especially steady.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]], it is half a channel. Its owner may sell best when someone with gate 44 senses what people need.
- **Completed** by [[hd-gate-44|gate 44]], Alertness, in the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]], it forms the [[hd-channel-44-26|channel of Surrender]]. The Spleen and the Heart are then defined, joining instinctive memory for people with the will to persuade. With this channel alone, the chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]]’s, with [[hd-authority-splenic|splenic authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 26, *Ta Ch’u*, is the Taming Power of the Great in Richard Wilhelm’s version. Keeping Still, the Mountain, sits over the Creative, Heaven: great strength held and stored rather than spent. Wilhelm links it to holding firm and to learning from the past. James Legge’s translation ends its judgement: “It will be advantageous for him to cross the great stream” (1882).
- **Astrology.** The gate covers the middle of [[sagittarius|Sagittarius]], the mutable fire sign. The Sun crosses it for about six days in mid-December.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays a wheel of 64 gates over the tropical zodiac. The wheel starts with gate 41 at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of longitude. Each gate covers 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the order of the Human Design wheel. Gate 26 comes 57th, after gate 5 and before gate 11. It runs from **17°00′00″ to 22°37′30″ Sagittarius**.

Thirteen bodies are read twice: at birth, and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. They are the Sun and the Earth, the Moon, the true North Node and the South Node, and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours the gate, with its line. With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by [[hd-profile|profile]]:
