## Meaning

Perspective is one of the four arrows of [[hd-variable|Variable]], the finest layer Human Design reads in a chart. Many teachers call it View. It comes from the North Node of the Personality, at the moment of birth. In the tradition’s drawing it is the arrow at the bottom right.

The Nodes are the two points where the Moon’s path crosses the Sun’s. Human Design reads them as the setting and direction of a life, the South Node for its first half, the North Node for its second. The Personality is the layer read as the mind, conscious. There, the Node’s finest shade becomes Perspective: how the mind sees the world around it, and what it is built to notice.

The arrow carries two things. Its colour, one of six, names a way of seeing with a single word, such as Possibility or Probability. Its tone, one of six within that colour, gives its direction.

- Pointing left, tones 1 to 3: Human Design reads a focused, active way of seeing. It is at its best fixing on one thing at a time.
- Pointing right, tones 4 to 6: a receptive, open way, at its best taking in the whole scene and its edges.

Neither side is better. The tradition reads both as styles of the same function, and many teachers describe the right arrow’s way as peripheral, the left’s as focused.

## In practice

Human Design offers Perspective as something to observe over time. Teachers suggest working with it only after a long time of living by [[hd-type|type]], [[hd-strategy|strategy]] and [[hd-authority|authority]]. In this tradition the mind’s gift is to observe and to advise others. Perspective describes what that observer naturally sees.

- Two people at the same meeting may come away with different pictures of it. The tradition reads both as true to their own view.
- With a left arrow, someone may notice they understand best by looking closely at one thing.
- With a right arrow, someone may notice that what matters reaches them from the side, while looking elsewhere.
- The arrow’s word names a lens, not a judgment: the tradition does not rank one way of seeing above another.

Perspective is read together with its partner on the same side, [[hd-arrow-motivation|Motivation]]. The two Personality arrows describe the mind: what drives it and how it sees. On the other side, [[hd-arrow-determination|Determination]] and [[hd-arrow-environment|Environment]] describe the body.

## Across traditions

Astrology reads the [[north-node|North Node]] as a point of direction and growth, and the [[south-node|South Node]] as what is already familiar. Human Design uses the same two points but reads them through gates, lines and, here, the arrow. The two traditions share the astronomy, not the meanings.

## Origin and history

Ra Uru Hu founded Human Design in 1987. He divided each line of the wheel into colours, tones and bases, levels the I Ching does not have. Perspective belongs to the later, advanced part of his teaching. His school teaches it, with Motivation, in its course called Rave Psychology.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune takes the true North Node at the moment of birth, as Jovian Archive’s charts do, not the mean node. It subtracts 302°, where [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] begins, and counts the result in bases of 18.75″. From that one count come the [[hd-gate|gate]], the [[hd-line|line]], the colour, the tone and the base.

The colour gives the word shown with the arrow. The tone sets the side: 1 to 3 left, 4 to 6 right. The Personality South Node, exactly opposite, shares the colour and tone. Ulune computes the base but does not read it.

The arrow sits on the North Node row of the Personality column, on the right of the bodygraph. Choosing that row opens its reading. In the Keys table, Perspective is the second letter after P: in PLR DLL it points right.

The Node moves slowly, on average about 3′ of arc a day, backwards through the zodiac. A tone is about 1′34″, so the Node takes around half a day to cross one. Ulune still casts the chart half an hour before and after the birth time. If the colour or side changes there, the arrow is marked as unsteady. That seldom happens for this arrow. Without a birth time, Ulune shows no arrows.
