## Meaning

Gate 36 is the gate of Crisis. Human Design reads it as emotional inexperience that goes looking for new experience. It leaps into a new relationship or venture, meets the turbulence that follows, and grows through it.

The gate sits in the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]], the emotional motor of the bodygraph. The tradition describes its energy as a wave, rising in hope and falling in disappointment. Clarity comes over time, not in the moment. Seen this way, a crisis is not a failure: it is often how feeling becomes experience.

It belongs to the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]] of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuitry]], the circuit of learning by living. What gate 36 goes through becomes, once digested, something others can learn from.

## In practice

- **Personality or Design.** A Personality planet (in ink on Ulune’s bodygraph) makes the longing for the new something people recognise in themselves. A Design planet (in red) lets others see the pattern of fresh starts and storms first. In both layers, the theme is doubly present. See [[hd-layers]].
- **Hanging.** Coloured without gate 35, gate 36 is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the emotional hunger has no direct way to the Throat. Someone with gate 35 can complete it for a while, often in a lively meeting.
- **With gate 35**, it makes the [[hd-channel-35-36|channel of Transitoriness]], joining the Solar Plexus to the Throat. Without a defined Sacral, Ulune reads a [[hd-manifestor|Manifestor]]; with one, a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]].
- When a channel defines the Solar Plexus, the authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]]. The tradition suggests waiting for clarity before jumping in, which spares much of the turbulence.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 36, míng yí, is Earth (kun) above Fire (li), the light gone below the ground. Wilhelm calls it Darkening of the Light. Legge’s judgment advises one “to realise the difficulty” and stay upright: a time to guard the inner light.
- **Astrology**: the gate covers the last third of [[pisces|Pisces]], the closing sign of the zodiac, read as feeling and longing. In most years the Sun crosses it from about 13 to 18 March.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune’s gate wheel starts at gate 41, at 2°00′00″ Aquarius (302° of tropical longitude). The other gates follow in the order of the Rave I Ching, each 5°37′30″ (5.625°) wide. Gate 36 is the 10th on the wheel, between gates 22 and 25. It spans **22°37′30″ to 28°15′00″ Pisces** (352.625° to 358.25°).

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 22°37′30″ Pisces, line 2 at 23°33′45″ and line 3 at 24°30′00″. Line 4 starts at 25°26′15″, line 5 at 26°22′30″ and line 6 at 27°18′45″. A longitude exactly on a boundary belongs to the gate or line that begins there.

Ulune places thirteen bodies twice: at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. They are the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours gate 36.
