## Meaning

Gate 34 is the gate of Power. Human Design reads it as raw, independent working power: energy that wants to be busy. It can work for hours on something that caught it, without needing anyone else. Aimed at the wrong things, the same power becomes mere busyness.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral centre]], the motor of life force and work energy. The Sacral answers rather than starts. The tradition therefore reads this power as best spent on what a person has responded to.

Like gates 10, 20 and 57, gate 34 belongs to three [[hd-channel|channels]]. Those towards the Throat and the Spleen are part of the [[hd-circuit-integration|Integration circuit]], the channels of self-empowerment. The one towards the G centre belongs to the [[hd-circuit-centering|Centering circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuitry]].

## In practice

- **Personality or Design.** A Personality planet (in ink on Ulune’s bodygraph) makes the drive to stay busy something people know in themselves. A Design planet (in red) makes it more bodily, often noticed by others first. In both layers, the theme is doubly present. See [[hd-layers]].
- **Hanging.** Coloured while gates 20, 10 and 57 are not, gate 34 is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the power comes in bursts. Someone with one of those gates can complete it for a while.
- **With gate 20**, it makes the [[hd-channel-34-20|channel of Charisma]], a motor joined directly to the Throat. Ulune then always reads a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]].
- **With gate 10**, it makes the [[hd-channel-10-34|channel of Exploration]]: Sacral power given to one’s own convictions.
- **With gate 57**, it makes the [[hd-channel-34-57|channel of Power]]: strong energy guided by the Spleen’s instant intuition.
- Each of the three defines the Sacral, so the chart is a [[hd-generator|Generator]] or a Manifesting Generator. Its authority is [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral]] unless the Solar Plexus is defined too.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 34, dà zhuàng, is Thunder (zhen) above Heaven (qian), four strong lines rising under two yielding ones. Wilhelm calls it The Power of the Great. Legge’s judgment asks strength “to be firm and correct”.
- **Astrology**: the gate opens [[sagittarius|Sagittarius]], from its first degree to almost 6°. In most years the Sun crosses it from about 22 to 28 November.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune’s gate wheel starts at gate 41, at 2°00′00″ Aquarius (302° of tropical longitude). The other gates follow in the order of the Rave I Ching, each 5°37′30″ (5.625°) wide. Gate 34 is the 54th on the wheel, between gates 14 and 9. It spans **0°07′30″ to 5°45′00″ Sagittarius** (240.125° to 245.75°).

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 0°07′30″ Sagittarius, line 2 at 1°03′45″ and line 3 at 2°00′00″. Line 4 starts at 2°56′15″, line 5 at 3°52′30″ and line 6 at 4°48′45″. A longitude exactly on a boundary belongs to the gate or line that begins there.

Ulune places thirteen bodies twice: at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. They are the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours gate 34.
