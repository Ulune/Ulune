## Meaning

Gate 7, fully called the Role of the Self in Interaction, is about guiding a group towards a future direction. Human Design reads it as leadership that often works from just behind the visible leader: the adviser or deputy whose suggestions quietly end up setting the team’s course.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], the centre of identity, direction and love in the bodygraph. Gate 7 turns that sense of direction outwards, towards a role in the group. The tradition places it in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], also called the Understanding circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit group]]. Human Design teaches that this circuit looks at patterns to secure the future, and gate 7 is where those patterns turn into a direction others can follow.

The role works best when it is welcomed. The tradition reads resistance from a group not as a rejection of the person but as a sign that the role has not yet been recognised.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: a planet stood in gate 7 at birth. Human Design reads it as the side of the theme a person knows in themselves.
- **Design**, in red: a planet stood here about three months before birth, when the Sun was 88° earlier. It is the side others often see more clearly.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: gate 7 is coloured but its partner, [[hd-gate-31]], is not. The sense of where the group should go is there, and the recognised voice that states it is often found in others who carry gate 31.
- **Completed**: with gate 31 coloured too, the [[hd-channel-31-7|channel of the Alpha]] is defined, joining the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]] to the G centre. Ulune’s texts describe a consistent voice for leadership: someone the group asks to lead, a representative or a spokesperson. Human Design holds that this leadership works when it is recognised or chosen, not simply claimed.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 7, Shī 師, which Wilhelm called The Army. Earth stands over Water: water held within the earth, as strength rests within a people until it is gathered. The text asks for a leader who deserves trust; Legge renders it as “(a leader of) age and experience” (Legge, 1882).
- **Astrology**: the gate covers the middle of [[leo|Leo]], a sign astrologers read as warmth, confidence and the wish to lead. The Sun crosses it each year roughly from 5 to 11 August.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] around the tropical zodiac from gate 41 at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius, each 5.625° or 5°37′30″ wide, in the wheel’s order. Gate 7 is the 35th, between gates 33 and 4, so it begins 34 × 5.625° = 191.25° after gate 41. It runs from 13°15′00″ to 18°52′30″ Leo, that is from 133.25° to 138.875°.

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 13°15′00″, line 2 at 14°11′15″, line 3 at 15°07′30″, line 4 at 16°03′45″, line 5 at 17°00′00″ and line 6 at 17°56′15″ Leo.

Any of the thirteen bodies Ulune places at birth and at the Design moment that falls in this span colours gate 7, with its line. Without a birth time, a gate or line that could differ at another hour of the day is marked.
