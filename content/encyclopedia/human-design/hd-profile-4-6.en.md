## Meaning

A [[hd-profile|profile]] pairs two [[hd-line|lines]]. The first is that of the [[hd-layers|Personality]] Sun, at birth, which Human Design reads as conscious. The second is that of the Design Sun, about three months earlier, read as unconscious and often clearer to others. In the 4/6 the first is the [[hd-line-4|Opportunist]], the second the [[hd-line-6|Role Model]]. Each is the line of the gate where that Sun stands, a hexagram of the I Ching counted from the bottom: lines 1 to 3 form its lower trigram, 4 to 6 its upper one.

Human Design teaches that the fourth line works through its network: its chances come from people it already knows, and its influence travels through friendship. The sixth line lives in three phases: experiment until about 30, observation until about 50, then a life that embodies what was learned. The tradition links these turns to the [[saturn|Saturn]] return and the [[chiron|Chiron]] return.

In the 4/6 the person consciously invests in relationships, a stable circle of friends, colleagues and allies. Underneath, the three phases of the sixth line unfold. The early years can bring many changes, often through the network itself; the middle years more distance; the later ones a place as someone the circle trusts and looks to. Human Design reads the friendships as the medium through which the example is passed on.

Both lines sit in the upper trigram, the half of the hexagram that Human Design reads as turned toward others. The 4/6 is still a profile of the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], which the tradition reads as a personal destiny, lived mostly through one’s own process. Its [[hd-cross|Incarnation Cross]] carries that angle.

## In practice

- A teacher whose first years were spent moving between schools later becomes the colleague everyone consults.
- A group of old friends comes, over decades, to treat one of its members as a quiet reference.
- Losing a close bond can shake the ground. In this reading it is a signal to tend the network, not a failing.
- Being looked to as an example, the tradition suggests, works best when it is not chased.

## Across traditions

In Richard Wilhelm’s I Ching, the fourth line is the minister’s seat beside the ruler. The top line often stands apart. Lines in the same place of each trigram correspond, and Human Design calls them harmonic. The 4 and the 6 are not a pair; their partners make the [[hd-profile-1-3|1/3]].

## How Ulune calculates it

The first number is the line of the Personality Sun, at birth; the second, the line of the Design Sun, when the Sun stood exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. From [[hd-gate-41|gate 41]] at 302° of tropical longitude, each [[hd-gate|gate]] spans 5.625° and each line 0.9375°. The Earth always shares the Sun’s line.

88° is about 93.87 lines. So the Design line usually falls two places after the Personality line, counting on from 6 to 1. It falls three places after only in the last 0.125° of a line. The 4/6 is one of the six common profiles, each about 14.4% of the zodiac, against 2.2% for each rare one. As the Sun moves, the profile steps through the twelve in a fixed order: [[hd-profile-3-6|3/6]], then the 4/6 for about 20 hours, then [[hd-profile-4-1|4/1]] for about three.

Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at noon and at every hour of that day. The Sun moves about a degree a day, more than a line, so the profile is always marked ~: another hour gives a neighbour, usually the 3/6 or the 4/1. The [[hd-cross|cross]] is marked too when its angle or gates could change.
