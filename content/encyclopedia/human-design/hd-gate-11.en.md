## Meaning

Gate 11 is the gate of Ideas. It holds a steady stream of ideas and images, many drawn from past experience. Human Design reads them as material meant to be shared, a gift that stimulates others, rather than a personal to-do list that must all be carried out.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]], the centre of mental awareness, where Human Design places the processing of ideas, concepts and opinions. The tradition places gate 11 in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], also called the Abstract circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit group]]. Human Design teaches that this circuit learns by living through experiences and then looking back on them, so that their meaning can be told to others. Gate 11 is where those memories turn into images and ideas.

Someone with this gate may arrive at every dinner with a new idea. The tradition reads the pressure to act on each one as a misunderstanding of the gift, not as a duty left undone.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: a planet stood in gate 11 at birth. Human Design reads it as the side of the theme a person knows in themselves.
- **Design**, in red: a planet stood here about three months before birth, when the Sun was 88° earlier. It is the side others often see more clearly.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: gate 11 is coloured but its partner, [[hd-gate-56]], is not. The ideas are there, and the storyteller who gives them a voice is often found in others who carry gate 56.
- **Completed**: with gate 56 coloured too, the [[hd-channel-11-56|channel of Curiosity]] is defined, joining the Ajna to the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. Ulune’s texts describe a steady flow of ideas turned into stories: a natural teacher, writer or storyteller, always looking for new experiences to talk about. The ideas are there to inspire, not all to be acted on.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 11, Tài 泰, which Wilhelm called Peace. Earth stands over Heaven: heaven rises from below and earth sinks from above, so the two meet and everything flows. Legge’s judgment opens with “the little gone and the great come” (Legge, 1882).
- **Astrology**: the gate covers the last degrees of [[sagittarius|Sagittarius]], a sign astrologers read as wide horizons, philosophy and the teacher’s voice. The Sun crosses it each year roughly from 14 to 20 December.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] around the tropical zodiac from gate 41 at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius, each 5.625° or 5°37′30″ wide, in the wheel’s order. Gate 11 is the 58th, between gates 26 and 10, so it begins 57 × 5.625° = 320.625° after gate 41. It runs from 22°37′30″ to 28°15′00″ Sagittarius, that is from 262.625° to 268.25°.

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 22°37′30″, line 2 at 23°33′45″, line 3 at 24°30′00″, line 4 at 25°26′15″, line 5 at 26°22′30″ and line 6 at 27°18′45″ Sagittarius.

Any of the thirteen bodies Ulune places at birth and at the Design moment that falls in this span colours gate 11, with its line. Without a birth time, a gate or line that could differ at another hour of the day is marked.
