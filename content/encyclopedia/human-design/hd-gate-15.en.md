## Meaning

Gate 15 is one of the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] of the bodygraph, and Ulune calls it Extremes. It sits in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], which Human Design reads as the seat of identity, love and direction. Its theme is a love of humanity that makes room for a wide range of people and rhythms.

Its own rhythm, though, is irregular. Someone with this gate may sleep late one week and rise at dawn the next. The tradition does not read this as a lack of discipline. It reads it as the gate’s way of holding the extremes, so that it feels at ease with almost anyone, from any background.

The tradition places gate 15 in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], also called the Understanding circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective]] circuitry. Human Design links that circuit with patterns that can be tested and shared for the good of many.

## In practice

- **Personality** (the planets at birth, drawn in ink): the gate is conscious. Its owner tends to recognise their changing rhythms as their own. See [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]].
- **Design** (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red): the gate works below awareness. Others may notice the swings in pace before its owner does.
- **Both**: the theme is coloured twice, and tends to be a familiar part of the person.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the welcome is there, and it reaches for gate 5, its partner across the channel. People who carry gate 5 can lend it a steady routine.
- **Completed** by gate 5 in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]], it forms the channel of [[hd-channel-15-5|Rhythm]]. Ulune reads it as a person attuned to natural rhythms, who can set a pace others fall into. Steady routines tend to ground them; broken schedules can unsettle them more than most.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 15, *Qian*, places earth above the mountain, the high hidden under the low. Wilhelm called it Modesty. His judgment opens: “Modesty creates success.” Its image speaks of reducing what is too much and adding to what is too little, a balance close to the gate’s extremes.
- **Astrology**: the gate starts in the last degrees of [[gemini|Gemini]] and runs into the first degrees of [[cancer|Cancer]], the sign of home and care.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the gates on the tropical zodiac, from gate 41 at 302° (2° Aquarius), each 5°37′30″ wide. Gate 15 is the 27th gate of that wheel, between gate 12 and gate 52. It covers 28°15′00″ Gemini to 3°52′30″ Cancer. Its six [[hd-line|lines]] of 0°56′15″ begin at 28°15′00″ and 29°11′15″ Gemini, then 0°07′30″, 1°03′45″, 2°00′00″ and 2°56′15″ Cancer.

Ulune places thirteen bodies on the wheel: the Sun and the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes, and Mercury to Pluto. It does so at birth and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. Any of these 26 positions in the span colours the gate. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon. Ulune then flags any body whose gate or line could change at another hour.

When the Personality Sun stands here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] after gate 15. The [[hd-profile|profile]] sets its angle: [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]] or [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle]].
