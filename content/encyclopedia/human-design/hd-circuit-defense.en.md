## Meaning

The Defense circuit is the smaller of the two circuits in the [[hd-circuit-tribal|Tribal circuitry]], the group whose keynote is support. It has only two [[hd-channel|channels]], and both start in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral centre]], the motor of life force, work and fertility. The tradition reads it as the circuit that keeps the next generation alive.

Its name is about protection, not hostility. Human Design sees this circuit as the Tribe’s care for what is most vulnerable. That means closeness between people, the children that may come of it, and the values that keep a family healthy. Where the [[hd-circuit-ego|Ego circuit]] provides through work and agreements, the Defense circuit provides through intimacy and nurture.

The two channels:

- [[hd-channel-59-6|Mating]] (6–59), Sacral to [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]]: the drive to create closeness, filtered by emotional mood. The tradition reads it broadly, in friendship and work as well as in love.
- [[hd-channel-50-27|Preservation]] (27–50), Sacral to [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]]: the energy to care for others, guided by instinctive values about what keeps a group well.

Its four gates make two pairs. [[hd-gate-59|Gate 59, Sexuality]], is the energy that dissolves barriers so people can get close. [[hd-gate-6|Gate 6, Friction]], decides through mood how far that closeness goes. [[hd-gate-27|Gate 27, Caring]], is the Sacral’s energy to nourish and look after others. [[hd-gate-50|Gate 50, Values]], is the Spleen’s sense of the rules and responsibilities that keep the group healthy.

## In practice

- **Finding it.** Ulune draws the channels but does not group them by circuit. Mating or Preservation defined in the chart means Defense energy is consistent.
- **Type and authority.** Both channels define the Sacral, so the chart is a [[hd-generator|Generator]] or a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]]. Mating also defines the Solar Plexus and gives [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional authority]]. Preservation alone gives [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral authority]].
- **Caring within limits.** The tradition reads gate 27 as generous, sometimes too generous. Its counsel is to care for others without forgetting to care for oneself.
- **Closeness and timing.** With Mating, the emotional wave decides how close is close enough. Ulune suggests waiting until the wave has settled before going deeper.
- **Beyond family.** Human Design does not limit this circuit to parents. Teachers, carers and anyone who looks after a group can live its theme.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Wilhelm titles hexagram 27 The Corners of the Mouth, with the subtitle Providing Nourishment. Hexagram 50 is The Caldron, the vessel that cooks and offers food. Hexagram 59 is Dispersion, and 6 is Conflict. Nourishment and the shared pot suit a circuit of care, though Human Design did not group its gates for that reason.

## Origin and history

The Defense circuit belongs to Ra Uru Hu’s teaching on circuits. The Definitive Book of Human Design (Ra Uru Hu and Lynda Bunnell, 2011) describes it. With two channels, it shares with the [[hd-circuit-centering|Centering circuit]] the place of smallest circuit.
