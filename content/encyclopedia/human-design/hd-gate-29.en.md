## Meaning

Gate 29 sits in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral centre]], the motor Human Design gives to life force, work and response. Its theme is commitment: saying yes, and persevering through what follows. The yes is meant as a response to something life brings, not a plan made in the head.

The tradition places it in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit group]]. Collective energy is read as sharing: what one person lives becomes a lesson for many. The Sensing circuit learns by going through experiences, and understands them afterwards.

Human Design reads this gate’s strength with care. Once given, the commitment is so strong that it carries a person through difficulty. That is why the choice of what to say yes to matters so much. The tradition reads a yes given out of pressure, rather than true response, as a signal to pause.

## In practice

A planet colours the gate either at birth, in the [[hd-layers|Personality]], or about three months before, in the Design.

- **Personality.** The power of a yes is usually felt as one’s own.
- **Design.** The gate is read as working out of sight: a person finds themselves committed before they have thought it through.
- **Both.** The theme is marked twice and reads as especially steady.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]], it is half a channel. Its owner may commit more easily near someone with gate 46.
- **Completed** by [[hd-gate-46|gate 46]], Determination of the Self, in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], it forms the [[hd-channel-46-29|channel of Discovery]]. The G centre and the Sacral are then defined: the energy to commit fully and discover along the way. With this channel alone, the chart would be a [[hd-generator|Generator]]’s, with [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Hexagram 29, *K’an*, is the Abysmal, or Water, in Richard Wilhelm’s version. The trigram of Water is doubled: danger upon danger, a gorge that water fills and flows on through. James Legge’s translation finds in it “the possession of sincerity, through which the mind is penetrating” (1882). Wilhelm reads it as staying true to oneself while passing through danger.
- **Astrology.** The gate covers the last degrees of [[leo|Leo]], the fixed fire sign, and just touches [[virgo|Virgo]]. The Sun crosses it for about six days in mid to late August.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays a wheel of 64 gates over the tropical zodiac. The wheel starts with gate 41 at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of longitude. Each gate covers 5°37′30″ (5.625°), in the order of the Human Design wheel. Gate 29 comes 37th, after gate 4 and before gate 59. It runs from **24°30′00″ Leo to 0°07′30″ Virgo**.

Each of its six [[hd-line|lines]] spans 0°56′15″:

| 6 | 29°11′15″ Leo | 0°07′30″ Virgo |

Thirteen bodies are read twice: at birth, and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. They are the Sun and the Earth, the Moon, the true North Node and the South Node, and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours the gate, with its line. With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by [[hd-profile|profile]]:

- from 1/3 to 4/6, the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle Cross of Contagion 3]];
- in 4/1, the [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition Cross of Commitment]];
- from 5/1 to 6/3, the [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle Cross of Industry 2]].
