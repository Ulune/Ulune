## Meaning

Gate 16 is one of the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] of the bodygraph, and Ulune calls it Skills. It sits in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], the centre of speech and action. Its theme is developing a skill through repetition, carried by the enthusiasm that keeps the practice going.

Think of the musician who plays scales for years, or the cook who makes the same dish until it is right. The gate brings the will to try, and the joy of trying again. Human Design reads its lesson kindly: enthusiasm opens the door, and practice is what walks through it. Without practice, enthusiasm can drift into overconfidence.

The tradition places gate 16 in the [[hd-circuit-logic|Logic circuit]], also called the Understanding circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective]] circuitry. Human Design links that circuit with patterns that are tested, repeated and refined, then shared for the good of many.

## In practice

- **Personality** (the planets at birth, drawn in ink): the gate is conscious. Its owner tends to know the pleasure of a craft practised daily. See [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]].
- **Design** (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red): the gate works below awareness. Others may see the talent growing before its owner names it.
- **Both**: the theme is coloured twice, and tends to be a familiar part of the person.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the enthusiasm is there, and it reaches for gate 48, its partner across the channel. People who carry gate 48 can bring the depth that gives the practice its substance.
- **Completed** by gate 48 in the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]], it forms the channel of [[hd-channel-16-48|the Wavelength]]. Ulune reads it as a consistent talent, depth joined to love of practice. Such a person can reach real mastery in a craft through years of repetition.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 16, *Yu*, places thunder above earth, a sound breaking out of the ground. Wilhelm called it Enthusiasm. Its image tells how “the ancient kings made music” to honour merit, a fitting echo for a gate of practised skill.
- **Astrology**: the gate falls in the first half of [[gemini|Gemini]], the sign of quick hands, curiosity and learning.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the gates on the tropical zodiac, from gate 41 at 302° (2° Aquarius), each 5°37′30″ wide. Gate 16 is the 23rd gate of that wheel, between gate 20 and gate 35. It covers 5°45′00″ to 11°22′30″ Gemini. Its six [[hd-line|lines]] of 0°56′15″ begin at 5°45′00″, 6°41′15″, 7°37′30″, 8°33′45″, 9°30′00″ and 10°26′15″.

Ulune places thirteen bodies on the wheel: the Sun and the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes, and Mercury to Pluto. It does so at birth and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. Any of these 26 positions in the span colours the gate. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon. Ulune then flags any body whose gate or line could change at another hour.

When the Personality Sun stands here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] after gate 16. The [[hd-profile|profile]] sets its angle: [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]] or [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle]]. The three names are in the table below.
