## Meaning

Gate 46, Determination of the Self, is one of the eight gates of the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], the seat of identity, love and direction. Its theme is love of the body, and the good fortune of being in the right place at the right time.

Human Design reads this gate as trust in being physically present. The body is not a tool to push but the vehicle of a life, to enjoy and care for. Its lucky breaks come from showing up fully where one already is, not from forcing plans.

The tradition places gate 46 in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], also called the Abstract circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit]] group. This circuit learns by living through experiences, and gate 46 brings the body to that learning.

## In practice

A gate is coloured when a planet stands in it at birth or in the Design, as [[hd-gate|Gate]] and [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]] explain.

- **By a Personality planet**, at birth: the love of the body tends to be conscious, and so does its sense of timing.
- **By a Design planet**, about three months earlier: the theme works more quietly. Others may notice the person’s timing before they do.
- **By both**: the theme is doubled, known and lived.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: without gate 29, the gate looks for an experience to commit to. People with [[hd-gate-29|gate 29]], Saying Yes, often draw it into one.
- **Completed into a channel**: with gate 29, the [[hd-channel-46-29|channel of Discovery]] is defined, joining the G centre to the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]]. It gives steady energy to commit fully and to discover along the way. The key is to commit to the right things, since it will see them through either way.

With the Sacral defined, the chart belongs to a [[hd-generator|Generator]] or a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]], whose strategy is to wait to respond.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 46, *Shêng*, places the Earth above the Wind, or Wood. Its trigrams are *K’un*, the Receptive, over *Sun*, the Gentle. Wilhelm titles it Pushing Upward: a tree growing up out of the soil, slowly and without strain. Legge’s text ends on a hopeful note: “Advance to the south will be fortunate” (Legge, 1882). Turned upside down, it becomes hexagram 45, the image of [[hd-gate-45|gate 45]].
- **Astrology**: the gate spans 28°15′00″ [[virgo|Virgo]] to 3°52′30″ [[libra|Libra]].

## How Ulune calculates it

- Gate 41 opens the wheel at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of tropical longitude. Each gate then spans 5°37′30″ (5.625°) in a fixed order.
- Gate 46 is the 43rd, from 28°15′00″ Virgo to 3°52′30″ Libra (178.25° to 183.875°). Its six lines are 0°56′15″ wide and begin at 28°15′00″ Virgo, 29°11′15″ Virgo, 0°07′30″ Libra, 1°03′45″ Libra, 2°00′00″ Libra and 2°56′15″ Libra. The change of sign falls inside line 2.
- Ulune reads thirteen bodies at birth and again when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The Sun, the Earth, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto each colour the gate they stand in.
- When gate 29 is coloured too, at either moment, the channel 46–29 is defined. Ulune notes whether its gates come from the Personality, the Design or both.
- With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by the profile. Profiles 1/3 to 4/6 give the Right Angle Cross of the Vessel of Love 3. Profile 4/1 gives the Juxtaposition Cross of Serendipity. Profiles 5/1 to 6/3 give the Left Angle Cross of Healing 2.
