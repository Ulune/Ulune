## Meaning

Gate 47, Realisation, is one of the six gates of the [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]], the centre where Human Design places thinking: sorting, comparing and forming concepts. Its theme is making sense of what has already happened.

Human Design reads it as a slow inner sorting. Memories of past experience turn over in the mind, and for a while this can feel like mental pressure. Then, often when least expected, the pieces fall into place and the meaning is clear.

The tradition treats the waiting as part of the gift, not a flaw. Forcing the answer usually deepens the frustration; letting the experience settle lets the realisation come.

The tradition places gate 47 in the [[hd-circuit-sensing|Sensing circuit]], also called the Abstract circuit, part of the [[hd-circuit-collective|Collective circuit]] group. Gate 47 is where its reflection on lived experience becomes an insight to share.

## In practice

A gate is coloured when a planet stands in it at birth or in the Design, as [[hd-gate|Gate]] and [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]] explain.

- **By a Personality planet**, at birth: the person tends to recognise the fog, and to trust that it lifts.
- **By a Design planet**, about three months earlier: the sorting goes on below awareness. Insights may seem to arrive from nowhere, after a night’s sleep or a walk.
- **By both**: the theme is doubled, known and lived.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: without gate 64, the gate waits for material to work on. People with [[hd-gate-64|gate 64]], Confusion, bring the stream of images it longs to order.
- **Completed into a channel**: with gate 64, the [[hd-channel-64-47|channel of Abstraction]] is defined, joining the [[hd-centre-head|Head]] to the Ajna. It gives a steady way of thinking: past images are sorted until they form a meaningful story. Confusion is part of the process and usually clears with time.

Neither the Head nor the Ajna is a motor. When this is a chart’s only channel, Ulune finds a [[hd-projector|Projector]] with [[hd-authority-mental|mental authority]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 47, *K’un*, places the Lake above Water. Its trigrams are *Tui*, the Joyous, over *K’an*, the Abysmal. Wilhelm titles it Oppression, or Exhaustion: a lake whose water has drained away below it. Even so, Legge’s judgement still promises “progress and success” to the firm and correct (Legge, 1882). Turned upside down, it becomes hexagram 48, the image of [[hd-gate-48|gate 48]].
- **Astrology**: the gate spans 17°00′00″ to 22°37′30″ [[virgo|Virgo]], the sign of sorting and careful analysis.

## How Ulune calculates it

- Gate 41 opens the wheel at 2°00′00″ Aquarius, 302° of tropical longitude. Each gate then spans 5°37′30″ (5.625°) in a fixed order.
- Gate 47 is the 41st, from 17°00′00″ to 22°37′30″ Virgo (167° to 172.625°). Its six lines are 0°56′15″ wide and begin at 17°00′00″, 17°56′15″, 18°52′30″, 19°48′45″, 20°45′00″ and 21°41′15″ Virgo.
- Ulune reads thirteen bodies at birth and again when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The Sun, the Earth, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto each colour the gate they stand in.
- When gate 64 is coloured too, at either moment, the channel 64–47 is defined. Ulune notes whether its gates come from the Personality, the Design or both.
- With the Personality Sun here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] by the profile. Profiles 1/3 to 4/6 give the Right Angle Cross of Rulership 3. Profile 4/1 gives the Juxtaposition Cross of Oppression. Profiles 5/1 to 6/3 give the Left Angle Cross of Informing 2.
