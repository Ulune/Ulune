## Meaning

Gate 10 is the gate of the Behaviour of the Self. Its theme is self-love in a practical sense: behaving as oneself, and accepting that. Human Design reads it in the person who acts the same way whoever is watching, and who would rather lose approval than put on a manner that is not theirs.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], the centre of identity, direction and love in the bodygraph. Gate 10 is one of only four gates that belong to three channels. Through gate 34 it joins the [[hd-circuit-centering|Centering circuit]] of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuit group]], which Human Design associates with self-empowerment. Through gates 20 and 57 it joins the [[hd-circuit-integration|Integration channels]], which the tradition treats apart, as the wiring of individual survival.

The theme carries its own caution. The same demand to be true to oneself can turn into self-criticism. The tradition reads that inner harshness as a signal to return to acceptance, not as a verdict.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: a planet stood in gate 10 at birth. Human Design reads it as the side of the theme a person knows in themselves.
- **Design**, in red: a planet stood here about three months before birth, when the Sun was 88° earlier. It is the side others often see more clearly.
- **Alone, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]**: gate 10 is coloured but none of its three partners is. Others who carry gate 20, 57 or 34 can each complete a different channel.
- **With [[hd-gate-20]]**: the [[hd-channel-10-20|channel of Awakening]] joins the G centre to the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], for saying what one thinks and living by it.
- **With [[hd-gate-57]]**: the [[hd-channel-10-57|channel of Perfected Form]] joins it to the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]], for authentic behaviour guided by intuition.
- **With [[hd-gate-34]]**: the [[hd-channel-10-34|channel of Exploration]] joins it to the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]], with strong energy to follow one’s own convictions. It makes the chart a [[hd-generator|Generator]] or a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]].

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 10, Lǚ 履, which Wilhelm called Treading, with Conduct as its second title. Heaven stands over Lake: the strong above, the joyful below, and right conduct between them. Its image is “treading on the tail of a tiger, which does not bite him” (Legge, 1882).
- **Astrology**: the gate straddles the end of [[sagittarius|Sagittarius]] and the start of [[capricorn|Capricorn]], and holds the December solstice, 0° Capricorn, in its second line. The Sun crosses it each year roughly from 20 to 25 December.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune lays the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] around the tropical zodiac from gate 41 at 302°, 2°00′00″ Aquarius, each 5.625° or 5°37′30″ wide, in the wheel’s order. Gate 10 is the 59th, between gates 11 and 58, so it begins 58 × 5.625° = 326.25° after gate 41. It runs from 28°15′00″ Sagittarius to 3°52′30″ Capricorn, that is from 268.25° to 273.875°.

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 28°15′00″ and line 2 at 29°11′15″ Sagittarius; line 3 starts at 0°07′30″, line 4 at 1°03′45″, line 5 at 2°00′00″ and line 6 at 2°56′15″ Capricorn.

Any of the thirteen bodies Ulune places at birth and at the Design moment that falls in this span colours gate 10, with its line. Without a birth time, a gate or line that could differ at another hour of the day is marked.
