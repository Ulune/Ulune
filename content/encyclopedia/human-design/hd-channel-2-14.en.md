## Meaning

The channel of the Beat is often called the Keeper of Keys. It joins a consistent sense of direction with the energy to generate resources. Human Design reads it as a compass wired to a motor. The person knows where things should go, and has the power to put work and means behind it.

[[hd-gate-2|Gate 2, Direction of the Self]], sits in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], the centre of identity, direction and love. It carries an inner sense of where things should go. [[hd-gate-14|Gate 14, Power Skills]], sits in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]], the strongest motor of the bodygraph. It is the energy to put into work that generates resources.

The tradition places the channel in the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]], within the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuitry]]. Individual energy is read as bringing something new, often ahead of its time. Here the new thing is a direction, and the resources follow it rather than the other way round.

## In practice

- **Personality**, in ink: both gates are coloured at birth. The person knows their sense of direction as their own.
- **Design**, in red: both gates are coloured about three months before birth. Others may see where this person is heading before they do.
- **One gate from each layer**: the channel is just as defined; its halves come from two moments.
- **Gate 2 alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a sense of direction that looks for energy, often in people who carry gate 14.
- **Gate 14 alone**: strong working power, which someone with gate 2 can point somewhere.

Day to day, someone with this channel defined often ends up steering money, time or projects towards what they believe in. The tradition says the resources follow when the direction feels right to them. In the Sacral’s way, the right direction is recognised by response, not by planning alone.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 2, The Receptive in Wilhelm’s version, doubles Earth: six yielding lines, strength that follows rather than leads. Hexagram 14, Possession in Great Measure, places Fire over Heaven, a light shining from on high. Receptive direction and abundance: the two halves of the channel.
- **Astrology**: gate 2 spans 13°15′00″ to 18°52′30″ [[taurus|Taurus]]. Gate 14 spans 24°30′00″ [[scorpio|Scorpio]] to 0°07′30″ [[sagittarius|Sagittarius]], on the opposite side of the zodiac.

## How Ulune calculates it

The channel is defined when gates 2 and 14 each hold at least one of the thirteen activations of the [[hd-layers|Personality or the Design]]. The G centre and the Sacral then become defined.

As a chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-generator|Generator]]: the Sacral is defined, but no motor reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]]. The strategy is to wait to respond. The authority is [[hd-authority-sacral|sacral]], and the [[hd-definition|definition]] single. In any chart, this channel makes the type Generator, or [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]] when a motor also reaches the Throat.

On the bodygraph, each half takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, red dashes over ink for both. A defined channel is drawn thicker and its centres are filled; a hanging gate colours only its half. Its line crosses that of [[hd-channel-44-26|44–26]]; Ulune draws defined channels above hanging ones and those above bare ones, so a click opens the coloured line. Without a birth time, a channel another hour could change is drawn faded.
