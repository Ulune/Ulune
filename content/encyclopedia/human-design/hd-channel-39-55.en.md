## Meaning

The channel of Emoting links the [[hd-centre-root|Root]] at [[hd-gate-39|gate 39]] to the [[hd-centre-solar-plexus|Solar Plexus]] at [[hd-gate-55|gate 55]]. Ulune reads it as a consistent, rich emotional life with marked moods, and the ability to stir feelings in others.

Gate 39, Provocation, is the Root’s pressure to stir things up, to test whether there is real spirit in a person or a moment. Gate 55, Spirit, is the emotional wave at its fullest: abundance one day, emptiness the next. Joined, they make moods that are vivid, changing and catching.

The Root is both a pressure centre and a motor, the source of the adrenaline that gets life moving. The Solar Plexus is a motor too, the centre of emotions, moving in waves. Here the Root keeps pushing the wave, so the moods are strong and personal.

The tradition places this channel in the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]]. With the [[hd-circuit-centering|Centering circuit]], it forms the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuitry]]. Individual channels are read as personal, working in their own rhythm and touching others by example. The melancholy of this channel is read the same way: a creative source, not a problem to fix.

## In practice

- **Personality, Design or both.** Defined by the Personality (the planets at birth, drawn in ink), the moods tend to be known as one’s own. Defined by the Design (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red), they work more bodily, and others may feel them first. With one gate in each [[hd-layers|layer]], the channel is just as defined.
- **One gate alone.** Gate 39 alone is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: a knack for provoking, without a steady wave behind it. Gate 55 alone feels the highs and lows, without the Root’s constant pressure.
- **Day to day.** Someone with this channel often has creative depth that draws on those moods, in music, writing or art.
- **Decisions over time.** Ulune’s reading notes that big decisions go better when made over several days, rather than at a peak or a low. The tradition reads a mood as weather to wait out, not as a verdict.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching.** Gate 39 is hexagram 39, which Wilhelm calls Obstruction: Water over the Mountain, a gorge beyond a steep slope. Gate 55 is hexagram 55, Abundance: Thunder over Fire, a fullness like the sun at noon, which must then decline. Obstacle and fullness frame the channel’s swing of moods.
- **Astrology.** Gate 39 spans 9°30′00″ to 15°07′30″ [[cancer|Cancer]]. Gate 55 spans 0°07′30″ to 5°45′00″ [[pisces|Pisces]]. Both are water signs, which astrology links to feeling.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune casts 26 activations: 13 bodies at birth for the Personality, 13 for the Design, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. The channel is defined when gate 39 and gate 55 each hold at least one, from either layer. The Root and the Solar Plexus then become defined.

Run as the chart’s only channel, Ulune’s logic gives a [[hd-projector|Projector]]. Two motors are defined, but none reaches the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], and the Sacral stays undefined. The authority is [[hd-authority-emotional|emotional]], since a defined Solar Plexus comes first in Ulune’s order of authorities. The definition is single.

On the bodygraph, each half of the channel takes its gate’s colour: ink for the Personality, red for the Design, ink dashed with red for both. Defined, the line is drawn thicker than a hanging half, and the two centres are filled in gold. In the Personality or Design view, only that layer’s bodies count.
