## Meaning

The [[hd-cross|Incarnation Cross]] gathers four gates: the Sun and the Earth at birth, the Personality, and in the Design, about three months earlier. Human Design reads it as the theme of a life. Its angle, one of three, comes from the [[hd-profile|profile]]. The Juxtaposition is the rarest.

Only one profile gives it: the [[hd-profile-4-1|4/1]], with the [[hd-line-4|Opportunist]] at birth and the [[hd-line-1|Investigator]] in the Design. Line 4 opens the upper trigram of the hexagram, which Human Design reads as its transpersonal half. Line 1 opens the lower, personal half. The 4/1 stands with one foot in each, and the tradition places its cross between the two other angles.

Human Design describes a Juxtaposition cross as a fixed path. The life keeps to its own track and does not bend easily. It leans neither towards its own process, as with the [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], nor towards others, as with the [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle]]. The word juxtaposition means set side by side. Some teachers call this angle a fixed fate.

The tradition does not read this steadiness as rigidity. A Juxtaposition life influences others by staying what it is, much as a landmark does, rather than by persuasion.

## In practice

As with every cross, Human Design teaches that the theme appears by itself when someone follows their [[hd-type|type]], [[hd-strategy|strategy]] and [[hd-authority|authority]].

- Someone may notice that their life keeps returning to the same theme, whatever they try.
- Efforts to force a change of direction often feel heavy. In this reading, change comes when the foundation itself shifts.
- Being out of step with others’ plans is common, and the tradition reads it as keeping to one’s own track, not as a failure.

## Origin and history

The three angles belong to Ra Uru Hu’s system, founded in 1987. The Definitive Book of Human Design, written with Lynda Bunnell, ties them to the profiles and gives the Juxtaposition to the 4/1 alone. The name Juxtaposition belongs to Human Design; it has no counterpart in the I Ching.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the profile: the line of the Personality Sun, then that of the Design Sun. The Design Sun is the Sun exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. A profile of 4/1 gives Juxtaposition, and nothing else does.

The 88° arc equals about 93.87 lines. The Design line is three places after the Personality line only in the last 0.125° of a line. In line 4, that last stretch gives the 4/1. It is about three hours of the Sun’s motion in each gate, roughly 2% of the Sun’s path.

In that same stretch, the Design Sun has just crossed into the next gate, fifteen places behind the Personality Sun’s instead of sixteen. Its four gates are therefore the same as those of a Left Angle cross. The Juxtaposition Cross of Self-Expression, 1/2 | 4/49, shares its gates with the Left Angle Cross of Defiance 2. Only the profile tells them apart.

There are 64 Juxtaposition crosses, one for each gate of the Personality Sun, and each has its own name. The name often echoes that gate’s own: the Now for [[hd-gate-20|gate 20]], Power for [[hd-gate-34|gate 34]], Self-Expression for [[hd-gate-1|gate 1]]. Others differ, such as the Driver for [[hd-gate-2|gate 2]]. Ulune’s French names follow the French Human Design sources, as in “Croix de juxtaposition du conducteur”.

Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at every hour of that day. The 4/1 lasts only about three hours, so another hour always gives a 4/6 or a 5/1, and a Juxtaposition cross is always marked ~.
