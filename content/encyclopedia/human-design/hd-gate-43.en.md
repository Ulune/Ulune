## Meaning

Gate 43 is the gate of Insight. Human Design reads it as the source of sudden, individual insights that arrive already formed. It is the solution that pops up in the shower, and the difficulty of explaining where it came from. Others may not be ready for it, so timing matters greatly.

It sits in the [[hd-centre-ajna|Ajna]], the centre of the mind, where thoughts are formed and turned into concepts. The Ajna is not a motor: it processes rather than drives. In gate 43, the processing is inward and unusual, a knowing that skips the expected steps.

It belongs to the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]] of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual circuitry]], the circuitry of what is new. Once it finds words, its insight can change how others think.

## In practice

- **Personality or Design.** A Personality planet (in ink on Ulune’s bodygraph) makes the insights a familiar way of thinking. A Design planet (in red) lets them surface unexpectedly, sometimes to the person’s own surprise. In both layers, the theme is doubly present. See [[hd-layers]].
- **Hanging.** Coloured without gate 23, gate 43 is a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: insight has no steady way to the voice. Someone with gate 23 can complete it for a while, helping to put it into simple words.
- **With gate 23**, it makes the [[hd-channel-43-23|channel of Structuring]], joining the Ajna to the Throat. Original insights can then be voiced consistently. Explained simply and at the right moment, they sound brilliant; blurted out early, strange.
- That channel defines only the Ajna and the Throat. With it alone, a chart would be a [[hd-projector|Projector]] with [[hd-authority-mental|Mental authority]].
- Being misunderstood is read as a matter of timing, not as proof that the insight is wrong.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 43, guài, is Lake (dui) above Heaven (qian), five strong lines pushing out the last weak one. Wilhelm calls it Break-through (Resoluteness). In Legge’s judgment, one must “make announcement in his own city”: what is decided is said openly.
- **Astrology**: the gate falls in the last third of [[scorpio|Scorpio]], a sign read as depth and truths found below the surface. In most years the Sun crosses it from about 11 to 16 November.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune’s gate wheel starts at gate 41, at 2°00′00″ Aquarius (302° of tropical longitude). The other gates follow in the order of the Rave I Ching, each 5°37′30″ (5.625°) wide. Gate 43 is the 52nd on the wheel, between gates 1 and 14. It spans **18°52′30″ to 24°30′00″ Scorpio** (228.875° to 234.5°).

Its six [[hd-line|lines]] are 0°56′15″ each. Line 1 starts at 18°52′30″ Scorpio, line 2 at 19°48′45″ and line 3 at 20°45′00″. Line 4 starts at 21°41′15″, line 5 at 22°37′30″ and line 6 at 23°33′45″. A longitude exactly on a boundary belongs to the gate or line that begins there.

Ulune places thirteen bodies twice: at birth for the Personality, and for the Design when the Sun stood exactly 88° of longitude earlier. They are the Sun, the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes and Mercury to Pluto. Any of them in this span colours gate 43.
