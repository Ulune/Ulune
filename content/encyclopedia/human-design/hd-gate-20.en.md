## Meaning

Gate 20 is one of the 64 [[hd-gate|gates]] of the bodygraph, and Ulune calls it the Now. It sits in the [[hd-centre-throat|Throat]], the centre of speech and action. Its theme is awareness expressed in the present moment.

Its owner reacts quickly and accurately to what is happening right now, in a meeting or an emergency. Naming what is in front of them comes more easily than planning far ahead. Human Design reads this as a gift of presence, not a lack of foresight.

Gate 20 is one of the four gates, with 10, 34 and 57, that have three channels. The tradition places two of them, with gates 10 and 34, among the [[hd-circuit-integration|Integration]] channels, which it links with self-empowerment. The third, with gate 57, belongs to the [[hd-circuit-knowing|Knowing circuit]] of the [[hd-circuit-individual|Individual]] circuitry. Some teachers count it with the Integration channels too.

## In practice

- **Personality** (the planets at birth, drawn in ink): the gate is conscious. Its owner tends to think best on their feet. See [[hd-layers|Personality and Design]].
- **Design** (the planets about three months earlier, drawn in red): the gate works below awareness. Others may notice the quick replies before its owner does.
- **Both**: the theme is coloured twice, and tends to be a steady trait.
- **Alone**, as a [[hd-hanging|hanging gate]]: the voice of the moment is there, and it reaches for gates 10, 34 and 57.
- **Completed** by gate 10 in the [[hd-centre-g|G centre]], it forms the channel of [[hd-channel-10-20|Awakening]]: one’s own way of being, lived and spoken now.
- **Completed** by gate 34 in the [[hd-centre-sacral|Sacral]], it forms the channel of [[hd-channel-34-20|Charisma]]. It joins a motor straight to the Throat, so its owner is a [[hd-manifesting-generator|Manifesting Generator]].
- **Completed** by gate 57 in the [[hd-centre-spleen|Spleen]], it forms the channel of [[hd-channel-20-57|the Brain Wave]]: intuition put into words at once.

## Across traditions

- **The I Ching**: hexagram 20, *Guan*, places wind above earth. Wilhelm called it Contemplation (View). Its image begins: “The wind blows over the earth.”
- **Astrology**: the gate opens [[gemini|Gemini]], the sign of quick speech and of the present exchange.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the gates on the tropical zodiac, from gate 41 at 302° (2° Aquarius), each 5°37′30″ wide. Gate 20 is the 22nd gate of that wheel, between gate 8 and gate 16. It covers 0°07′30″ to 5°45′00″ Gemini. Its six [[hd-line|lines]] of 0°56′15″ begin at 0°07′30″, 1°03′45″, 2°00′00″, 2°56′15″, 3°52′30″ and 4°48′45″.

Ulune places thirteen bodies on the wheel: the Sun and the Earth opposite it, the Moon, the true Nodes, and Mercury to Pluto. It does so at birth and at the Design moment, when the Sun stood exactly 88° earlier. Any of these 26 positions in the span colours the gate. Without a birth time, the chart is cast at noon. Ulune then flags any body whose gate or line could change at another hour.

With the Sun in gate 20, the Earth of the same layer stands in gate 34, exactly opposite, and the reverse. Charisma is then always defined, so Ulune finds a Manifesting Generator.

When the Personality Sun stands here, Ulune names the [[hd-cross|incarnation cross]] after gate 20. The [[hd-profile|profile]] sets its angle: [[hd-cross-right-angle|Right Angle]], [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]] or [[hd-cross-left-angle|Left Angle]]. The three names are in the table below.
