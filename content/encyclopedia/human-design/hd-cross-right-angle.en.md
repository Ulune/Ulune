## Meaning

The [[hd-cross|Incarnation Cross]] gathers four gates: the Sun and the Earth at birth, the Personality, and in the Design, about three months earlier. Human Design reads it as the theme of a life. Its angle, one of three, comes from the [[hd-profile|profile]]. The Right Angle is the most common.

Human Design describes a Right Angle cross as a personal destiny. The life unfolds mostly through its own process: its own experiences, choices and discoveries. Other people matter deeply, but the tradition reads them as part of that personal journey rather than as its purpose. Some teachers put it as a life that is, in a healthy sense, about itself.

Seven profiles give a Right Angle cross:

- [[hd-profile-1-3|1/3]] and [[hd-profile-1-4|1/4]], with the [[hd-line-1|Investigator]] at birth.
- [[hd-profile-2-4|2/4]] and [[hd-profile-2-5|2/5]], with the [[hd-line-2|Hermit]].
- [[hd-profile-3-5|3/5]] and [[hd-profile-3-6|3/6]], with the [[hd-line-3|Martyr]].
- [[hd-profile-4-6|4/6]], with the [[hd-line-4|Opportunist]].

In each, the line of the Personality Sun is 1 to 4. Lines 1 to 3 form the hexagram’s lower trigram, which Human Design reads as its personal half. The 4/6 completes the group; the [[hd-profile-4-1|4/1]], the other profile with a line 4 at birth, has its own angle, the [[hd-cross-juxtaposition|Juxtaposition]].

## In practice

The tradition does not ask a Right Angle person to find their purpose. It teaches that the purpose shows itself when they follow their [[hd-type|type]], [[hd-strategy|strategy]] and [[hd-authority|authority]].

- Someone may notice that their turning points came from their own experiments, more than from what others asked of them.
- Meetings and partnerships still shape them, and the tradition reads those meetings as chapters of their own story.
- Feeling self-centred for following one’s own track is common. Human Design reads it as fitting for this angle, not as a fault.

## Across traditions

In a Right Angle cross the four gates sit exactly a quarter of the wheel apart. They form a square, like the four points of an astrological [[grand-cross|grand cross]]. The likeness is geometric only: in Human Design two of the points come from the Design, a different moment from the birth.

## Origin and history

The three angles belong to Ra Uru Hu’s system, founded in 1987. The Definitive Book of Human Design, written with Lynda Bunnell, ties them to the profiles. The tradition divides the wheel into four quarters, named Initiation, Civilization, Duality and Mutation. The Right Angle crosses take their number from them.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune reads the profile: the line of the Personality Sun, then that of the Design Sun. The Design Sun is the Sun exactly 88° of ecliptic longitude earlier. A Personality line of 1 to 4 gives Right Angle, unless the profile is 4/1.

With these profiles the Design Sun always stands in the gate sixteen places behind the Personality Sun’s, a quarter of the wheel. The Personality Sun and Earth and the Design Sun and Earth therefore form a square. The same square serves four crosses, one for each of its gates as the Personality Sun. Ulune gives them one name, followed by the quarter the Personality Sun stands in, 1 to 4. The quarters begin at [[hd-gate-13|gate 13]], gate 2, gate 7 and gate 1.

So there are 64 Right Angle crosses with 16 names. In the Keys table, the Right Angle Cross of the Sphinx 4 is written 1/2 | 7/13. Ulune’s French names follow the French Human Design sources, as in “Croix de l’angle droit du Sphinx 4”.

The Right Angle covers about 64% of the Sun’s path, so most people have one. Without a birth time, Ulune casts the chart at every hour of that day. The cross is marked ~ when its gates or angle could differ.
