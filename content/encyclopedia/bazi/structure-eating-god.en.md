## Meaning

The Eating God structure is the frame of a chart whose month brings the [[ten-god-eating-god|Eating God]], the phase the Day Master produces, of the same polarity. Ulune’s reading puts it in a sentence: it frames a life around expression and enjoyment, talent shared with ease, good taste, and a generous, unhurried way of giving. The Eating God is the god of the good table: what the self makes and offers, and the pleasure of making and offering it.

The tradition reads an easy, open gift: someone whose talents flow without strain, who enjoys what they do and shares it gladly, and around whom others relax. Such a chart often has a feeling for craft, food, music, teaching and every art that gives pleasure, and a way of turning ease into something useful.

Its difficulty is the other side of ease: pleasure can stand in for effort, and comfort can make a gift wait. The classics like the Eating God best with a purpose to serve, and Ulune reads the structure as a way of giving, never as ease promised.

## In practice

- **When Ulune names it**: the month branch holds the Day Master’s Eating God and it comes first, shown among the stems before the month’s other hidden stems, or as the month’s main qi, peers aside, when nothing else shows.
- **Its months, by main qi**: Jia in Si, Yi in Wu, Bing in Chen or Xu, Ding in Chou or Wei, Wu in Shen, Ji in You or Xu, Geng in Hai, Xin in Zi, Ren in Yin, Gui in Mao.
- **What the classics look for**: Wealth, which the Eating God produces, the books’ “Eating God producing wealth”, ease turned into livelihood, with [[ten-god-indirect-wealth|Indirect Wealth]] its natural partner; or Seven Killings, which the Eating God tames, talent mastering pressure. They protect it from the [[ten-god-indirect-resource|Indirect Resource]], which controls it, the image they call “the owl snatching the food”.
- **With strength**: output draws on the Day Master, so the classics like a Day Master strong enough to feed its Eating God; a weak one is read with the Resource and the peers that sustain it. Ulune shows the strength beside the structure.
- **In time**, a decade of the Eating God is one of expression and ease, talents that flow, pleasures, teaching and making, as the luck pillars’ readings say: a time to enjoy what you do.

## Across traditions

- **In Japan and Korea** the structure is the shokujin-kaku of Shichū Suimei and the siksin-gyeok of saju.
- **In Western astrology** the closest theme is the fifth house, of play, pleasure and what one creates, with Venus’s ease: the same joys, read from a house and a planet rather than from the month.

## Origin and history

The Yuanhai Ziping of the Song names the Eating God structure among the standard frames, and the San Ming Tong Hui gathers the sayings that made its name: the Eating God produces wealth, the Eating God controls the Killings, the owl snatches the food. Shen Xiaozhan’s Ziping Zhenquan, in the 18th century, counts it among the four gods to be supported and explains why: though output draws on the self, the Eating God is welcome because it produces Wealth. Xu Lewu’s commentary of 1936 kept the structure among the most admired of the frames.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune names the Eating God structure when, after the Prosperity and Sword Edge tests, the first of the month branch’s non-peer hidden stems to show among the stems of the year, month and hour is the Day Master’s Eating God, or, if none shows, when that stem is the first of them. The Eating God is the stem of the phase the Day Master produces, of the same polarity: Bing for Jia, Ding for Yi, Wu for Bing, Ji for Ding, Geng for Wu, Xin for Ji, Ren for Geng, Gui for Xin, Jia for Ren and Yi for Gui.
