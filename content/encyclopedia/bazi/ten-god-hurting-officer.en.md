## Meaning

The Hurting Officer is a stem of the phase the [[day-master|Day Master]] produces, of the other polarity: Ding for a Jia Day Master, Bing for a Yi, Xin for a Wu. It belongs to output, the family of what the self brings forth, beside the [[ten-god-eating-god|Eating God]]; but where the Eating God expresses with ease, the Hurting Officer expresses with an edge: a quick mind, a gift for words, an eye for what does not work and the wish to say so.

Its name, shāngguān, “hurting the officer”, comes from the cycles: as output of the other polarity, it controls the Day Master’s [[ten-god-direct-officer|Direct Officer]], the god of rules, duty and standing. The old books saw in it the critic of every authority. Ulune reads it as the Zi Ping school’s consensus does: brilliance and a sharp tongue, originality and a readiness to question rules, at its best with tact.

Like all output, it draws on the Day Master: for a strong one it is an outlet, for performance, invention, reform; for a weak one, the classics advise feeding the self before spending it. What it gives, in the tradition’s reading, is the courage to see differently.

## In practice

- **On the board**: for a Jia Day Master, a Ding stem among the pillars, or the Ding hidden in Wu, Wei and Xu.
- **Its reading**: shown, a bright, outspoken mind, originality and an eye for what does not work, brilliance at its best with tact; only in the branches, a critical, creative streak that speaks when it is ready; absent, you may challenge rules less readily, and the tradition suggests trusting your own voice.
- **Strength and balance**: as output, it draws on the Day Master; when the Day Master is strong, output is among the phases that bring balance.
- **The structure**: when the month’s structure comes from its Hurting Officer, revealed among the stems or as the month’s main qi, Ulune names the Hurting Officer structure (傷官格).
- **Luck pillars and years**: a decade of the Hurting Officer is one of originality and change, speaking up, questioning, creating, a time to choose your battles and to channel the spark; a year, one of new ideas and frank words, good for changing what no longer works, with tact.
- **Two people**: when the other’s Day Master is your Hurting Officer, your Day Master produces theirs: you inspire and feed, they take the idea further.

## Across traditions

- **In Japan, Korea and Vietnam** it keeps its name: shōkan in Shichū Suimei, sanggwan in Korean saju, thương quan in Vietnamese.
- **In Western astrology** its themes are shared between a sharp Mercury and Uranus, the planet of the unconventional: the same gift for seeing what others miss, read by planets rather than by a relation of phases.

## Origin and history

The Yuanhai Ziping, under the Song, names the Hurting Officer beside the Eating God, and the Ming books devote whole sections to it, for no god divides the classics more. Its oldest warning is “the Hurting Officer meeting the Officer”: the critic face to face with the rule, read as friction with authority. Its oldest praise is just as famous: “the Hurting Officer produces wealth”, talent turned into livelihood, and “the Hurting Officer wearing the seal”, its brilliance given a discipline by the Direct Resource, which controls it.

The Ziping Zhenquan, under the Qing, counts it among the four forceful gods, to be tempered rather than suppressed, and builds its structures on those two remedies: the resource that schools it, the wealth that puts it to work. The Di Tian Sui, with Ren Tieqiao’s commentary, judges it as it judges every god, by the balance of the whole: brilliant where the chart can carry it.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune names a stem the Hurting Officer when its phase is one step from the Day Master’s along the generating cycle, the phase the Day Master produces, and their polarities differ. It looks among the stems of the year, the month and the hour and among the hidden stems of the branches with their weights, counts the Hurting Officer shown and hidden, and reads it as shown, hidden or absent. Output draws on the Day Master in the strength tests, and the month gives the Hurting Officer structure when the Hurting Officer is the first of the month branch’s hidden stems, peers aside, to show among the stems, or, if none shows, the first of them.
