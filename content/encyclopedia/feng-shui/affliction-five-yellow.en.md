## Meaning

The 5 Yellow, 五黃 wǔ huáng, is the [[flying-star-5|fifth of the nine stars]] as it flies with the year: the Lo Shu’s own centre, Lian Zhen, the strongest star, which has no direction of its own and so lands somewhere new each year. Where it lands, the classics counsel quiet for the year in that part of the home: heavy works, digging or renovation are best left until it moves on at Lichun.

It is a matter of timing, not of the place. The same palace that rests this year is free the next, and a room there is used as usual: the counsel concerns works, the breaking of ground and walls, not living, sleeping or working. Practitioners add only that a year’s 5 meeting a home’s own 5 or 2 asks for a little more quiet still.

In 2026 the 5 Yellow is in the south, in 2027 in the north, in 2028 in the south-west; in a year whose own star is the 5, as in 2022 and 2031, it stays in the centre. The month has its 5 Yellow too, which visits another palace with each solar month.

## In practice

- **In the Home lens** the year’s 5 Yellow is named in the list of what to leave quiet this year, and its palace carries a dashed frame inside the chart; the palace’s reading says so beside its stars.
- **In the Cycles lens**, when the year’s 5 Yellow falls in one of your directions, the counsel is a year to rest that side rather than start work there, and for new starts and long tasks to face another of your directions.
- **The month’s 5 Yellow**: in the month it visits a direction, a month to keep that side quiet; in October 2026 it is in the north.
- **Planning works**: practitioners suggest scheduling heavy works in a part of the home the 5 Yellow is not visiting, or waiting for Lichun, when it moves on.

## Across traditions

- **Nine Star Ki** watches the same star each year and calls the direction where it stands the gosatsu, 五黄殺, advising against moving house towards it that year.
- **The centre of the square**: Yellow is the colour of the centre and of Earth in the five-phase correspondences, and of the emperor who stood at the centre of the world; the 5 Yellow carries that weight wherever it flies.
- **Elsewhere**, many building traditions keep works for the right season, as Vastu’s choice of a muhūrta for breaking ground; the 5 Yellow is Feng Shui’s yearly calendar of when to leave a side alone.

## Origin and history

The 5 Yellow is the star the Xuan Kong verses treat with the most care, and the almanacs carry its yearly place among the spirits of the year. Its counsel, quiet and no works, is among the most widely known of all Feng Shui’s, and Ulune keeps it in the classics’ own measure: a year’s note about timing, no more.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune sets the year’s star in the centre, the 1 White in 2026 and one less each year after, and flies it forward through the nine palaces in the Lo Shu’s order; the 5 Yellow is where the 5 lands. As the year’s star goes back one each year, the 5 Yellow walks forward one palace along the same path, centre, north-west, west, north-east, south, north, south-west, east, south-east, and comes back to a palace every nine years. The month’s 5 Yellow is found in the same way from the month’s star, which goes back one each solar month.
