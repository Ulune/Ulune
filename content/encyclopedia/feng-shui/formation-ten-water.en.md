## Meaning

The Combination of Ten for the water stars is one of the [[flying-star|Flying Stars]]’ formations that Ulune names, a pattern that runs through the whole of a home’s [[flying-star-chart|chart]]: in all nine palaces the water star and the period star add up to ten, a 1 with a 9, a 2 with an 8, a 5 with a 5.

向星合十, xiàng xīng hé shí, the water stars, the stars of the facing, combine to ten. The classics read it as a chart whose stars work together, for activity and resources, a current that runs through the whole home, and they let it soften what else the chart carries.

Of the 216 main charts, the twenty-four facings of each of the nine [[period|periods]], 12 carry this formation, in Periods 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9.

## In practice

- **What practitioners suggest**: nothing to change; they read the home’s activity as carried by the pattern, and give its palaces’ own pairs their usual care.
- **In the Home lens**: the chart’s reading names its formations. Built in Period 9, a home facing [[mountain-qian|Qian]] or [[mountain-hai|Hai]] carries this one; built in Period 8, a home facing [[mountain-chou|Chou]].
- **Near an edge**: the replacement chart, which Ulune shows beside the main one for a facing close to the edge of its mountain, has formations of its own, named the same way; it may keep this one or not.

## Across traditions

- **The Lo Shu’s own pairs**: every two numbers facing each other across the Lo Shu’s centre make ten; the water stars of this chart give each palace that balance with the period.
- **The facing’s stars**: the water stars are cast from the facing palace, so their Combination of Ten ties the whole home to its front, as the mountain stars’ ties it to its back.

## Origin and history

The Combination of Ten of the water stars mirrors that of the mountain stars across the compass: twelve of the 216 main charts, in Period 9 for a home facing Qian or Hai. The classical phrase 向星合十 names the water stars by the facing, 向, since the water star is cast from the facing palace.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune casts each home’s chart, then checks it against every formation it knows. For this one the rule is: in all nine palaces the water star and the period star add up to ten, a 1 with a 9, a 2 with an 8, a 5 with a 5. It is read on the main chart and on the replacement chart alike, each on its own. Ulune’s tests check its formations against charts worked by hand.

An example: a home facing [[mountain-qian|Qian]], built in Period 9. The period chart sets 9 in the centre; the mountain star goes to the centre as a 8 and flies forward, the water star as a 1 and flies backward. At the back, in the palace of the south-east, stand mountain star 7 and water star 2; at the front, in the palace of the north-west, mountain star 9 and water star 9. Palace by palace, water star and period star: centre 1 + 9, north-west 9 + 1, west 8 + 2, north-east 7 + 3, south 6 + 4, north 5 + 5, south-west 4 + 6, east 3 + 7, south-east 2 + 8.
