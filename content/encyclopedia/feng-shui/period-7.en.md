## Meaning

Period 7, 七運 qī yùn, is the seventh of the nine [[period|periods]] of twenty years that the Flying Stars count in a cycle of 180: from 1984 to 2003, then from 2164 to 2183, ruled by the [[flying-star-7|7 Red]]. It is the first period of the lower age, one of the cycle’s three ages of sixty years.

Its star is the 7 Red, Metal and the trigram Dui, the lake, the youngest daughter, which the classics give to speech, charm and pleasure. It opened the lower age of the cycle in 1984, the third Jia-Zi year since 1864.

Its period chart sets the 7 in the centre and flies it forward along the Lo Shu’s path: north-west 8, west 9, north-east 1, south 2, north 3, south-west 4, east 5, south-east 6. It is the base of the [[flying-star-chart|chart]] of every home built in it, over which the mountain and water stars then fly.

## In practice

- **Period 7 homes**: a home built or last fully renovated between 1984 and 2003 is a Period 7 home. For its main chart, a front facing [[mountain-mao|Mao]], [[mountain-yi|Yi]], [[mountain-chen|Chen]], [[mountain-you|You]], [[mountain-xin|Xin]] or [[mountain-xu|Xu]] gives the [[formation-prosperous|prosperous chart]]; facing [[mountain-jia|Jia]], [[mountain-xun|Xun]], [[mountain-si|Si]], [[mountain-geng|Geng]], [[mountain-qian|Qian]] or [[mountain-hai|Hai]], the [[formation-reversed|reversed chart]]; the other facings bring the period’s two stars together, at the front or at the back.
- **The period’s stars**: through Period 7 the 7, the 8 and the 9 are timely, the 7 being the period’s own star; the 6 is waning, and the other four are dormant.
- **Read today**: a Period 7 home keeps its Period 7 chart; the Home lens reads its stars by their state in Period 9, the present period, and says so under each palace.
- **A full renovation**: if the home has been fully renovated since, the Home lens asks for that year, which gives another period and another chart; some schools take instead the year one moved in, and Ulune follows the more common rule.

## Across traditions

- **The sixty-year cycle**: Period 7 began in 1984, a Jia-Zi year, 甲子, of the Rat; every period begins in a Jia year, since twenty years make exactly two rounds of the ten stems.
- **Jupiter and Saturn**: the great conjunction of Jupiter and Saturn of 2000 falls in Period 7, as one falls in each of the nine; some modern writers have linked the two twenty-year rhythms, which the classics count by the calendar, not by the planets.
- **The lake**: the 7’s trigram, Dui, is the Shuo Gua’s lake at the west, the middle of autumn where all things rejoice.

## Origin and history

The count of three ages of sixty years, the Three Yuan, 三元, belongs to the Chinese calendar, and the Xuan Kong school divided each into three periods of twenty years, each ruled by a star of the Lo Shu. This cycle’s Period 7 ran from 1984 to 2003; it comes back from 2164 to 2183. The school’s manuals, Shen Zhureng’s first among them, give for each period the charts of its twenty-four facings.

## How Ulune calculates it

Ulune finds a home’s period from the year it was built or last fully renovated: twenty years a period from 1864, so that any year from 1984 to 2003 gives Period 7. It counts by the calendar year alone: a home finished in January 1984, before Lichun, would strictly belong to the period before, and the Home lens shows the year it used. The period chart sets the 7 in the centre and flies forward; the stars’ states follow the period’s rule, timely for its number and the two after it, waning for the one before.
