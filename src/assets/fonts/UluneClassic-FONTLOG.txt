Ulune Classic
=============

Ulune Classic is a modified version of Astronomicon, Copyright (c) 2021,
Roberto Corona (http://astronomicon.co), licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 (UluneClassic-OFL.txt), which reserves the name "Astronomicon".

Changes, 27 September 2026, for Ulune (https://ulune.app):
- kept the 50 glyphs Ulune draws (planets, signs, points, aspects);
- renamed "Ulune Classic" (name table: family, full name, PostScript name,
  unique ID), as the licence asks of a modified version;
- stored as WOFF2.

The glyph outlines, metrics and character map are unchanged.
