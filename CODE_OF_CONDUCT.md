# Code of conduct

In issues, and anywhere else Ulune is discussed here:

- Be kind and assume good faith. People come to astrology, Human Design and
  numerology with every kind of belief, including none: argue about ideas,
  never about people.
- Keep other people's personal data out, starting with birth details.
- No harassment, slurs, sexual content or spam.

Comments that break these rules are removed; repeated or serious cases are
blocked. To report something, write to limiel.ulune@protonmail.com.
